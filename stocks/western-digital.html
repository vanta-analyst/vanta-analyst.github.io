    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Pure HDD business after the Sandisk spin-off (Feb. 2025); multiples before FY2025 include the flash business and are only partly comparable. TTM net income of USD 9.3bn (EPS $24.28) includes a gain of about $6.5bn on Sandisk shares and is not repeatable - trailing P/E 16 is therefore misleading; forward P/E (19.6) is used. The share count rose 6.7% due to conversion of convertible bonds (about 21m new shares), not share-based compensation. Seagate's HAMR lead has been examined only superficially, and the long-term customer agreements (2029-2031) have not yet been signed. The fiscal year ends in early July.
