      Stock-based compensation was 1,642m $ TTM, more than the entire FCF of 1,195m $, so FCF after stock compensation is approx. -450m $. Stock compensation is 29% of revenue in Q2 (38% a year earlier). Share count rose 3.4% despite buybacks of 1.4bn $.

      Non-GAAP product gross margin fell to 74.7% from 76.4%, and full-year guidance is 74.0%. Snowflake buys the inference it resells, so AI growth has a lower contribution margin.
