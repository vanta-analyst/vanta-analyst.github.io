    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B, P/TBV and P/E, and the payout is held against net profit. EPS and P/E for 2021 are distorted by the spin-off of Línea Directa, so history is measured mainly from 2022. Stockanalysis shows +2.3% in share count over a year, but the cash flow shows only small offsetting purchases and sales of own shares (about EUR 81m each way); the figure is not reconciled, and no deduction has been made for dilution. Bankinter pays no Spanish bank tax (IMIC) due to the deductions in the law, so there is no bank tax deduction. Forward P/E and EPS forecasts are adjusted figures. Insider ownership 0.67% according to stockanalysis; a large family shareholder (Cartival) has not been investigated and not counted. Q3 figures 22 Oct 2026.
