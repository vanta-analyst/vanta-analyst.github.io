    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Penny deep dive. US graphite company (Alabama) building the Kellyton plant for battery graphite (anode material) and owning the Coosa graphite deposit; former uranium producer (no production since 2009). Listed on NYSE American (not Nasdaq). Price USD 0.5089 and market cap USD 65.4m from stockanalysis 2/10-2026 (Nordnet showed DKK 436m). The latest 10-Q is as of 30/6-2026; USD 2.4m was used to redeem the convertible notes 16/7-2026, so cash is lower today. The 10-Q contains no formal going concern warning (management believes cash lasts more than a year for non-discretionary expenses), but earlier years have had one. The USD 25m EXIM loan is approved but awaits final documentation. Management, insider trades and the largest shareholders have not been investigated in depth. The graphite price and policy (tariffs/anti-dumping against China) drive the economics, so cyclical = yes.
