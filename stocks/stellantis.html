    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Milan listing (STLAM). Cyclical = yes (cars, consistent with BMW, Mercedes, VW and Renault). Stockanalysis' net debt of €20.1bn and gross debt of €52.1bn include the financing companies; the industrial net financial position could not be determined unambiguously in the half-year statement and is therefore given neither a plus nor an extra deduction. The TTM net loss of €19.5bn stems mainly from the H2 2025 reset (about €22.2bn in costs); H1 2026 delivered net income of €670m. Forward P/E of 5.0 is based on adjusted 2026E EPS of €0.58 and an expected rise to €1.05 in 2027, which requires a marked margin improvement. No dividend in 2026. Exor (about 15%), the Peugeot family and the French state (Bpifrance) are major shareholders – mentioned, not scored. Analyst price targets continue to fall (Berenberg and Morgan Stanley in September). Q3 is due 28/10.
