    Bull case

    Updated resource and PFS confirm a large, low-grade copper project with approx. US$1.5bn NPV at higher copper prices, and a larger producer (Hudbay, Teck or a Japanese offtaker) comes in. At 0.2–0.3x of the attributable NPV (approx. US$1.3bn) the project is worth C$350–550m – approx. 2.5–3.5x today's market value.

    Bear case

    The copper price falls, security in Sinaloa worsens, or Mexico halts new open-pit mines; cash runs out around the turn of the year, and an issue at C$0.25-0.30 dilutes a further 25-40%. The stock could fall back toward P/B 0.9-1.0 (C$0.30), about -35%.
