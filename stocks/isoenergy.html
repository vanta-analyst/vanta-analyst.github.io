    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Uranium developer without revenue and without proven reserves; listed on the TSX (ISO) and NYSE American (ISOU) – the analysis follows the TSX listing in CAD. The shares were consolidated 1:4 in March 2025, so the history before that is adjusted. The acquisition of Anfield (Shootaring mill, Utah) was terminated by Anfield in January 2025 and is not included. Consolidated Uranium (American mines in Utah, Coles Hill in Virginia, Australian projects) was bought in December 2023, and Toro Energy (Wiluna, Western Australia, approx. 75m lb) in June 2026. Hurricane has 48.6m lb indicated at 34.5% U3O8 plus 2.7m lb inferred, but there is no PEA yet, so P/NAV cannot be calculated; T2 is based on P/B history and EV per lb. The Coles Hill figures (132.9m lb indicated) are historical and not a current resource. The Utah mines (Tony M etc.) are on standby with a tollmilling agreement with Energy Fuels; no restart decision before an economic study at the end of 2026. NexGen owns 27.8% and has influence. Quarterly spending (operations + exploration) was approx. CAD 7.4m in Q2 2026. Insider trades and short interest have not been examined (Stockanalysis shows no short data for the TSX). Q3 report expected 4/11-2026.
