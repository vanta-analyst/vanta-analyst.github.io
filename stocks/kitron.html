Business−7 of −30

Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger?

  • Moat
    Kitron is a contract manufacturer (EMS): customers own the design and can move production. The gross margin is 33% and the net margin 7.0% (TTM), and it is volume and delivery reliability – not its own technology – that carry the business.
    stockanalysis.com
    −3
  • Margin
    An EBIT margin of 9.6% in Q2 and 9.3% TTM is top of the EMS industry, but the target is only 9%, and the margin is lifted by the defence mix and high capacity utilisation that is unlikely to last in a normal cycle.
    globenewswire.com
    −2
  • Capital allocation and dilution
    A directed issue of 17m new shares at NOK 57.25 (NOK 973m) in November 2025 to buy DeltaNordic; the share count has risen 5.0% in a year.

Cracks−7 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • Supply chain
    Shortages of memory chips, processors and circuit boards; the allocation is expected to continue in H2. Management therefore did not raise guidance despite strong demand, and the stock fell 7.8% on the Q2 report.

    −3

    Defence & Aerospace has tripled to EUR 154m and is now about 50% of revenue and EUR 473m of the 794m order book. A ceasefire or budget pressure in Europe would hit both growth and the multiple.

    The order book fell 1.5% vs Q1 because large programmes were delivered, and book-to-bill year to date is only 1.15 – the 72% growth pace cannot be sustained without new large orders.
