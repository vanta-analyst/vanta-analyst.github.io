    Bull case

    Revenue is heading back towards EUR 780–800m, and the margin holds at 6.5% (EBIT approx. EUR 51m, net result approx. EUR 35m ≈ NOK 400m). At a P/E of 10 that is approx. NOK 4bn = approx. 2x. A 3x requires revenue back at EUR 900m (2022 level) and a 7% margin.

    Bear case

    A truck downturn, tariffs or new warranty cases send the margin back to 1–2% as in 2022–2025; then the stock is not cheap, and the price could fall 40–50% toward NOK 1.0–1.2. Zero is unlikely with gearing of 1.6x.
