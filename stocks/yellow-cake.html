    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Yellow Cake is a holding company (Jersey, AIM-listed) with two employees that owns physical uranium (24.37m lb after the Kazatomprom delivery in H2 2026) stored with Cameco (Canada) and Orano (France). The Vanta model's T1 criteria (margin, ROIC, moat, quality of growth) fit poorly: 'earnings' is revaluation of the uranium stock, so P/E 3.9 and ROE 24% are meaningless, and T2 is assessed on discount to NAV instead of earnings multiples. The NAV discount is calculated by me: 24.37m lb x spot USD 89.80 (9/10-2026) + USD 46.9m in net working assets and USD/GBP approx. 1.33 gives approx. GBP 6.69 per share vs a price of GBP 5.425, i.e. approx. 19% discount (approx. 15% vs the official pro forma NAV of GBP 6.42 from 24/7). The price equals approx. USD 72/lb. For comparison, Sprott Physical Uranium Trust (SPUT) traded at approx. 13.3% discount (9/10). The P/B history (0.83–0.94 in 2022–26) is used as discount history. The USD 10m buyback gives no plus, because operating cash flow is negative. The price formula of the Kazatomprom agreement is not public, and the 2026 purchase was at USD 86.15/lb, close to spot. Currency exposure: NAV in USD, the share in GBP.
