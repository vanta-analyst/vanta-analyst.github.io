    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Q3 2026 report is only due on 27 Oct; only Q3 deliveries (161 aircraft) are known. TTM figures run to 30 Jun 2026. The positive net income for 2025 and TTM is due to the sale of Digital Aviation Solutions (Jeppesen and others) in Q4 2025; operations are loss-making. Trailing and forward P/E are therefore meaningless and have not been used as history. Credit rating, KC-46/T-7 contracts in detail, union agreements and insider trading have not been investigated. 13F purchases give no points.
