Business−13 of −30

Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger?

  • Quality of growth
    Revenue has fallen four years in a row: from SEK 271.5bn in 2022 to 236.7bn in 2025 and 227.5bn TTM (−16%), and analysts expect −4.5% in 2026. Organic −1% in Q2 2026, and the Americas fell again.
    stockanalysis.com
    −5
  • Capital allocation
    Vonage was bought for about USD 6.2bn in 2022, and half of the goodwill and intangibles (SEK 32bn) was written down as early as 2023. The Enterprise segment has shrunk from SEK 25.7bn to 18.3bn (TTM), partly after the sale of iconectiv.
    ericsson.com
    −4
  • Margin
    Adjusted EBITA margin 13.1% in Q2 (13.2% a year earlier) and operating margin 13.6% TTM. The gross margin has been lifted to 48%, but most of the improvement is cost cutting: headcount has fallen from 91,937 to 86,536 in a year.
    ericsson.com
    −2
  • Moat
    Effectively a duopoly with Nokia outside China, but Huawei is bigger globally, and operators are holding mobile investment down; Ericsson has not been able to grow despite market share gains such as the AT&T contract.
    investing.com
    −2

Cracks−13 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • The Iraq case, DOJ and FCPA
    Ericsson pleaded guilty in 2023 and paid USD 206m for breaching the 2019 FCPA settlement (on top of USD 520m). The DOJ is still investigating the Iraq matters with unknown scope and duration, and four civil suits under the Anti-Terrorism Act (2022, 2024, 2025, 2026) naming Börje Ekholm as co-defendant await rulings on dismissal.
    sec.gov
    −6
  • 5G cycle
    Morgan Stanley downgraded to Underweight on 22 Sept 2026: the RAN market is expected flat into 2027, Networks revenue in North America fell about 5% in Q2 after the AT&T win, and the margin is expected to turn down. 8 of 20 analysts rate it sell.
    investing.com
    −4

    Management expects pressure on the Networks gross margin in Q3 because of more roll-out projects and rising component prices, to be offset with price increases and internal measures.
