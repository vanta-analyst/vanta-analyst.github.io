    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Penny deep dive (the price is above DKK 3). Taiwan/UK-based provider of AI infrastructure, GPU-as-a-Service, data centers and security solutions to governments (Egypt, Taiwan, Thailand) and to Yotta (India) and NeutraDC Batam (Indonesia); reports under IFRS as a foreign issuer (6-K). A price of USD 11.13 (9/10-2026) and 27.51m shares give a market cap of USD 306m (DKK 2,016m; Nordnet showed 2,162). The 2026 half-year figures are unaudited and unreviewed. The USD 125m convertible Series B (July 2026) is not included in stockanalysis' balance sheet at 30/6, so cash and debt are stated here pro forma (about 300m cash and about 200m debt, estimate before costs). The number of warrants and their prices have not been found. The status of the first Batam delivery (planned September 2026) is not confirmed. Analyst price targets of USD 24-44 (+115-295%) are probably optimistic given such high dilution.
