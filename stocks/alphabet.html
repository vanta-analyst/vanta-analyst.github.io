    • Cash flow and capex
      Free cash flow was negative in Q2 (−$5.9bn), the first time in about 20 years. 2026 capex guidance has been raised to $195–205bn (from 180–190bn), and analysts expect only $7.8bn of FCF for 2026 vs $73bn in 2025. The build-out is partly debt-funded: debt has risen from $30bn to 121bn since 2024, and buybacks have been cut from $62bn to 17bn a year.
