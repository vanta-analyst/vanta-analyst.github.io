The TTM operating margin is 22.8% vs 23.3% in 2025, still well up from 15–18% in 2022–23, but the most profitable segment is under pressure.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has stood still for four years ($4.12bn in 2022, 4.18bn in 2025), and 2026 is guided at −4 to −7%. Q2 sales fell 17% to $933m (Pool −42%, Water Solutions −5%, Flow +5%), and adjusted EPS fell 18% to $1.14. The expected growth in 2027 (+9%) is mainly bought through Taco.
      sec.gov
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      The purchase of Taco Group for $1.425bn (cash and a $1.4bn bridge facility) was announced two weeks after the guidance cut. It lifts net leverage to about 2.4x and adds only $0.10–0.15 of EPS in 2027 (about 2–3%). At the same time $150m of shares were bought back in Q2.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −24 of −35
    • Customers are shifting
      Destocking at the big pool distributors removed about $170m of Q2 sales and forced guidance down from sales +2–4% and EPS $5.30–5.40 to sales −4 to −7% and EPS $4.60–4.80. The stock fell 15% to $64.33 on 15/7 and has since continued down to about $53.
      newsfilecorp.com
      −8
    • Customer concentration
      Pool sales go through a few large distributors (especially Pool Corp), and their inventory decisions feed straight through; Pool fell from about 37% of group sales to 26% in Q2.
      sec.gov
      −3
    • Governance (CFO change)
      CFO Nicholas Brazis left with immediate effect on 14/7 after only four months, the same day as the guidance cut. Former CFO Robert Fishman is back as interim CFO, so the post has changed three times in about a year.
      sec.gov
      −4
    • Lawsuits
      Several class actions (including Robbins Geller, Hagens Berman and Kaplan Fox) over misleading statements about channel inventory and internal controls for the period 11/3/2025 to 14/7/2026; the lead-plaintiff deadline is 2/10/2026.
      newsfilecorp.com
      −4
    • Macro
      The company itself cites higher interest rates and inflation as the cause of the worsened conditions; new pools and renovations follow the housing and interest-rate market.
      sec.gov
      −3
    • Debt and rating
      Net debt of $1.67bn (1.6x EBITDA) rises to about 2.4x when Taco closes, funded with a $1.4bn bridge facility. S&P has affirmed BBB-, the lowest investment-grade notch; the target is below 1.5x within two years.

      Only small sales in February (the CTO for $0.67m and a board member for $0.70m, both at about $100). No insider buying despite a price fall of over 50%, but no sales after the guidance cut either.
