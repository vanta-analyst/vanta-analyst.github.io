    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −6 of −30
    • Moat
      Capital IQ is essentially an interface, and its value falls when AI agents fetch data directly. Market Intelligence is about 30% of revenue ($5.1bn TTM), but the smallest part of profit; Ratings, Indices and Energy have the regulatory and benchmark-based moats.

      −3
    • Quality of growth
      In Q2 Ratings grew 17% and Indices 20%, while Energy grew only 2% and Market Intelligence 6%. Growth is carried by the two most market-sensitive businesses (issuance and stock-market levels), not the subscription part.
      sec.gov

      ROIC is 11.1% vs a WACC of 9.15% (7.3% in 2023, 10.4% in 2025), held down by goodwill from the IHS Markit merger. Now parts of the merger are being unwound: Mobility spun off, upstream software sold to SLB, a Capital IQ spin-off under consideration.
