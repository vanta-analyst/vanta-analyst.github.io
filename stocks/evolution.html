    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −13 of −35
    • Unlicensed markets and regulation
      Asia accounts for 36.8% of revenue (EUR 190.5m in Q2), mostly unregulated or grey markets. In 2026 Evolution settled with the UK Gambling Commission for GBP 4.75m because its games were available on unlicensed sites – a direct threat to licences in regulated markets. At the same time European operators are losing customers to unlicensed sites (channelisation of about 50% in the UK and the Netherlands).
      evolution.com
      −8
    • Cybercrime in Asia
      Asia fell 8.9% y/y in Q2 because of syndicates copying Evolution's live streams; management describes an endless fight in which security measures also hit legitimate players.
      stockopine.com
      −4
    • Currency
      Reported revenue −1.2% vs +2.4% at constant currency in Q2; earnings are in EUR (and partly USD/Asian currencies), while the stock trades in SEK.
      inderes.fi
      −1
