    Bull case

    Airbus and Boeing raise rates, aerostructures go from −10.8% to +5% EBITDA margin, and the group reaches an 8% EBITDA margin on EUR 900m (about 70m). With an EV/EBITDA of 6, EV becomes 420m and equity about 260m, i.e. just under a doubling. A 3–10x requires debt to be paid down significantly – unlikely within 2-3 years.

    Bear case

    Margins stay near zero, the PGE agreement falls through or requires new equity, and Searchlight carries out another capital increase or buyout at a price far below now. Equity is only 51.5m vs 162.6m in net debt, so a new loss of 30-50m could halve the stock or more.
