    Bull case

    TotalEnergies' appraisal confirms Mopane (1.4bn boe+), PEL 90 or PEL 82 gives a new discovery, and the market starts to price Sintana's approx. 67m net barrels at USD 4–6/boe as before an FID. That gives an EV of USD 270–400m (approx. CAD 380–570m) – 2–3x – and a discovery on PEL 90/82 could give more; analysts' targets are CAD 0.55–0.87.

    Bear case

    Dry wells on PEL 90 and a disappointing appraisal on Mopane, TotalEnergies postpones FID, and the share is priced only on cash and option value (approx. CAD 0.05–0.15, −60–85%). Even with success, the development share may force a large issue or a cheap divestment.
