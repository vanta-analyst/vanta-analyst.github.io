    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −7 of −30
    • Weak segment (Industrial Automation)
      Industrial Automation (€7.2bn, 17% of revenue) has not grown since 2022 (€7.7bn); the margin rose only 50 bp in H1 despite +7.7% organic, and management says the turnaround to desired profitability may take up to two years.
      gurufocus.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      A string of expensive acquisitions: Cognite for USD 3.1bn (about 18 times its revenue of USD 170m; Morningstar: 'the price looks high') and the India minority for €5.5bn (RBC: 'not a cheap transaction'), following the AVEVA buyout in 2023. Net debt has risen from €9.4bn (2024) to €15.4bn, and the share count has risen 0.6% despite buybacks.
      morningstar.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Growth is driven by lower-margin system sales (+28% in Q2) and price increases (about 4 percentage points of product growth); gross margin improved only 10 bp organically, and software/services grew just 6%.
      gurufocus.com

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Schneider steers by adjusted EBITA and adjusted EPS (2025: 8.59 € vs reported 7.33 €); adjusted and reported figures are not the same. The company's net debt was 15.4 bn € at 30/6-2026 (stockanalysis: 16.3 bn € incl. leases). The purchase of Temasek's 35% of Schneider Electric India (5.5 bn €) and Cognite (3.1 bn USD) was not completed at the latest reporting and will raise debt further. Q3 revenue comes on 28/10. Insider trading and customer concentration among hyperscalers have not been investigated.
