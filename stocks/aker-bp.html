    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Accounts in USD, price in NOK. stockanalysis' forward P/E history (0.7–1.5 in 2023–2025) and the current forward P/E of 6.1 are distorted by a currency error (USD EPS vs NOK price). With a price of NOK 350 and an EPS estimate of USD 3.50, the P/E is about 10 on 2026 and about 12 on 2027 – T2 therefore rests on P/B, EV/EBITDA and trailing P/E. The net margin is low due to Norwegian petroleum tax (78.8%). According to the company, 2026 is the peak for investment; production is to rise to about 525,000 boe/day in 2028 if the projects deliver.
