Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Stockanalysis shows gross revenue (€6.3bn TTM) and a reported EBITDA of €1.2bn, while Nexi itself reports net revenue (about €3.6bn in 2026E) and EBITDA (€870m in H1, margin 50%); analysts' EPS (€0.61 in 2025) is adjusted, while the reported result was −€3.4bn due to a goodwill impairment of about €3.7bn. Forward P/E 6.4 is therefore on adjusted figures. Stockanalysis' 11% fall in share count does not match the buybacks in the cash flow (€300m in 2025) and is not given points. CVC considered a bid of about €9bn (EV) in the spring, but CDP has raised its stake towards 29.9% and does not want to sell – a bid is unlikely without government support. Some price targets were raised in August/September; the average is below the price. Insider trading not investigated.
