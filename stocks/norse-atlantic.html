    Bull case

    The strategic review ends with a bid from a larger player (e.g. an airline or lessor that wants the 787 fleet and Gatwick slots), the fuel price falls back, and the ACMI/Thailand routes fill the planes this winter. A bid of NOK 0.90–1.00 per share (NOK 2.7–3.0bn for the equity, EV about NOK 10bn) would give 3x; that requires a buyer to value the fleet, slots and the ACMI business far above the current price, which is below the issue price of NOK 0.50.

    Bear case

    Negative equity, NOK 7.9bn in debt (primarily leasing), only approx. USD 25m in free cash and an expensive loan maturing in May 2027. If the review does not produce a bid, and the winter is weak, Norse must raise money again far below NOK 0.30 or be restructured – the share could fall 80–100%.
