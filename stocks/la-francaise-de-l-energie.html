    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has stalled: EUR 39.2m (2022/23), 31.5, 30.4 and 29.1m (2025/26, -4%). Gas sales rose 14%, but power income fell with market prices. The target of over EUR 175m in revenue in 2030 requires a sixfold increase.

    • Capital allocation / ROIC
      ROIC is 3.6% and ROE 2.5%. Investments (EUR 38m TTM) are far above operating cash flow (16m), so FCF is −23m, and debt has doubled from EUR 68m to 131m in two years.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    FDE is NOT in restructuring (no sauvegarde or redressement), so the ordinary penny calibration is used. The share price is 18.94 EUR (Nordnet 1/10) and 17.76 EUR according to stockanalysis on 2/10. The market cap is calculated at 100.2 m EUR (5.29 m shares × 18.94 EUR, about 748 m DKK) vs Nordnet's 782 m DKK. The fiscal year ends 30/6; the balance sheet and cash flow are from the half-year at 31/12-2025, and the audited annual report for 2025/26 comes on 20/10-2026. The share price drop from 41.30 EUR (24/6) is partly due to hedging losses in July, but the drop from about 26 EUR to under 19 EUR in August and September has no company news that I have been able to find. The debt maturity profile is only partly known (green bond of 40 m EUR from 2021 with a 7-year term and 60 m EUR from 2024 with Edmond de Rothschild AM). Industry: gas from coal mines (CMM), power and biomethane, set to Energy: oil & gas; cyclical, because about half of power sales and gas sales follow market prices. Insider trades and short interest have not been examined. Peer figures are approximate.
