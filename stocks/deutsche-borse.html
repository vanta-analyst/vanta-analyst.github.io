    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The revenue forecast (−11.5% in 2026) is therefore a definitional artefact. The Allfunds acquisition (EUR 5.3bn, closing H1 2027) is not yet in the figures. Insider ownership is not disclosed.
