    Bull case

    Neuro.io pays the rest and comes in as a strategic owner, the US hospital channels lift revenue to CHF 5–10m in 2027–28, and reimbursement falls into place in Switzerland and France. With lower costs after the divestment the market could re-rate the share to EV/Sales 5–8, i.e. 3–5x. A merger at a higher value could give more.

    Bear case

    The Neuro.io money fails to arrive or comes too late, and cash is empty around the turn of the year. Emergency financing at CHF 0.05–0.10 with 50–100%+ dilution, or wind-down or sale of the assets at a fraction of the value.
