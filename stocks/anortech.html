    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Hudson Resources (TSX-V: HUD) changed its name to AnorTech Inc. in March 2024 and now trades as TSX-V: ANOR (OTCQB: ANORF), price CAD 0.125. White Mountain no longer belongs to the company: the mine sits in Lumina Sustainable Materials, where AnorTech only had a 31% minority stake. The main asset is now the Grønne Bjerg anorthosite 80 km north-east of Nuuk. The plan is to make alumina (SGA/HPA) from anorthosite, which is not commercially proven. Neither an NI 43-101 resource nor an economic study for Grønne Bjerg has been found. Cash was CAD 1.39m (June 2026) before CAD 1.38m in September from Sarfartoq. No analysts; insider trading has not been examined.
