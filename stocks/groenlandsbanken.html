  • Concentration in one small economy
    The bank has about 80% market share in Greenland, an economy of about 56,000 people that depends on fishing and the block grant. There is no geographical diversification, and net interest income fell 14% y/y in Q3 2025.
    ugebrev.dk
    −3
  • Speculative share price and liquidity
    DR calls the rise 'striking': the share price is driven by speculation about US investment after Trump's statements, not by fundamentals. On average only about 2,400 shares are traded a day (about DKK 2.9m).
    dr.dk

  • Dividend tax for Danish investors
    The bank withholds dividend tax at Sermersooq's total tax rate (about 42%), and Greenland has no general double-taxation agreement. That makes the high dividend yield worth less to a Danish investor.
