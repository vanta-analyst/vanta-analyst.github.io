    Bull case

    GenSight lands a financing of 15–20 m EUR or a partner agreement. AAC revenue grows to over 15 m EUR a year, FDA approves the RECOVER protocol in Q4 2026, and the study starts in 2027. The market then prices a probable European and US comeback for LUMEVOQ, and an EV of 80–120 m EUR (even after 50% dilution) could give 2–4x. 10x requires an acquisition.

    Bear case

    The financing fails to materialize before March 2027. Heights' repayments and the French repayments of 13.2 m EUR in 2027 strangle the company, and money is raised at 0.03–0.05 EUR with 100%+ dilution, or the company goes into sauvegarde/redressement, where the old shareholders are left with almost nothing.
