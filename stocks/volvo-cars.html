    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −12 of −35
    • Withdrawn guidance
      On 2 Oct. 2026 – 15 days after the capital markets day with an 8% margin target and 13 new models – Volvo Cars announced that the 2026 targets for volume and cash flow will not be met; Q3 deliveries fell 10.7% due to the China crisis and a slow recovery in the US, with a significant negative effect on Q3 earnings and cash flow.
      eletric-vehicles.com
      −7

      Geely is clearly the largest shareholder, and free float is only about 597 m of 2.96 bn shares (about 20%); minority shareholders have little influence, and there are close transactions with the Geely group (e.g. exclusive distribution of Lynk & Co in Europe).
