    Marked cyclical: ferry and ro-ro freight follows European industrial volumes and Turkish exports, and fixed vessel costs create high operating leverage (EBIT margin 9.2% in the 2022 freight boom vs 0.9% in 2025). A borderline case, because part of the decline is also company-specific (Turkey logistics, the Channel). stockanalysis' FCF (2.13bn) is before leases (1.76bn) and interest, so the 24% FCF yield is misleading; the company's own adjusted FCF guidance (about DKK 500m) is used. There is no trailing P/E (loss). The strategy review under the new CEO concludes around February 2027; the Naviera Armas acquisition in the Strait of Gibraltar is not in guidance.
