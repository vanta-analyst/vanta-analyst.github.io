    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company: valued on the discount to net asset value (NAV SEK 106.96 per share at 30/6 2026, next update in the Q3 report on 15/10). Stockanalysis shows negative 'revenue' (value changes), so P/E, P/S, ROIC and PEG are meaningless, and the PEG formula can give a skewed result. The portfolio is predominantly unlisted growth companies (incl. Spring Health, Perk/TravelPerk, Mews, Cityblock), valued by Kinnevik itself based on listed comparables. Kinnevik has high-voting A shares (approx. 38.5m); control arrangements and insider trades have not been examined in detail. Industry: closest match is 'Finance, data & insurance brokerage'.
