    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Automotive (SKF Vertevo) has not yet been spun off. The listing is planned for Q4 2026 and requires shareholder approval at the general meeting, so the figures are still the group's including Automotive. The 1.3 rule on pro forma applies only from the spin-off. The company has not published pro forma figures for Industrial alone, so the score must be recalculated after the listing. The Q3 report is due 21/10/2026, and the capital markets day is 11/11. Cyclical is set to false: Industrial (about 3/4 of sales and most of earnings) follows industrial production, not a price or commodity cycle, while Automotive is cyclical and about to be spun off. PEG is negative regardless of the flag. No insider buying found; there are only grants from option programmes. Peer multiples (Schaeffler, Timken) have not been checked in detail.
