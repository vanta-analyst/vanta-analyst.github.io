Cracks

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings−15 of −35
  • Salmon price and macro
    The export price for fresh salmon was NOK 71.4/kg in week 38 of 2026 and fell to NOK 48–50/kg in summer 2025, the lowest in four years. Higher supply and a strong NOK could again squeeze price achievement.
    ssb.no
    −4
  • Resource rent tax and regulation
    Since 2023 Norwegian farming has paid resource rent tax (effectively 25%) on top of 22% corporate tax; with a NOK 70m basic deduction it effectively hits only the five largest, including Mowi. Norway accounted for 85,000 of 150,000 t in Q2, so over half the harvest is taxed harder, and the regime could be tightened politically.
    sticos.no
    −4
  • Competition law (EU cartel case)
    In January 2024 the European Commission sent a statement of objections to Mowi and five other Norwegian farmers over illegal exchange of price and volume data in 2011–2019. A fine of up to 10% of global revenue is possible; Mowi rejects the charges.
    salmonbusiness.com
    −3
  • Debt
    Net interest-bearing debt EUR 2.81bn excl. IFRS 16 after Q2. Total debt rose from EUR 2.68bn (2024) to 3.65bn, and net debt/EBITDA is 3.4x vs 1.8–2.3x in 2021–2024; interest cover 5.3x.
    stockanalysis.com
    −3
  • Biology
    Algae and jellyfish attacks (Pseudochattonella) killed 43,601 salmon at Langavika near Stavanger in March 2026; the biological risk recurs across the industry.
    ilaks.no
    −1
