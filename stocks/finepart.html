      Revenue depends on 3-5 machine sales a year: Q4 2025 accounted for 75% of the year's revenue of SEK 23.6m, and the order book was only SEK 1.5m at 30/6-2026. The former largest customer in California changed owner and has sold three of its six machines.

    • Capital allocation
      The share count rose from 41.8m to 329.5m in 2025 (×7.9) via a rights issue at SEK 0.08, TO2 at SEK 0.048 and a directed issue to the underwriters. Issue costs were SEK 4.3m of 20.7m (21%). A 1:500 reverse split followed. In June 2025 machinery was written up by SEK 2.4m, which flatters equity.

      −7

      With six employees in Bollebygd, Finepart is a niche producer of micro waterjet cutting. The technology is patented, but competes with laser micro-machining, wire EDM and larger waterjet manufacturers with global sales and service.
