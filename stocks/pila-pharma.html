    Bull case

    Part 1 shows that 16 mg ×2 is tolerated without hyperthermia and gives a weight signal. Part 2 shows over 5% placebo-adjusted weight loss at 12 weeks with an oral tablet that does not work via incretins, and that could attract an obesity partner. The market cap could then go from about SEK 80m to 300–800m (4–10x). The obesity theme draws a lot of attention even to small signals.

    Bear case

    High doses cause side effects (hyperthermia, reduced heat sensation), or the weight does not move, as in the rat experiments and the diabetes study. Part 2 must be financed with an issue below DKK 1, and the share count doubles. The price could fall toward DKK 0.3–0.7.
