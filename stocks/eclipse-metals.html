    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    ASX: EPM, price AUD 0.014, 3.29bn shares. The main asset is Ivigtût–Grønnedal in South Greenland (the old cryolite mine and the Grønnedal carbonatite with rare earths); there are also smaller Australian projects. stockanalysis' figures only run to December 2025 (TTM), and the annual report for the fiscal year to June 2026 is not included. The resource is 208 Mt at 0.72% TREO, but 97% is Inferred. There is no scoping study or PEA, and the exploitation licence application is planned for 2027. The uranium and thorium content at Grønnedal (relevant for the uranium law) and AFR's mention of a "$20m mystery" about Cape Desolation have not been examined. No analysts.
