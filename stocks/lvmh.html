    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Luxury does not fit the industry list: placed under 'Consumer: food, beverages & restaurants' as the nearest consumer category (like Pandora and adidas). Forward P/E and EPS estimates are adjusted (non-GAAP) figures; analysts (e.g. Barclays 18 Sept.) are cutting Q3 expectations, so the forward multiple may be too low. Q3 revenue is due 9 Oct. 2026. The Arnault family controls about half the capital and the majority of votes – mentioned, but scored only for succession uncertainty. Insider trades, lawsuits (including the Dior subcontractor case in Italy 2024) and rating have not been examined in depth.
