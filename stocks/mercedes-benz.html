Business−20 of −30

Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger?

  • Margin
    The adjusted return on sales at Mercedes-Benz Cars was 4.0% in Q2 2026 (5.1% a year earlier), and management now expects the lower half of its 3–5% guidance for 2026, vs 12–14% in 2022–23. The group operating margin is 6.0% TTM vs 11.5% in 2022–23, and the gross margin has fallen from 22.0% to 16.2%.
    investing.com
    −8
  • ROIC
    ROIC has fallen from 7.2% (2022) and 7.0% (2023) to 3.7% TTM, and ROE from 18.6% to 5.6%. The figure is pulled down by the financing arm, but the trend (halved returns) is the same in the car business.
    stockanalysis.com
    −3
  • Quality of growth
    Neither bigger nor stronger: revenue is €129.5bn TTM vs 152.4bn in 2023 (−7% TTM), EPS 5.28 vs 13.46. Car sales fell 8% in Q2, and the average selling price fell from €67,700 to 64,700, so pricing power in the luxury segment has weakened.
    stockanalysis.com
    −4
  • Future segment (EVs and software)
    EV sales rose 51% in Q2 (87% in Europe), but from a low level: the xEV share is expected to be only 23–25% in 2026. In China, the largest market, Mercedes is losing to local brands strong in EVs and software, and sales in China fell markedly while the market fell about 20%.
    gurufocus.com
    −3
  • Capital allocation
    In H1 2026 €3.3bn was paid in dividends and €2.0bn of own shares bought, €5.3bn in total, vs industrial FCF of €2.96bn (4.22bn a year earlier, including €1.1bn of severance payments). Full-year FCF is expected slightly below €5.4bn, so payouts exceed FCF and erode net liquidity.
    investing.com
    −2

Cracks−15 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • China
    The Chinese market fell about 20% in H1, and Mercedes' sales in China fell markedly. Mercedes wrote down its Chinese investments (including a joint venture) by €844m in Q2, and the Chinese JVs contribute lower earnings. China was the company's largest market in 2025.
    investing.com
    −6
  • US tariffs
    Tariffs cost about €1.1bn in 2025 and were a main reason operating profit fell 57% to €5.8bn. Tariffs on EU cars keep squeezing the margin in 2026, and a large part of US sales is imported from Europe.
    cnbc.com
    −3
  • Regulation (Chinese ownership in the US)
    A US bill would ban carmakers with over 15% Chinese ownership from selling in the US for five years. BAIC (9.98%) and Geely founder Li Shufu (9.69%) together own about 19.7% of Mercedes. A Senate committee passed the bill in July; Mercedes wants the limit raised to 25% and could get a deadline to 2030 or a waiver, but the bill has not yet been passed.
    cnbc.com
    −4
  • Execution (full-year targets)
    UBS points out that the full-year targets depend on a strong fourth quarter. Berenberg cut its target from €56 to 52 in September, and the stock trades just above its 52-week low of €41.05.
    ad-hoc-news.de
    −2

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

stockanalysis' net debt of €85bn and EV of €124bn include Mercedes-Benz Financial Services' loan book; the industrial business itself has net liquidity of €30.4bn (about 80% of the €38bn market value). EV multiples and ROIC are therefore distorted and used only cautiously. Cyclical = yes (car sales follow the economy and credit). The share trades close to its 52-week low (€41.05), RSI 27. The 8.5% dividend yield rests on the dividend for 2025 (€3.50); analysts expect €3.16 for 2026. Q3 comes 28/10.
