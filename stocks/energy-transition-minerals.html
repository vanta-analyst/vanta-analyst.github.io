    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Company without real revenue, listed only on ASX (formerly Greenland Minerals). The Nordnet API does not find the share. Kvanefjeld has no usable NPV (the 2021 uranium law blocks the project), and Penouta in Spain was bought out of a bankruptcy without a published restart study, so EV vs NPV cannot be calculated. The TTM net loss of A$25.7m may include one-offs (litigation, acquisitions); it has not been broken down. There is no analyst coverage. Insider trading has not been examined. The company is considering challenging the licence refusal in the Greenlandic courts.
