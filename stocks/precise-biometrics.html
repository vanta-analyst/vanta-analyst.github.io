    Bull case

    The synergies of SEK 45m are realised, and total revenue (about SEK 156m in 2025) grows to 200m with EBITDA of SEK 40m. At EV/EBITDA 8 plus a net cash of about 80m, equity becomes about SEK 400m, about 3x today. A takeover of the whole company at 1x revenue plus cash gives about 2x.

    Bear case

    Both businesses keep shrinking (Precise −29% in Q2, FPC's sensors under Chinese pressure), the synergies are eaten by restructuring, and the cash is used up in 2028, leading to a new issue. The price could halve, but net cash of about SEK 0.4 per share sets a floor as long as it is not burned.
