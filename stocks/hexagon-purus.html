    Bull case

    The convertibles are restructured so that creditors (Mitsui, Hy24, Hexagon Composites) take a large part of the debt at a price above the current one, costs are cut by a further NOK 500m, and infrastructure keeps growing. With an EV of NOK 2.3bn and a market value of only NOK 184m, even a small value shift from debt to equity could lift the stock 3–5x.

    Bear case

    About NOK 2bn of debt vs NOK 41m of equity: a conversion at a low price or a forced restructuring would leave current shareholders with a few percent of the company – the price could fall 80–100%.
