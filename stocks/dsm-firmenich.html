Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Swiss AG with main listing in Amsterdam. Reported result is negative (EUR −1.41bn TTM) due to impairments on the sale of Animal Nutrition & Health, while core adjusted EPS (EUR 1.84 in H1, +14%) is what analysts use; forward P/E is adjusted. The multiple history covers only 2023–25 (merger with Firmenich in May 2023). The share price (about EUR 98) is above analysts' average price target (EUR 93.9) after a rise of about 42% in 2026; some price targets have hardly been updated. Buybacks give no plus because dividend + buybacks (EUR 1.73bn in 2025) exceeded FCF (0.82bn). The ANH sale to CVC is expected to close in H2 2026. Customer concentration (large FMCG customers), tariffs and currency are not examined. Fragrance/ingredients do not fit exactly in the industry list (placed under food like Novonesis, as Taste, Texture & Health and HNC are more than half of sales).
