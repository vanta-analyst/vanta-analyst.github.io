    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −13 of −30
    • Capital allocation (ÖoB)
      The ÖoB takeover lifted revenue from NOK 9.5bn to 15.1bn, but EBIT has not risen (1.40bn TTM vs 1.51bn in 2021). EPS has fallen from 6.72 to 5.24, ROIC has halved from 21.8% to 11.5%, and ÖoB still lost NOK 107m in H1 2026. Europris has become bigger, not stronger.
      stockanalysis.com
      −8
    • Margin
      The gross margin has fallen from 46.9% (2021) to 41.4% and the operating margin from 17.5% to 9.2%. Wage increases of about 5% and higher handling and distribution costs keep squeezing.
      investing.com
      −3
    • Moat
      Europris is under pressure from Normal (everyday low prices), Rusta, Jula, NorgesGruppen's Dollarstore/Gigaboks and online players such as Temu. A broad discount range has little differentiation.
      nettavisen.no
      −2
