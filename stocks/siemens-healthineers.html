    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Siemens AG owns about 67% and will distribute about 30% of the Healthineers shares directly to its shareholders after a vote at Siemens' AGM in February 2027, and over time go below 20%; free float therefore rises from about 32% to over 60%. FY2026 EPS guidance is lifted by a one-off refund of US tariffs (IEEPA), and Q3 FCF was also helped by it. A large part of the debt stems from the Varian acquisition and financing via Siemens; how it will be refinanced after the separation has not been examined. Competition from Chinese imaging makers and insider trading have not been examined. Q4 results 5/11.
