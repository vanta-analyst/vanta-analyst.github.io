    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Acciona S.A. owns 91.1% and is exploring anything from a minority partner to a full sale or delisting; the share price may therefore be affected by bid speculation and is not fundamental – no extra points have been given for it. Reported earnings are dominated by gains on asset sales (EUR 697m in 2025, EUR 206m TTM); there is no official adjusted earnings figure in the sources. Stockanalysis' FCF forecast of EUR 1.59bn for 2026 probably includes proceeds from asset sales and has not been used. The share count (−0.6%) is negligible and not used. Price targets have not been checked for updates. Cyclical set to false per the rule for utilities, even though earnings largely follow power prices (captured price −13.7% in H1); flagged as a model observation. Insider trading and US tariffs on solar panels have not been investigated.
