    Bull case

    The projects in Greece and Romania are delivered with a positive gross margin, new orders of SEK 300m+ are coming, and revenue of SEK 300–400m gives positive EBITDA. At 1x sales the market cap would be SEK 300–400m, about 1.5–2x – a tripling also requires a new hydrogen wave and no further dilution.

    Bear case

    Milestone payments are delayed, the bridge loan squeezes cash, and the company must do another share issue far below SEK 0.20 (the previous one was subscribed only 29%). Shareholders are heavily diluted again, and the price could halve or more.
