    Bull case

    The 400 ramp gives access to broader zones, Mallay reaches 600 t/d in 2027 and produces approx. 2m oz AgEq a year at silver above $70/oz, and deeper drilling increases the resource markedly. With EBITDA of C$40–60m and EV/EBITDA of 5–6, the market cap could reach C$300–450m – 2–3x today's; Tres Cerros and Kilgore are free options.

    Bear case

    The ramp-up and stable operation are delayed again, the narrow remaining zones give low grade and high costs, and cash runs out, so shares must be issued at a low price. The company has previously closed Platosa and had negative equity; a new failure could send the stock to 0.10–0.15 C$.
