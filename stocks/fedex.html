    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −11 of −30
    • Margin
      The continuing company (without Freight) had an operating margin of only 5.5% in calendar 2025 (revenue $82.5bn, operating profit $4.55bn). The high-margin LTL business is gone, and the Federal Express segment's adjusted margin fell in Q4 to 7.7% from 8.4% a year earlier, because wages, purchased transport and fuel rose faster than revenue.
      sec.gov
      −5
    • ROIC
      ROIC 9.2% vs a WACC of 7.9%; flat at 8.3–9.5% in FY2022–26. A capital-heavy network that earns only slightly more than its cost of capital, and the figure still includes the more profitable Freight.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 7.7% in FY2026, but driven by prices and fuel surcharges; the CEO himself said the margin would only have risen without the fuel surcharge. Analysts expect revenue −0.3% and EPS +3.1% next year and only 1.75% annual revenue growth over three years. Revenue was flat from FY2022 ($93.5bn) to FY2025 ($87.9bn).
      stockanalysis.com
      −4

    Today's multiples against the company's own history and its peers. · 1 finding    −13 of −35
    • Multiples vs own history and peers
      The forward P/E on the continuing company is about 16.5 (price $289.65 vs guidance of $16.90–18.10) and about 19.5 on 2025 EPS ($14.83), vs a forward P/E of 10.0–13.1 in FY2022–26 for the whole group including Freight. GuruFocus: P/E 21% above the 5-year median of 12.4. EV/EBITDA of 8.3 is mid-range of 7.6–11.2 and P/FCF 13.4 below 17.5–20.5, but both are calculated on cash flow incl. Freight and lifted by record-low investment. You pay a higher multiple than historically for a lower-margin business.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −20 of −35
    • Customers are shifting
      Amazon's and retailers' own delivery networks are taking market share from FedEx and UPS (ShipMatrix). Morgan Stanley cut its target to $160 from 230, citing Amazon's expansion into third-party shipping, and reiterated Sell on 18/9.
      tikr.com
      −5
    • Guidance and execution
      2026 guidance of $16.90–18.10 in adjusted EPS was clearly below consensus of about $19.9, and the stock fell about 6–7% after the report on 23/6. Management has warned of about $600m of stranded overhead after the spin-off and a headwind of about $200m from a new pilot contract.
      insiderfinance.io
      −4
    • Governance (CFO change)
      CFO John Dietrich has left, and Claude Russ is interim CFO and chief accounting officer in the middle of the spin-off and the change of fiscal year. The company enters its first report as a pure parcel company without a permanent CFO.
      ortex.news
      −3
    • Debt and pensions
      Net debt $29.4bn (net debt/EBITDA 2.5 including Freight EBITDA, which is now gone); total debt $42.9bn and Altman Z 2.56. Pension contributions of $475m are budgeted for 2026.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Insider trading
      Insiders have sold about $18.1m over 12 months with no purchases. In April–June 2026 Chief People Officer Tracy Brightman sold $6.2m and directors Susan Schwab and Paul Walsh $2.1m and 1.6m.
      secform4.com
      −2
    • Macro
      Tariffs and the EU's removal of the €150 de minimis threshold from 1/7/2026 create friction in international e-commerce, and US 10-year yields above 5% with rising recession fears hit a cyclical freight company with a beta of 1.36. The stock fell 4.1% to $279.54 on 24/9.
      pomegra.io
      −4

    • Costs and cash flow
      The transformation programme delivered over $1bn of savings in FY2026; investment fell to 4.0% of revenue, the lowest in FedEx's history, and FCF rose to a record $5.1bn (from 3.0bn).
      investors.fedex.com

      Buybacks ($0.8bn) and dividends ($1.37bn) totalled $2.2bn in FY2026, clearly below FCF of $5.1bn; the share count fell 1.65%. In FY2025, though, payouts exceeded FCF ($4.4bn vs 3.0bn).
      stockanalysis.com
      +1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    FedEx Freight was spun off on 1/6/2026, and the fiscal year has changed to the calendar year (transition period June–Dec. 2026). stockanalysis' key figures (P/E 15.6, ROIC, debt) still mix Freight earnings with the post-spin-off price, so the P/E on the continuing company is in reality about 16.5 (guidance) to 19.5 (2025). The first pure parcel report has not yet been published, and the CFO post is filled on an interim basis.
