      Organic growth fell from 4.5% in 2024 to −0.5% in 2025 and only 1.5% in H1 2026; net revenue in H1 was €1,907m (+1%). Gross revenue has fallen from €5.0bn in 2023 to €4.9bn (TTM), and Places has fallen from 1.94 to €1.76bn.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • ROIC
      ROIC 11.5% (TTM) vs 14.9% in 2024 and 15.3% in 2021 – falling trend, only slightly above WACC of 7.5%.

      The board unanimously rejected WSP's bids of €48.50 and €51.50 (half cash, half shares) without negotiating; WSP withdrew on 23/9, and the stock trades about €10 below the last bid.     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Consulting engineering business – no industry fits exactly; 'Construction & materials' is the closest. Arcadis reports on net revenue (about EUR 3.8bn) and 'operating EBITA'; stockanalysis uses gross revenue (EUR 4.9bn) and an EBITDA without lease depreciation, so debt/EBITDA of 3.1 is exaggerated relative to the company's own definition. The share price fell about 10% intraday on 23/9 when WSP abandoned the bid; price targets (latest 4/8) partly reflect bid speculation. CMD 29/9 gave targets for 2027–29, but the divestment of architecture and China and 1,000 positions cost one-off costs, which are not quantified. Peer multiples (WSP, Stantec) are not retrieved. Q3 trading update 29/10.
