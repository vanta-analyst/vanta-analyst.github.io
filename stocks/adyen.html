      CFO Ethan Tandowsky left on 31/8 to go outside fintech; an interim CFO runs finance, and Klarna's CFO Niclas Neglen only starts on 1/2/2027 (requires DNB approval and an extraordinary general meeting). The stock fell 4.7% on the news on 24/9.
      ad-hoc-news.de

    • Guidance
      Q4 2025 missed by about 2% on revenue and volume, and the 2026 guidance (20–22%) was below the earlier ambition of growth in the low-to-mid twenties and about 5% below consensus on margin; the stock fell 17%.
      investing.com
      −3
    • Customers are shifting
      The take rate was 16.2 bp in H1 2026: volume grew 24%, but net revenue only 19% (21% in constant currency), because the largest customers move down to cheaper price tiers. The goal of stabilising the take rate around 16.5 bp has not been met. The more volume, the lower the price per transaction – payment processing is becoming more of a commodity.
      nikhs.substack.com
      −3
    • Customer concentration
      One large customer left in H2 2025: volume growth was 12% including and 19% excluding this customer. Single customers can move growth by several percentage points.
