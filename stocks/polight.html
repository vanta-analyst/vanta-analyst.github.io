    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −25 of −30
    • Margin
      The operating loss is NOK 127m on revenue of 34m TTM (operating margin −371%). The loss has grown every year from −NOK 54m (2021) to −127m, and there has never been a profit. Gross margin swings from −8% to 69% per quarter.
      stockanalysis.com
      −12
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue is +245% TTM, but from a very low level (NOK 34m after more than 10 years on the market). It has swung widely: 22.5m in 2023, 9.6m in 2024 and 20.5m in 2025. AR orders in Q2 totalled about NOK 4m. There has not yet been a single consumer product launch in volume.
      inderes.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      Ongoing dilution: 63.7m new shares to Q Tech at DKK 2.69 (33% of the company) and a subsequent issue of up to 19.1m shares in 2025. The share count has roughly quadrupled since 2021 (about 47m adjusted for 1:5 split). ROIC is -24%.
      silicon.co.uk
      −5
    • Moat
      TLens (piezo-based autofocus) is patented and unique in speed, size and power consumption, which is relevant for AR glasses. It must, however, displace cheap VCM autofocus motors (TDK, Alps Alpine), and liquid lenses (Corning Varioptic, Optotune) compete in the niches. The smartphone breakthrough after 2019 failed to materialise.

      The entire investment case rests on one consumer AR design-in at an unnamed OEM (upgraded after an order of NOK 0.8m 10/7-2026) with an expected product launch 'during 2026'. Volume, price per unit and the customer's identity are unknown.
