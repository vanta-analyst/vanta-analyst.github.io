    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −13 of −30
    • Future segment (robotaxis)
      Self-driving cars could make Uber's driver network less valuable and let AV owners go straight to the customer. Waymo now operates commercially in 10 US cities with its own app (Dallas, Houston, San Antonio and Orlando from February 2026), aims for over 1m rides a week by year-end and is preparing 20+ cities. Tesla is building its own robotaxi network. Uber wants to be 'the world's largest AV platform', but owns neither the technology nor the fleet.
      wusf.org
      −7
    • Margin and share-based pay
      GAAP operating margin has risen from 3.0% (2023) to 12.1% TTM, and ROIC from 5.8% to 19.2%, so the business is getting stronger. But adjusted EBITDA is only 4.9% of gross bookings, and share-based pay of $1.94bn TTM equals 29% of operating profit ($6.7bn). The margin is thin for a platform.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Uber has a continuous stream of cash acquisitions (USD 647m in Q2 2026 and USD 804m in Q2 2025) and a large portfolio of equity stakes (Didi, Grab and AV companies), whose fair value adjustments swing earnings by billions each quarter. Capital is spread across many bets.
      s23.q4cdn.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −10 of −35
    • Partner leaves the platform
      Waymo and Uber ended their cooperation in Phoenix in May 2026. Waymo is lifting exclusivity in Austin and Atlanta and launching its own app there from January 2028; the current fleet stays on Uber until May 2028. The disagreement is about economics, routing and cleaning. The leading AV operator is thus choosing to compete rather than distribute via Uber.
      thenextweb.com
      −4
    • Litigation and regulation
      The FTC and 21 states plus DC are suing Uber over deceptive Uber One subscriptions (sign-up without consent, up to 23 screens to cancel). Trial is set for 20/9/2027, and a private class action was filed on 1/10/2026. Q2 included $141m in new legal and regulatory provisions.
      abcnews4.com
      −3
    • Quality of cash flow
      FCF of $10.1bn TTM is flattered by share-based pay ($1.94bn, not a cash expense) and by growing insurance provisions (+$0.8bn in H1 2026; $387m of Q2's operating cash flow). These are reserves for future claims, not free cash.
      s23.q4cdn.com
      −2
    • Debt
      Net debt has risen from USD 0.1bn (end 2025) to USD 4.7bn (June 2026). Buybacks, acquisitions and investments have swallowed the cash, and net borrowing rose USD 1.7bn TTM.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    GAAP earnings are heavily distorted: TTM includes a tax gain of $4.9bn (release of valuation allowance on Dutch deferred tax assets, Q3 2025) and about $3.1bn in gains on equity stakes (Q3 2025 and Q2 2026). Trailing P/E 15.6 and thus PEG 0.65 are based on inflated earnings; adjusted trailing P/E is about 28. Robotaxi risk cannot be quantified. The stock is down 24% in 52 weeks, just above the 25% safeguard for stale price targets. The Q3 report comes on 4/11 and is not included. The sexual assault cases are not examined.
