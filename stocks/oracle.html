    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • Margin
      The gross margin has fallen from 79.1% (FY22) to 71.4% (FY24), 65.8% (FY26) and 64.0% TTM, and analysts expect 59.3% in FY27. The GAAP operating margin has so far risen to 34%, but the mix is shifting towards low-margin business.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen every year from 20.8% (FY22) to 16.1% (FY24), 13.2% (FY26) and 11.3% TTM vs a WACC of 10.5%; ROCE is 9.6%. According to internal documents (The Information), GPU rental yields a gross margin of only 14–16%.
      datacenterdynamics.com
      −6
    • Quality of growth
      In Q1 FY27 cloud infrastructure (IaaS) grew 121% to $7.4bn, while the high-margin software business fell 3% to $5.5bn and SaaS grew only 10%. Growth comes from capital-heavy GPU rental, not the strong core.
      sec.gov
      −7
    • Capital allocation
      Capex of $75.7bn TTM is funded with $40bn of new net debt in FY26 and a $20bn share sale via an ATM programme, now completed (share count +2.9% in one quarter). About $40bn more must be raised in FY27, yet the dividend ($5.8bn a year) is maintained.
      sec.gov
      −5
