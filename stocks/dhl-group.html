    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company has been called DHL AG since 1 Sept 2026 (formerly Deutsche Post). Stockanalysis' FCF (7.1 bn) is before lease payments; the company's own FCF (excl. M&A) is about 3 bn EUR, and the payout is measured against that. Q2 is lifted by a one-off effect of about 150 m from the Iran war's air freight capacity shortage. The share price (57 EUR, +51% over a year) is above the analysts' average price target (55.12), consensus 'Hold'. Peers (UPS, FedEx, DSV) and union relations have not been investigated.
