    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B, P/TBV and P/E. Growth in Turkey and Argentina is measured in high-inflation currency (hyperinflation accounting), so nominal growth overstates real growth. The failed Sabadell bid (Oct 2025) has not been counted as a finding. Consensus is 'Hold', and the average price target (23.2) is below the price (25.2); upside points are formulaic and not counted here.
