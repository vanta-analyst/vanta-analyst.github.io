    Bull case

    The first commercial pre-liquefier is delivered, GenH2 gets more orders for storage systems (buses, space, industry), and ProtonH2 shows cheap geological hydrogen. With revenue of EUR 10–15m in 2–3 years and EV/revenue 4–6, EV could reach EUR 40–90m; after debt and dilution of 30–50% that gives 1.5–3x – a 10x requires the ProtonH2 technology to work at large scale.

    Bear case

    Delivery of the first unit is delayed, the hydrogen market stays cold, and the major shareholder stops lending money. The cash (a few million EUR after leaseback) is burned in 1-1.5 years, and an issue at a very low price or an insolvency hits the few free shareholders. The AmeriMark stake at fair value may prove hard to realize.
