    • Quality of growth
      Revenue grew only 5% in Q2 (6,651 vs 6,337m kr.) and is expected at only +3.5% in 2026 vs the target of 10% annual growth. Growth comes from new builds, while the high-margin aftermarket business is weak: customers are postponing larger upgrade projects in particular.

      Offshore Energy is the largest segment in newbuild orders in H1 2026 (ahead of Naval & Government, Cargo and Tug), so a large share of order intake is tied to oil and gas investment rather than the green/autonomous shipping that is the investment case.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Spun off from Kongsberg Gruppen and listed 23 Apr. 2026; the history is pro forma figures from the parent's segment (2023–2025), so there is no own multiple history, and T2 is based on absolute level and peers. EPS 2025 (DKK 4.30) is lifted by one-offs (pre-tax margin 16.8% vs operating margin 12.8%), so analysts' 2026E EPS of DKK 2.86 is a decline. Stockanalysis shows 31% revenue growth TTM, which is a pro forma distortion; the real growth rates are 6.3% in 2025 and 5% in Q2 2026. Not flagged cyclical, but 87% of the order book is tied to newbuilds and thus to the shipyard cycle. Insider trades have not been examined.
