    Penny deep dive (the price is above DKK 3). Canadian company (London, Ontario) with hydrochemolytic technology (HCT) that converts plastic waste, heavy oil and fats into hydrocarbons; listed on Nasdaq (ADUR) and in Canada, so the group is set to USA after the Nasdaq listing. Fiscal year June-May in CAD; amounts in the penny object are converted to USD (1 CAD = 0.705 USD). A price of USD 11.11 (9/10-2026) and 35.70m shares at 31/8-2026 give a market cap of USD 397m (DKK 2,611m; Nordnet showed 2,755). Cash is a pro forma estimate: CAD 38.3m at 31/5-2026 plus the June offerings (about USD 23.3m gross) before costs and burn in June-August. The FOAK plant's total capex budget and financing plan have not been disclosed. Insider trades in 2026 have not been found. Industry is set to the closest (chemical recycling). The next report (Q1 FY2027) is expected 23/10-2026.
