    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Advertising and media agencies do not fit the list; the nearest industry is 'Software, cloud & internet' (the data and technology part: Epsilon, Sapient). Publicis steers by net revenue (excl. pass-through media buying, about 15 bn €) and 'headline' figures: headline operating margin 18.2% in 2025 and headline EPS 7.48 €, while reported EPS is 6.52 € (2025) and 6.42 € TTM; stockanalysis' revenue (17.65 bn €) is gross revenue. Net debt of 3.3 bn € at 30/6 is seasonal (dividend of 0.9 bn € in H1 and working capital); the LiveRamp acquisition (EV 2.2 bn USD, cash) closes only around year-end and is not in the figures. Élisabeth Badinter owns about 7% with double votes – mentioned, not scored. Client concentration, specific account losses and insider trading have not been investigated. Q3 revenue about 15/10.
