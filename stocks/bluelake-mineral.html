    Bull case

    The pre-feasibility study shows robust economics for Joma and Stekenjokk-Levi at the current high copper price, LNS becomes contractor and possible co-financier, and the environmental permits go through. A revaluation toward Mangold's DCF price target of 3.35 SEK gives about 2.5x. A 3–5x (market cap 0.8–1.3 bn SEK) requires a PFS NPV of several bn SEK and a financing solution with limited dilution.

    Bear case

    The pre-feasibility study disappoints, or the permits drag on for years while the copper price falls. The share price falls below 1.70 SEK, so TO7 is not exercised, and the company must raise money at 0.5–1 SEK with heavy dilution. The construction phase may require many times the market cap: −60–85%.
