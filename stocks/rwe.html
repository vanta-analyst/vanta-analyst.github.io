    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' IFRS figures are distorted by unrealised fair-value adjustments of derivatives: operating profit €0.48bn and EBITDA €2.4bn TTM vs. the company's adjusted EBITDA guidance of €5.57–6.35bn for 2026, so ROIC 0.5%, EV/EBITDA 18.6 and interest cover 0.9x are not representative; the findings are based on the company's own adjusted figures where possible. The 'share issuance' item of €8.4bn TTM is probably capital from partner sales of minority stakes (incl. KKR), not new RWE shares – the share count is −3.1% over a year. Cyclical is set to false: about 40% of H1 EBITDA came from Flexible Generation and Supply & Trading (market prices, spreads, capacity payments), but the majority is contracted or regulated renewable energy, and 75% of 2031 EPS is secured via long contracts; the assessment is borderline. Credit rating and insider trading have not been investigated.
