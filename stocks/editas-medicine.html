    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Penny deep dive (the price of DKK 19.09 is above DKK 3). CRISPR company that, after the stop of reni-cel, now has only one clinical track: EDIT-401 (in vivo editing that upregulates the LDL receptor). A price of USD 2.90 (stockanalysis 9/10-2026) and 153.58m shares at 31/7-2026 (10-Q) give a market cap of about USD 445m. Nordnet showed DKK 2,780m. Stockanalysis' dilution of +21.7% is misleading: the share count has risen from about 84m (Q2 2025, calculated from EPS) to 153.6m, i.e. about +82% in 12 months. The number of pre-funded warrants from the May offering has not been disclosed. Revenue (47m TTM) is mostly booked deferred license revenue and is not cash. New ATM of USD 150m (Leerink) under a USD 400m shelf filed 31/8-2026. No going concern warning. Largest owners and insider trades not reviewed in detail.
