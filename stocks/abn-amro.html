    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valued on P/B, P/TBV and P/E, and payouts are measured against net profit. The trailing P/E of 15.7 is on reported EPS of 2.78; the forward P/E of 11.9–14.2 is on adjusted EPS and assumes +26% EPS in 2026, partly from consolidating NIBC. NIBC is consolidated from Q3 and lowers CET1 by 70–75 bp (pro forma 15.9%). Stockanalysis' dividend per share (1.24, 2.8% yield) looks low against the forecast (1.70 for 2026). The state's stake (NLFI) has not been confirmed exactly; free float 550m of 815m shares. The 2021 money-laundering settlement is not included because no working source was found. The median price target is only about 3% above the price. Q3 report on 11 Nov.
