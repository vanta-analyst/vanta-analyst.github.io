      The agreement with GE Vernova Hitachi and Samsung C&T on four BWRX-300 (1.2 GW) is neither an investment decision nor a construction permit, and the first reactor is expected mid-2030s at the earliest. The project depends on state support (application submitted to the Ministry of Finance in June 2026) and on Swedish energy policy.
