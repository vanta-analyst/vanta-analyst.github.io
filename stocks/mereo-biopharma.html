    Bull case

    FDA accepts a pediatric indication for setrusumab based on bone density and vertebral fractures, so Ultragenyx milestones become realistic, and Sentynl exercises the option (40 m USD ≈ 60% of market cap). With an EV of only 35 m USD this could give 3–5x; 10x requires positive phase 3 data for alvelestat.

    Bear case

    FDA requires a new study, Ultragenyx gives up setrusumab, and Sentynl does not exercise the option. The cash (30 m) is burned down in 2027, and an issue at a low price plus the class action presses the stock a further 50–70%.
