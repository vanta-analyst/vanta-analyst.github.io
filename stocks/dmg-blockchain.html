    Bull case

    A final 12-year agreement for 50 MW with an investment grade tenant could give annual rental income in the neighbourhood of C$100m+ (at typical colocation prices), and other miners that have made similar deals have been re-rated many times. If the project is financed mainly with debt against the lease, the stock could rise 3–5x.

    Bear case

    The letter of intent falls through, or the deal requires a large share/convertible issue; at the same time the bitcoin price falls, so both mining income and the BTC holding shrink while the loans must be repaid. Without the AI story the stock is worth about the liquid assets and the machines (P/B 0.5–0.8, about C$0.20–0.30), i.e. −40–60%.
