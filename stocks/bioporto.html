    Bull case

    NGAL sales in the US are growing 30–40% a year, and the FDA approves the adult indication in 2028, which opens a much larger market. Revenue reaches DKK 150–200m in 2029–30 with break-even; at EV/Sales 6–8 that equals DKK 1–1.5bn, i.e. 2–3x. 5–10x requires rapid adoption in US hospitals or an acquisition.

    Bear case

    Further delays or weak growth data, slow adoption and more quality problems. Cash is empty in 2027, and a series of share issues at DKK 0.6–0.8 dilutes 30–50%. The price could halve or worse.
