    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −15 of −30
    • Future segment
      Tender-assisted drilling rigs for mature gas fields in the Gulf of Thailand plus 2D seismic (SeaBird) is a fossil niche segment without structural growth. The Ventura merger would add ultra-deepwater drillships for Petrobras, which is a better segment, but it is only a letter of intent.
      worldoil.com
      −4
    • Moat and pricing power
      PTTEP gave two long-term contracts (the work EDrill-1 and T-15 perform today) to Foresight Offshore Drilling at an estimated day rate of approx. 70,000 USD. Pricing power over the competitors is limited.
      smallcaptreasures.substack.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has risen 48% TTM, but driven by the merger and high utilization. Analysts now expect revenue -15% and EPS -32% in 2027 (previously -10% and -19%), and the next growth leap is another acquisition (Ventura).
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has risen 11% over a year at the same time as distributions of 109% of profit. Now a pure share purchase is planned with up to 605m new shares (+83%) at a price that is 22% lower than a year ago, and the company goes from almost debt-free to net debt.
      energy-pedia.com
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −30 of −35
    • Contract expiry and lost tender
      EDrill-1 and T-16 expire in Nov. 2026 and T-15 in Jan. 2027 (50% of the rig fleet). The PTTEP tender for two of the rigs was lost to Foresight (about USD 70,000/day), and three tenders will only be decided in Q4. Earnings and distribution in 2027 are unresolved, and Pareto cut the price target to NOK 8.50 from 10.50 (29/9).
      marketscreener.com
      −12
    • Customer concentration
      PTTEP is the dominant customer and has options on several rigs, and Energy Drilling accounts for 88% of revenue. With Ventura, Petrobras joins as almost the only customer for the drillships.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Macro and regulation
      The company depends on oil and gas companies' capex in Southeast Asia (incl. Myanmar with sanctions and governance risk), and seismic is highly cyclical.
      marketscreener.com
      −5
    • Ownership structure and liquidity
      Pioneer Logistics owns approx. 40%, and the free float is only approx. 157m of 731m shares. HitecVision sold its entire stake (approx. 1bn NOK) in June, and the share price is −22% over 52 weeks.
      marketscreener.com
      −3
    • Merger and refinancing (Ventura)
      Non-binding LOI (11/9) to buy Ventura Offshore for up to 605m new shares, so the Ventura owners get c. 45%. The exchange ratio depends on new contract starts and capex. Ventura's 10% bond matures in April 2027 and must be refinanced via a USD 250m bridge from DNB, and pro forma net debt will be c. USD 240m. The drillship Catarina lacks a contract after 2026, and closing (Q1 2027) requires due diligence, a general meeting and court approval.
      freightandbarrels.substack.com
      −5
