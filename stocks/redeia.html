    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Redeia (formerly Red Eléctrica) is the regulated Spanish transmission monopoly and system operator, so negative FCF in an investment phase is partly structural. Stockanalysis' 'Issuance of Common Stock' (1.8 bn € TTM) is hybrid bonds (incl. 500 m € in April 2026), not equity issuance – the share count is unchanged (+0.06%), so no deduction for dilution has been given. Net income in 2024 was weighed down by the write-down/sale of the satellite company Hispasat, so the EPS growth in 2025 (+32%) is a base effect. According to Spanish media, CNMC's sanction case could result in a fine of 6–60 m € (the amount is not verified in the source used); compensation claims from the 28/4-2025 power outage and state ownership (SEPI 20%) have not been examined further. Credit rating and insider trading have not been investigated.
