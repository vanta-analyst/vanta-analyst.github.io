    Bull case

    The ISP approves, Bacchus completes the investment (company fully funded through 2027), the environmental application is filed, and the market starts pricing the PFS NPV8 of EUR 924m at just 1–2%. That equals EUR 9–18m over about 190m shares after dilution – around 5–10 pence, i.e. 1–2x today's price. A 3–10x requires an environmental permit and a large steel partner.

    Bear case

    The ISP rejects or drags it out, the bridge financing lasts only a few weeks, and the company has to raise money far below 3 pence or go into administration. With build capex of about 60 times the market value and strong Sami opposition, Kallak could remain a paper project.
