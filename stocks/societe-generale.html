    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (stockanalysis shows capex of EUR 8.8bn, which is Ayvens' leasing cars); valuation on P/B and P/E, and the payout is held against net profit. The trailing P/E history (5.1–16.0) is distorted by the Russia loss in 2022; P/B is used as the main multiple. Peers: P/B 0.64 and forward P/E 8.3 at ROE 9.5% are in line with BNP Paribas (0.78/7.9/10.5%) and Deutsche Bank (0.73/8.5/8.9%), so no peer deduction. Consensus EPS is adjusted. The Capital Markets Day on 21 Sep set targets of RoTE 13–14% in 2029 and over 15% in 2030; execution is unproven. Compliance history and the BoursoBank strategy have not been investigated in depth.
