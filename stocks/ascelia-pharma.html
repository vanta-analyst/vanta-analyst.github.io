    Bull case

    The FDA's minutes show that the CRL can be resolved with documentation and a reanalysis without a new study, and resubmission takes place in 2027 (class 2, about 6 months of review). A partner with payment on signing and on approval gives funding without heavy dilution. The market cap could then go from about 110 m to 400–800 m SEK (previously 400–1,000 m SEK before the CRL), i.e. 3–7x.

    Bear case

    FDA requires a new prospective re-read or a new phase 3 study. Then 100+ m SEK must be raised at 0.3–0.5 kr., the share count doubles or triples, and the share price could fall toward 0.2–0.3 kr. Without a partner and with only 8 months of cash, there is a real risk of an emergency issue.
