    Bull case

    BGN deliveries continue at about USD 10m a month (about 120m annually), the SAF conversion succeeds, GNCU grants forbearance, and the merger brings capital. With a positive gross margin and P/S of 1–1.5, the market cap could reach USD 150–250m after dilution – at most 1–2x today, because USD 257m of debt ranks ahead of the shareholders.

    Bear case

    GNCU accelerates the loan again, or the BGN deal fails: the plant is taken over by creditors and shareholders lose everything. Alternatively the stock is delisted to OTC in December 2026 – effectively zero for a Nordnet investor.
