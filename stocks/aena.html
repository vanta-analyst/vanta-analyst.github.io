    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The share was split 10:1 in 2025; stockanalysis' history is adjusted. The Spanish state owns 51% via Enaire, and tariffs are set by the government (DORA). Stockanalysis' FCF of EUR 2.18bn TTM is before the coming investment wave; analysts expect EUR 1.74bn in 2026, and DORA III quintuples annual investment from 2027 – used in the valuation. EPS forecasts are adjusted; reported EPS is EUR 1.49 TTM. Price targets (average EUR 27.12) were last updated 21 Aug – before DORA III was adopted on 15 Sep. Luton, Brazil (BOAB) and the newly acquired Leeds Bradford/Newcastle have not been analysed separately. Insider trading not relevant (state majority).
