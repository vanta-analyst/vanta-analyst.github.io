    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −10 of −30
    • ROIC and capital allocation
      ROIC fell from 9.8% (2023) to 5.8% (2024), 7.3% (2025) and 6.5% TTM – only slightly above WACC of about 5.6%. Impairments on equity holdings (EUR 687m in 2024) and the FIFCO purchase for USD 3.2bn (completed Jan. 2026) have weighed on the return on capital.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell in both 2024 (−1.8%) and 2025 (−3.6%); in H1 2026 organic growth was only 2.7%, and consolidated volume rose just 0.4% – total volume (+1.6%) is lifted by licence volume (+23%) in China and India, which generates far less earnings.
      theheinekencompany.com
      −3
    • Margin
      Reported operating margin 11.9% TTM vs 13.9–14.1% in 2021–22, and net margin only 7.7% (15.2% in 2021). The lifted beia margin (14.6%) rests on cost savings, not volume.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Future segment
      Beer in mature markets is stagnating: Europe volume −0.6% in H1, and analysts questioned China, where the beer market is falling. Growth has to come from Vietnam, Ethiopia, India and Nigeria with higher currency and macro risk.
      investing.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −8 of −35
    • Customers are on the move (Americas)
      Americas volume fell 3.4% in H1 with flat revenue: declines in Mexico in a weak consumer economy, the Brazilian beer market −3.6% and subdued consumer confidence in the US. Management declared itself dissatisfied with the region, which is about a third of revenue.
      investing.com
      −3
    • Debt
      Net debt/EBITDA 2.6x after H1 (above the target of below 2.5x) after FIFCO; stockanalysis shows net debt of EUR 17.8bn (3.2x EBITDA), current ratio 0.75 and quick ratio 0.51. Management itself said that pro forma leverage is approaching the ceiling for the rating.
      foodbusinessmea.com
      −3
    • Management change
      New external CEO Rafa Oliveira takes over 1/10/2026 for four years; analysts expect him, as an outsider, to implement major changes (transition and execution risk), and the share closed at its lowest level since June (EUR 69.48).
      cashcow.nl
      −1
    • Governance
      Heineken Holding (controlled by the de Carvalho-Heineken family via L'Arche Green) owns just over 50% of Heineken N.V.; the free float is only about 259m of 547m shares, so minority shareholders have limited influence.
      en.wikipedia.org
      −1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Management reports 'beia' figures (before exceptional items): beia operating margin of 14.6% in H1 vs reported operating margin of 11.9% TTM at stockanalysis; forward P/E (12.6) is based on adjusted EPS. BofA cut its price target from 88 to 76 EUR (Sept.), while Jefferies and Bernstein raised theirs – price targets enter only via the formulas. New CEO Rafa Oliveira (formerly JDE Peet's) takes over 1/10/2026. Analysts' FCF estimate for 2026 (2.9bn) is far below TTM FCF (4.0bn).
