    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Betolar started with cement-free building materials (Geoprime) and now has three business areas: metal recovery from mine waste (MET), protection of critical infrastructure (CIP) and circular materials. 'Construction & materials' is chosen as the nearest industry; the metal recovery could also belong under 'Commodities & uranium'. The share count is 21.57m according to the notice for the extraordinary general meeting (July 2026); the convertible announcement mentions 23.08m incl. something that is not clarified. Nordnet's market cap (DKK 139m) is outdated; the market cap is calculated by ourselves: 21.57 × 0.98 = EUR 21.1m ≈ DKK 158m. It is unclear whether the EUR 3m loan from Danske Bank was drawn on 30/6, and whether the convertible notes (3m) were paid in; runway is calculated with cash on 30/6 plus the loan. No analysts on stockanalysis. Short register and insider trades have not been systematically checked. The stock is very illiquid (about DKK 9,000 a day).
