    Bull case

    Fujirebio signs a licence for the blood test (a shorter route to market), and phase 2 starts on time. Once TO5/TO6 are exercised, the study is fully funded. A partner or licence on ALZ-101 with Fast Track could lift the diluted value from about SEK 0.24bn to 1–2bn (4–8x on the diluted value, more on today's market value).

    Bear case

    The share issue is taken up only by the guarantors, who sell their shares. TO5 at DKK 0.50 is not exercised because the price is below. Phase 2 cannot be funded without another issue at a lower price, and the Fujirebio track fizzles out. The share count triples, and the price could fall towards DKK 0.10–0.20.
