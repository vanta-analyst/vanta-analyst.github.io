    Bull case

    Summer drilling finds more high-grade lenses, so Pike Zone can grow towards a first resource; high-grade discoveries in southeastern Athabasca (neighbours of McArthur River) have historically led to takeovers. With an EV of only about CAD 40m, a few strong holes could send market cap back to the 2025 level (about CAD 150–250m), i.e. 2–4x.

    Bear case

    The summer results disappoint, uranium stocks fall further, and the company must raise CAD 10–15m at CAD 0.20–0.30 (+20–35% shares) to keep drilling. Cash covers about 18 months, so zero is not imminent, but a slow dilution towards CAD 0.10–0.20 is realistic.
