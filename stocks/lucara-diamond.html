    Bull case

    The UGP is delivered in H1 2028 with no new overruns, the market for large stones holds, and Karowe returns to USD 200m+ in revenue with about 50% gross margin. With EBITDA of USD 100–120m and net debt of about USD 200m, an EV/EBITDA of 4–5 could give a market cap of USD 250–400m – about 1.5–2.5x today's. A 3x requires a marked recovery in diamond prices or large finds like Sewelô (1,758 ct).

    Bear case

    The stockpile ore gives lower income in 2027, diamond prices fall further under pressure from lab-grown stones, and the UGP is delayed again. With the 12.5% bond and a funding gap, a new share issue far below CAD 0.16 or a bond restructuring could hit shareholders.
