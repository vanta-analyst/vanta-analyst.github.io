    Bull case

    The updated PEA shows an NPV markedly above US$200m at gold around US$3,200+ (the resource has grown 22%, and the infrastructure exists), permits come in 2027, and a mid-sized producer or a stream partner finances construction. A re-rating from about 0.5 × to 1 × NAV plus a higher gold price could give 2–3x; 5x+ requires an acquisition or a very high gold price.

    Bear case

    The gold price falls back, the PEA disappoints on the sulphide portion or capex rises, and construction must be financed with an issue of US$100m+ at a low price (the share count could double). From P/B 3.2 – a record high – the price could easily halve back toward C$0.15–0.20.
