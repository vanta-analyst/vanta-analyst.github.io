    Bull case

    A licence deal for mesdopetam with, say, USD 20–40m upfront (SEK 200–400m) and milestones would on its own exceed the market value of about SEK 270m; add positive LIFT-PD data in 2027. The value could then reach SEK 0.8–1.2bn = SEK 5–8 per share (3–4x).

    Bear case

    No partner for mesdopetam after three years of searching, a new CEO has to find a way, and the cash is used up in 2027: a new share issue at about SEK 1 or lower with 50–70% dilution, and the price falls towards SEK 0.8–1.0. Negative LIFT-PD data remove the Otsuka track.
