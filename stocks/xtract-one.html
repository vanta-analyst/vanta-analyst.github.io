    Bull case

    The order book gets installed, revenue reaches approx. CAD 40m in FY2027 (analyst estimate 41m), and the company becomes durably EBITDA-positive with a rising subscription share. At EV/Sales 6–8 on CAD 40m the market value could reach CAD 250–330m – approx. 2–2.5x today's CAD 130m; a takeover by a larger security group could give more.

    Bear case

    Installations are postponed, revenue falls again as in FY2025, and competition from Evolv and metal detectors squeezes prices; an incident involving an undetected weapon type could hit the entire category. Then money will have to be raised again at C$0.30–0.40, but the net cash and the order book make a total loss unlikely in the short term.
