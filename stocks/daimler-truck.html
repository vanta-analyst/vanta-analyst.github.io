Cracks−9 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • US tariffs
    Tariffs cost about €221m of gross profit in Trucks North America in Q2. The 25% Section 232 tariff on trucks and parts applies, and production in Mexico is only protected as long as USMCA holds; from 1/7/2026 each country can withdraw with six months' notice.
    investing.com
    −3
  • North America and EPA27
    The order season and pre-buying ahead of the EPA 2027 rules ended in August (18,200 Class 8 orders), and orders cooled. The final NOx rules for 2027 are still not set, so part of 2026's strength may be pre-buying that will be missing in 2027.
    ccjdigital.com
    −3
  • Europe and buses
    Mercedes-Benz Trucks' return on sales is only 6.0% because of the start-up of the new parts centre in Halberstadt and higher development costs, and the bus sales forecast has been cut to 20,000–25,000 units because of Latin America and Mexico.
    investing.com
    −2
  • Share sale by Mercedes-Benz
    Mercedes-Benz has begun selling its stake (a first sale of €0.4bn in H1 2026). Further sales could weigh on the share price.
    investing.com
    −1

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Industry set to 'Machinery & automation' like Volvo (heavy trucks and buses). Cyclical = yes (truck cycle, freight and replacement). stockanalysis' net debt of €18.7bn and EV multiples include Daimler Truck Financial Services; the industrial business itself has net liquidity of €8.3bn. Stockanalysis' trailing P/E (30.5) does not match net income (€2.55bn TTM, about P/E 13) and is not used. TTM results and Q2 FCF are lifted by the ARCHION transaction (merger of Mitsubishi Fuso and Hino, €1.4bn cash effect), and Trucks Asia is deconsolidated, so the revenue decline is partly technical. China exposure is small (joint venture with Foton). Electric trucks (eActros/eCascadia) have not been examined and give 0 points. Q3 comes 6/11.
