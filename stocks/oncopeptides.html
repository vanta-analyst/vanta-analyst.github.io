    Bull case

    Third-line approval widens the market, and sales reach SEK 300–400m in 2028 with positive cash flow without a new share issue. At EV/sales of 4–6 that equals SEK 1.5–2.5bn, i.e. 2–3x from 0.86bn. A partner for the glioblastoma project or an ex-EU sale could give more, but it is not documented.

    Bear case

    Sales flatten at SEK 120–150m a year, cash runs out around mid-2027, and a new share issue at a deep discount (as in 2026 at SEK 1.55) dilutes 40–60%. The SEK 135m loan ranks ahead of shareholders. The share price could fall 50–80%.
