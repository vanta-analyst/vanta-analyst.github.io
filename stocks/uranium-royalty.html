    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company was transformed in July 2026: URC merged with Sweetwater Royalties (soda ash/trona royalties and 5.3m acres of land in Wyoming) under a new US parent, paid with about USD 813m in new shares at USD 3.64, about USD 330m in cash and assumption of Sweetwater's debt (USD 625m as of 1/4/2026). The share count rose from 146.6m to about 381m. Almost the entire physical uranium inventory was sold (inventory USD 136m in January vs 0.2m as of 31/7) to finance the purchase, so the exposure today is predominantly soda ash plus the old uranium royalties (McArthur River, Cigar Lake etc.). Industry is nevertheless set to 'Commodities & uranium' on the user's instruction; soda ash really fits better under chemicals/commodities. The TSX listing ceased 28/7/2026, so trading takes place on Nasdaq (UROY) in USD. The TTM figures (revenue USD 214m, P/E 11) are dominated by one-off uranium sales and say nothing about future earnings. P/NAV has not been published; I have used the trading values (EV about USD 1.9bn for Sweetwater), analysts' price target (USD 4.20 ≈ the price) and P/B as an approximation, so P/NAV is about 1.0x. The debt's interest rate and maturity as well as Sweetwater's revenue have not been found. The going concern disclosure and the revenue breakdown come from a secondary source (sci-tech-today), not directly from the 10-Q. Insider trades have not been examined.
