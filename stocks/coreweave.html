    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Industry set to the closest: Software, cloud & internet (AI cloud/GPU rental, in reality capital-intensive infrastructure). Listed only since March 2025, so own multiple history is one fiscal year. Stockanalysis' share growth of +76% is distorted by the IPO conversion; the real increase is c. 13% (487 → 552m shares). Total debt of USD 51.6bn includes lease liabilities; the company itself reported c. USD 35bn in loans at 30/6 before new convertible loans of 4.2bn in September. The Core Scientific acquisition was rejected by Core Scientific's shareholders in October 2025 and has not been resumed. Not flagged cyclical: revenue rests on multi-year take-or-pay contracts, but GPU prices and residual values are a real risk. Price USD 91.72, market cap 48.7bn, EV USD 94.8bn (7 Oct 2026).
