    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Penny deep dive (the price is above DKK 3). GoldMining is a project bank without production: gold-copper projects in Alaska (Whistler via the subsidiary U.S. GoldMining, about 74%), Brazil (São Jorge), Colombia (La Mina, Titiribi, Yarumalito), Peru, Canada and the USA, plus a large shareholding in Gold Royalty. Stockanalysis consolidates U.S. GoldMining, so cash and burn also include the subsidiary's figures. Price CAD 1.31 and market cap CAD 281.4m (214.8m shares) from stockanalysis 2/10-2026; Nordnet showed DKK 1,346m. Sum-of-parts is my own estimate: cash and short-term securities 81.6m + long-term investments (primarily Gold Royalty shares) 96.6m at 31/5-2026 + about 74% of U.S. GoldMining's market cap (about CAD 96m) − the subsidiary's own cash ≈ CAD 264m, i.e. about CAD 1.23 per share. The company itself reported about USD 185m in cash and listed securities at 24/6-2026. P/B makes limited sense, as exploration is expensed. Short interest and the largest individual owners have only been partially investigated.
