    Bull case

    The price war fades, the inventory of 101m kr is sold at book value, and the gross margin returns to 18–20% on 200m kr of revenue. That gives EBITDA of 10–15m and an EV of 150–200m; after debt the stock could double or triple from 70m kr.

    Bear case

    The price war continues, inventory must be written down by 20–30m kr, and the bank tightens credit. Then an issue at well below 2 kr with heavy dilution is required, and with 72m kr of net debt equity could become very small.
