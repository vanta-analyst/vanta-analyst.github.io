    Bull case

    Sales hold at 60m per quarter or more (240m+ a year) via Aira, Vattenfall and Elvy, EBITDA turns positive in H2 2026, and inventory is released so an issue is avoided. With 300–400m in revenue in 2027–28 and a 5–8% EBIT margin, EV/Sales of 1.5–2 can give a market cap of SEK 500–750m, i.e. 2–3x.

    Bear case

    Q2 was a one-off wave (inventory stocking at partners), sales fall back, and liquidity forces an issue at 1–1.5 kr with 50–100% dilution. A partner like Aira may run into trouble, and new inventory write-downs as in 2024 may come.
