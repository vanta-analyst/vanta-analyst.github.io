    Bull case

    NPCI or the RBI chooses Crunchfish's patented offline technology for the digital rupee or UPI, or GCash/Mercury signs a licensing agreement. Even a few SEK m in licence income and a strategic investor could lift the value from about SEK 180m to 500-900m (3-5x); a national Indian agreement could give more.

    Bear case

    No deal before March 2027, and a new issue at DKK 1.0–1.5 with 30–50% dilution becomes necessary. India chooses its own solution, and the company is left with patents without customers. The share price could halve again.
