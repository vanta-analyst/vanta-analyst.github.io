Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
The balance sheet is distorted by customer funds: 'trésorerie disponible' of EUR 10.1bn is matched against net customer prepayments of EUR 14.9bn (30/6-2026), so stockanalysis' net cash (EUR 9.9bn) and EV (EUR 12.5bn, EV/EBITDA 9) are not used – there is no real net cash. More than half of adjusted net profit (EUR 264m of 496m in H1) is the share of Thales' result (about 26.6% ownership, market value about EUR 12bn); the core is therefore traded at about EUR 10bn. Adjusted and reported earnings are not the same. The Dassault family (GIMD) owns about 2/3 and Airbus about 10%; float only about 17m shares and low liquidity – mentioned, not scored. India's 114-aircraft MRFA negotiation and Ukraine's 16 Rafale are not contracts yet and give no plus. H2 must deliver 16 Rafale vs 12 in H1.
