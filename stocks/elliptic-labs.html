    Bull case

    The laptop contracts exceed the minimum thresholds, another large laptop maker is joining, and smart glasses/wearables bring new licenses.     Deliveries remain within prepaid minimum contracts, H2 revenue disappoints again, and the cash is used up during 2027. A new issue at NOK 1.5-2.5 (record low was NOK 2.35 in August) could halve the price.
