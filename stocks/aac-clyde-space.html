    • Margin
      The gross margin is high (66% TTM), but the operating margin is −13.5% (EBIT −44m on 327m), and there has been a bottom-line loss every year since 2021 (−39 to −47m, except −6m in 2024). The company's EBITDA was 17.5m in 2025 and 8.0m in H1 2026.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 16% in 2025 (295m vs 353m) and swings greatly from quarter to quarter (143m in Q4 2024 vs 68m in Q1 2026). H1 2026 was +22%. Data & Services (Sedna, VIREON) has grown to 82m TTM from 30m in 2023, though, giving more recurring income.

      There have been many issues and a 1:50 reverse split in 2023. The share count is +15% in a year (Bonnier issues at 84.13 and earn-out shares to Spacemetric). ROIC is −3.6%, and equity is dominated by goodwill and intangibles (P/TBV 4.5 vs P/B 1.1).

      The combination of hardware (Starbuck power, Sirius data handling) and own data services in space (VDES/Sedna, VIREON) is more differentiated than pure cubesat production. The component market is nevertheless competitive, and the ESA programs are won in tenders.

    • Guidance credibility
      2025 guidance was cut from double-digit growth to about −10% because of delays in EPS-Sterna and SKAO, and revenue ended −16%. 2026 guidance (440–510m) requires 260–330m in H2 versus 180m in H1, i.e. a heavy back-end weighting.

    • Financing and prepayments
      Cash rose to 127m at 30/6 (27m in March), almost entirely because of prepayments (operating cash flow 107m in Q2). The money must be spent on deliveries. From Q3 2025 to Q1 2026 FCF was -33 to -39m per quarter, and guidance is merely 'positive operating cash flow' for the full year.
