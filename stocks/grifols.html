Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Scored on the A share (GRF). Stockanalysis multiplies the A share price by all 680.6 m shares incl. the non-voting B shares, which trade cheaper, so the market cap (5.8 bn EUR) is slightly overstated. Stockanalysis' FCF of 817 m EUR is before leases (118 m) and before minorities; the company's own FCF before M&A guidance is 500–575 m EUR for 2026 – this is used in T2. Stockanalysis shows +4.2% in share count, but the cash flow has no share issuance, so no deduction for dilution has been given. Leverage: the company reports 4.2x (adjusted EBITDA), stockanalysis 5.4x. Adjusted EBITDA margin (23.9%) is higher than reported; H1 includes 40 m EUR in restructuring costs. Not investigated: US pricing policy (MFN/tariffs) for plasma products. Price targets (median about 14.8 EUR) have not necessarily been updated after the 52-week fall of 21%.
