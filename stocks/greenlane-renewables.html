    Bull case

    Cascade LF becomes a success in landfill gas in Brazil and the USA, the order book grows to C$50m+, and revenue reaches C$60–70m with 40% gross margin and 8–10% EBITDA margin. At 0.8–1.0x sales that gives a market cap of C$60–80m – 2–2.5x today; a 3x+ requires an acquisition.

    Bear case

    The RNG market in the US remains weak, Cascade LF is delayed, and the loss continues at C$2–3m per quarter. In 1.5 years the cash is used up, and an issue at C$0.10 or lower dilutes heavily; the stock may fall 40–60%.
