    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −13 of −35
    • M&A and debt (Roku)
      Fox is buying Roku for about 22bn USD in EV, i.e. about 80% of its own market value: 96 USD in cash plus 0.9693 FOXA shares per Roku share. The cash portion is financed with a 12bn USD bridge facility; net leverage rises from about 0.9x to about 2.8x, and existing shareholders end up with 73%. The synergy target (400m) is low relative to the price, and the deal risk is large.
      au.variety.com
      −8
    • Lawsuits
      Smartmatic's USD 2.7bn defamation case is still pending with no trial date set; the Dominion case cost USD 787.5m in 2023.
      malaysia.news.yahoo.com
      −3
    • Governance (Murdoch control)
      The Murdoch family controls the company through the voting B shares, and FOXA has no voting rights. Lachlan Murdoch has won the control battle against his siblings and had his contract extended to 2030; minority shareholders have no influence over major decisions such as Roku.
      thehill.com
      −2
