Cracks

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings−16 of −35
  • Librela safety
    The FDA's 'Dear Veterinarian' letter (December 2024) on serious neurological side effects (seizures, paralysis and deaths) and the updated label (February 2025) have made vets cautious; Librela sales fell again in the US in Q2.
    ktmc.com
    −4
  • Customers are shifting
    Fewer vet visits and more price-sensitive pet owners are squeezing the US companion animal market; management itself talks about a 'more pressured Companion Animal market'.
    investor.zoetis.com
    −4
  • Guidance
    2026 guidance was cut sharply in August: revenue $9.12–9.32bn (from 9.68–9.96) and adjusted EPS $6.15–6.25 (from 6.85–7.00), after the stock had already fallen 21.5% on the disappointment on 6/5.
    investor.zoetis.com
    −4

    Securities lawsuit (City of Ann Arbor v. Zoetis, S.D.N.Y., class period 14/1/2025 to 6/5/2026) over misleading statements about Librela's safety, competition and market share. A consumer lawsuit over Librela, on the other hand, was dismissed by a judge.
