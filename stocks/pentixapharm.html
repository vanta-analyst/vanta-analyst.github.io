    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −24 of −30
    • Margin
      Almost no revenue (EUR 0.24m TTM, 0 in H1 2026). Operating result was -EUR 17.8m TTM and -19.9m in 2025, and the company guides a net loss of about EUR 21.6m in 2026 because the PANDA study increases burn in the second half.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Moat / segment of the future
      All the value rests on one diagnostic tracer, [68Ga]Ga-PentixaFor (CXCR4-PET), to distinguish unilateral from bilateral primary aldosteronism. The company itself points to market launch in 2030. Radiopharma is a hot theme, but diagnostics typically give lower prices and margins than therapy, and the PentixaTher therapy is only in phase I/II.
      edisongroup.com
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      The platform was reset in 2025: less advanced programmes were dropped, and the focus was narrowed to CXCR4. At the same time the share count was increased by 44% in July 2026 (11.02m shares at 1.85 EUR), only a year after the spin-off from Eckert & Ziegler.
      placera.se
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from EUR 1.76m (2023) to 1.43 (2024), 0.53 (2025) and 0 in H1 2026. There is no commercial income before an approval, which is expected around 2029-2030 at the earliest.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −21 of −35
    • Financial reporting and governance (BaFin)
      In a balance sheet review of the 2024 group accounts, BaFin concluded that intangible assets are overvalued by EUR 34.7m (capitalised development costs from the spin-off). BaFin also criticises the treatment of a patent/licence sale, the disclosure of the ultimate controlling party and transactions with related parties. The company disputes it.
      pr.washingtoncitypaper.com
      −7
    • Financing before data
      The company says the money lasts into 2028. Topline data from PANDA is not expected until H2 2028, so money will almost certainly have to be raised again before data. The convertible from Eckert & Ziegler (4%, 2024/2027, 4.0 of EUR 18.5m drawn) matures in 2027.
      boerse-express.com
      −7
    • Clinical risk
      PANDA (about 325 patients, expanded from 270) must show that the PET scan can replace adrenal vein catheterisation (AVS). An Australian pilot study (n=29) found high specificity, but only 50-64% sensitivity for detecting aldosterone-producing adenomas.
      jnm.snmjournals.org
      −5
    • Liquidity in the stock
      Average trading is about 10,000 shares a day (about 19,000 EUR), and free float is 18 m shares before the issue. That makes it hard to get in and out without moving the share price.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
