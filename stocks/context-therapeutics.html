    Bull case

    CTIM-76 maintains a response rate of about 30% in a larger cohort with dosing every third week, and CT-202 shows early activity; bispecific T-cell engagers in solid tumours are one of the hottest areas, and a licence deal with USD 50–100m upfront would be 2–4 times the market cap. A return to net cash alone (about USD 0.47 per share) gives +55%.

    Bear case

    The response does not hold in more patients, competitors with CLDN6 agents come first, and the company must raise USD 30–50m in 2027 at a market cap below 30m – a dilution of 100–200%. A reverse split and possible delisting to OTC press the price further. Downside 50–80% per share.
