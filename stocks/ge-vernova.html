    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only two to three years of own multiple history (spin-off from GE in April 2024). GAAP net income (USD 9.5bn TTM) is inflated by non-operating items and a tax income of USD 1.6bn - pre-tax income is 7.9bn against operating income of only 2.7bn (presumably a revaluation of the previously held stake in Prolec GE on acquisition) - so trailing P/E of 28.8 is misleading; forward P/E is used. FCF (12.4bn TTM) is lifted by customer prepayments and slot reservations. The share of orders from data centres is not disclosed. Insider trading has not been checked against Form 4 (only share-compensation-related trades for the CEO found). Q3 is due 28 Oct.
