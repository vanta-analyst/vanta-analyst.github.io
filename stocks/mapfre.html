    • Debt-financed acquisition (Safety)
      The purchase of Safety Insurance for USD 1.54bn (about EUR 1.35bn, 44% premium according to Insurance Journal) is financed with a bridge loan from Citi and Deutsche Bank, to be refinanced with about EUR 700m tier 2, 500m senior and bank debt; solvency falls about 10 percentage points. Closing only in Q1 2027, and integration takes place in the US, where Mapfre's current combined ratio is 94.6%.
