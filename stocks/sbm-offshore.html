    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Accounts in USD, share price in EUR. SBM manages on 'Directional' figures (leases treated as operating leases), which deviate sharply from IFRS: Directional EBITDA H1 USD 1.31bn vs IFRS USD 0.77bn. stockanalysis' FCF (USD 3.3bn TTM, FCF yield 48%) and net debt are distorted by the sale of FPSO ONE GUYANA and project financing, so P/FCF is not used. Forward P/E 8.1 is based on 2026 EPS, which is lifted by the sale; analysts expect 2027 EPS about 28% lower (only 5 estimates). Price targets latest 12/8. A large part of the debt is non-recourse project financing. Peer multiples (MODEC, Yinson, BW Offshore) not retrieved.
