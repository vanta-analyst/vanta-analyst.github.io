    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Adjusted vs reported: guidance and half-year figures are adjusted (adjusted net result €374m in H1), while stockanalysis' history is reported (€750m in 2025); the decline vs guidance may therefore be slightly exaggerated. Revenue swings with energy prices (€23bn in 2022, 12.9bn in 2024) and says little about growth. Cyclical set to false as for Enel: generation and trading are only part of earnings alongside grids, waste and district heating, but the merchant exposure is real. Price targets last updated 14 May (7 analysts) and are not a finding. The municipalities of Milan and Brescia are the controlling shareholders (free float 1.42 of 3.13bn shares); ownership share and shareholder agreement have not been checked, and no points are given for governance. If the government extends the hydropower concessions without tender, as the market is betting on, it is a possible positive catalyst, but it has not been decided. Credit rating has not been examined.
