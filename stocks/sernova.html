    Bull case

    The merger closes, BetaNova gets a Nasdaq listing, and SR-02 shows insulin production without donor cells in mid-2027 – combined with Cell Pouch's 8/12 insulin-independent. Stem cell islet cells are one of the hottest diabetes areas (Vertex has shown the way), so a combined company at 300–500 m USD is possible; Sernova shareholders' roughly 50% (before notes) could then be 3–8x today's roughly 29 m USD.

    Bear case

    The merger collapses (shareholders, the court or lack of financing), and Sernova is left with 63,000 C$ in cash and −11.7 m in working capital – that means an emergency issue at a few cents or insolvency. If the merger closes, Sernova shareholders are diluted by the notes and new issues before Nasdaq, and SR-02 could disappoint.
