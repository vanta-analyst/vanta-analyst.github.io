    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Price USD 8.25 (2/10-2026) after a 1:30 reverse split on 14/8-2026 (price USD 0.41 before the split, a record low). 515.8m shares before the split (10-Q, 5/8-2026) = 17.19m after the split, so the market cap is about USD 142m (about DKK 934m). Nordnet's DKK 2,449m is outdated. The net income of +USD 259m TTM is artificial: it stems from gains on the debt restructuring (TDR) and conversions of the 2030 notes (57.7m in Q2 alone); operations lose about 45% of revenue. Analyst price target USD 12.50 (3 analysts, consensus 'Sell'). No going concern wording in the 10-Q. The company cannot use its ATM program (no longer S-3 eligible). Nasdaq notice 4/3-2026 (deadline 31/8-2026); the reverse split was meant to resolve it, but regained compliance has not been confirmed in an announcement. Convertibles: 2027 notes (29.5m, maturity 15/3-2027) and 2030 notes (about 151m including PIK).
