    Bull case

    Leverage reaches 3.0x in 2028, servicing stabilizes, and interest expenses fall on refinancing. If net result returns to the 2021 level (3.1 bn SEK ≈ 1 SEK per share), a P/E of 9 gives a share price of about 9 SEK = 3x. Analysts' price targets are 4.70–6.40 SEK (+55–110%).

    Bear case

    Falling servicing income (Greece, Spain, UK) and high interest rates keep the result near zero (EPS 2026e SEK 0.17). If the market closes in 2027, a new restructuring or issue could again wipe out most of the shareholder value - it has happened once in two years.
