    • Margin and ROIC
      Operating margin 24.5% in FY26 after 1.8% in FY25 and 13.3% in FY24, but far below 57% in FY22. ROIC 7.9% – below a WACC of about 8% and vs 72.6% in FY22. Returns swing entirely with the NdPr price.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 76% to A$977.9m, but NdPr production only 11% to 7,260 t; the rest is price (average selling price A$80.7/kg vs 50.6). Production is well below the 10,500 t NdPr nameplate.
      investing.com
      −3
    • Cost position
      Sulphuric acid costs quadrupled, administration costs rose A$34m, and Kalgoorlie produced A$23m of under-absorbed start-up costs. Lynas is a price taker against state-backed Chinese producers with lower costs.
      investing.com
      −2

      The budget for the heavy rare earths (HRE) plant in Malaysia rose from A$180m to 294m (+63%) because of extra equipment, pricier sourcing outside China and geopolitical inflation; about A$1.5bn invested in expansions in total.
