    Bull case

    The sale of B2C to Canarbino triggers the full earn-out (EUR 27m + 16.5m), the B2B sale closes, and the creditors accept a restructuring agreement in which the old shareholders keep a listed shell with tax losses. That could give a short-lived speculative rise, but the figures do not support a 3–10x: proceeds of about EUR 75m vs liabilities of over 120m.

    Bear case

    Restructuring with debt-to-equity swap, composition or liquidation, where shareholders are wiped out or diluted 90–100%. The price has already fallen from EUR 0.66 (2023) to 0.078; zero is the most likely outcome.
