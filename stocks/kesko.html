    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Nordnet listing is the B share (KESKOB). Debt and EV include large lease liabilities (IFRS 16), so net debt/EBITDA looks higher than bank debt alone. The Dahl acquisition (EV EUR 1.52bn) has not yet closed (expected before year-end) and is not in the figures. Grocery, building materials/technical trade and car trade are reported together; the industry is set to food because grocery is the largest division (about 51%), but building trade becomes the largest division after Dahl.
