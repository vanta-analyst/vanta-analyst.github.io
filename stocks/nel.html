    Bull case

    The PA platform wins large orders (e.g. 2–3 projects of 100+ MW in 2027), Herøya fills up towards 500 MW–1 GW, and revenue rises from NOK 0.75 to 2–3bn with positive EBITDA. With NOK 1.1bn in net cash the share could then trade at 2–3x sales = NOK 5–8bn, i.e. 1.5–2x – a tripling also requires renewed hydrogen enthusiasm.

    Bear case

    No PA orders, EU subsidies fail to materialise, revenue falls towards NOK 600–700m, and a burn of NOK 150m per quarter forces a large share issue in 2027–2028 at a low price. The value falls towards net cash (NOK 0.6) or below.
