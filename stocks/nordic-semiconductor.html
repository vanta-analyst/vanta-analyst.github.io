Cracks

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings−8 of −35
  • Moat and competition
    Nordic's share of Bluetooth LE certifications fell below 30% in Q2 (31% before), and sales to the broad market are still almost 20% below the 2022 peak.
    finance.yahoo.com
    −3
  • Inventory and pull-forward
    Working capital is 26% of revenue (above the 25% target) after a deliberate inventory build, and management warns that customers pulling orders forward because of tight supply could hit later quarters. Operating cash flow was only USD 15m in Q2. In 2023 revenue fell 30% in a destocking.

    Industrial & Healthcare (40% of revenue) depends on a small number of large customers, which creates volatility; the consumer segment is 57%. The share per customer is not disclosed.

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Accounts in USD, price in NOK. Earnings are recovering from the 2023–24 trough (distributor destocking), so trailing P/E 116 and P/FCF 1,478 overstate the price; forward P/E 46.7 (NTM) and 67 on 2026E are the usable measures. TTM FCF is almost zero due to deliberate inventory build. Nordic is fabless and not on the list of cyclical industries, but revenue fell 30% in 2023 in an inventory cycle – hence cyclical: false, but see the model note. Peers (Silicon Labs, Espressif) and insider trades have not been examined in depth: an insider purchase on 30/4/2026 was found, but without name or amount, so no plus has been given.
