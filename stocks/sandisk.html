    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 2 findings    −11 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose from $7.4bn to $20.2bn in FY26, and gross margin was 84.6% in Q4 versus net losses in FY23-25. According to the company, about two thirds of Q4's 51% sequential growth came from higher prices. NAND is a commodity with five or six large suppliers (Samsung, SK hynix, Kioxia, Micron, YMTC), and peak prices are not a new normal.
      sec.gov
      −9
    • Delivery and capital tie-up
      Sandisk is wholly dependent on the joint ventures with Kioxia for its flash wafers and must fund 49.9-50% of the JVs' capex when their own cash flow falls short. In addition, Sandisk pays USD 1.165bn in 2026-2029 for the extension of the Yokkaichi agreement.
      autobound.ai
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −14 of −35
    • Price increases are fading
      Guidance for Q1 FY27 (10.3–10.8bn $) implies 18% sequential growth versus 51% in Q4, and the stock fell after the report on 'mixed' guidance. According to TrendForce, the increases in NAND contract prices are slowing from 70–75% to 10–15% in the quarter because PC and mobile customers are squeezed by record prices – and the Edge segment (PC/mobile) is 60% of revenue.
      seekingalpha.com
      −5
    • New capacity
      Kioxia and Sandisk announced in August investments of over 31bn $ in new Japanese NAND fabs through 2032 (Kitakami Fab3 from FY2029), and competitors are also increasing capex. That is the classic sign that supply is catching up with demand from 2027–28.
      blocksandfiles.com
      −4
    • Insider trading
      CEO David Goeckeler sold 33,838 shares on 14/9-2026 - his first sale ever and the company's largest insider sale - and approx. 53m $ on 17/9 via a 10b5-1 plan. Insider sales of over 100m $ in six months; no insider purchases found.
      finbold.com
      −3

      The share price has fallen from $1,745 at fiscal year-end to $1,582 during the September sell-off in memory stocks, and price targets range from $1,000 (-37%) to $3,600.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Listed 24/2-2025 via spin-off from Western Digital, so the only own multiple history is FY2025 (cycle trough); T2 is therefore assessed primarily against Micron and absolute level, as the addendum prescribes for newly listed companies. Forward P/E 7.4 is calculated on peak earnings (FY27 EPS approx. 215 $) - cycle trap; P/B and P/S are the better measures. The reason for the rise in share count (+6.9%) despite buybacks has not been clarified. Sandisk's share of JV capex in the Japanese programme of over 31bn $ is not disclosed. Customer concentration in the Data Center segment is not quantified. Fiscal year ends in early July.
