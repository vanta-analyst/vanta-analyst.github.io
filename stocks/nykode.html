    Bull case

    Positive interim data in Abili-T in 2027 trigger a licence agreement for abi-suva (the company estimates USD 2.3bn in sales in 2034) and a partner for VB10.NEO. Market cap could go from NOK 1.35bn to NOK 4–8bn – the 2022–23 level – i.e. 3–6x. Won tax case adds NOK 325m on top (about NOK 0.8 per share).

    Bear case

    Weak interim data in 2027: value falls towards net cash, which by then is about NOK 0.4bn (about NOK 1 per share, −70%). The tax case is finally lost, and phase 3 requires a new, expensive share issue.
