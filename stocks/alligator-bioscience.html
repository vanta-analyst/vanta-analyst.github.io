    Bull case

    Henlius gets positive phase 3 data and approval for HLX22 in China and globally; AbClon receives large milestones and royalties, and Alligator's 35% may be worth several hundred SEK million. At the same time mitazalimab is sold for a cash amount. From a diluted value of about SEK 130m this could give 3–5x, but it takes years.

    Bear case

    No buyer for mitazalimab, the cash runs out in 2027, and TO15/TO16 are exercised at SEK 0.01. The Fenja loan matures June 2027, and a new issue at an even lower price or a restructuring dilutes the current shareholders almost to zero. The HLX22 value may be sold cheaply to survive.
