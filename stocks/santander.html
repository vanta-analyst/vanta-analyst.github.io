    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 2 findings    −5 of −30
    • Capital allocation
      Two large acquisitions in mature markets in under a year: TSB (closed 30 April 2026) and Webster Bank for USD 12.2bn (14% premium, 2.05 Santander shares per Webster share on top of cash). Together they cost up to 210 bp of CET1, and the share component offsets part of the buybacks.

      −3
    • Earnings quality
      Cost of credit of 1.15% of loans and NPL of 2.93% are high for a large European bank; a large part of the high interest margin from consumer loans and Latin America is eaten by losses. RoTE of 15.6% is far from the target of over 20% in 2028.
