    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis has no Amsterdam page (quote/ams/UNA and ULVR give 404), so the path is the primary London listing (quote/lon/ULVR) with price in pence (GBX) instead of EUR; the accounts are in EUR. The share also trades in Amsterdam (Euronext), but the Nordnet lookup via the path will hit the London line. The fall in share count (−4.2%) is affected by the share consolidation (0.889:1) at the ice cream demerger in Dec. 2025; the buyback (EUR 1.5bn) corresponds to about 1.3% a year. Net profit for 2025 (9.5bn) is lifted by the gain on the ice cream demerger. Underlying EPS +2.4% vs reported −2.5% in H1. The Foods merger with McCormick (Reverse Morris Trust, USD 44.8bn) is expected to complete mid-2027; after that Unilever is a pure HPC company with about EUR 39bn in revenue. Jefferies (sell, 3,800p) cut its price target on 30/9.
