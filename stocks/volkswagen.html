Business−21 of −30

Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger?

  • Margin
    The operating margin was 3.8% in H1 2026 (4.3% before special items), and on 18/9 full-year guidance was cut to at most 1% (about 4% before special items) vs 8.3% in 2022. The gross margin has fallen from 17.7% to 13.8% TTM. CFO Arno Antlitz himself says the planned measures are not enough to lift the margin structurally.
    volkswagen-group.com
    −9
  • ROIC
    ROIC is 2.4% TTM and ROE 2.8%; returns have long been below the cost of capital, even before the financing arm pulls the figure down.
    stockanalysis.com
    −3
  • Quality of growth
    Revenue has been flat at around €322bn since 2023 and is now expected to fall to about €315bn in 2026. Deliveries fell 8.4% in H1 to 4.0m cars, and EPS is €10.46 TTM vs €31.81 in 2023.
    volkswagen-group.com
    −4
  • Future segment (EVs and software)
    Europe is going well (see Plus), but in China, where EVs and software decide sales, deliveries fell 37% in Q2 vs a market decline of about 20%. Production of the ID.4 in the US has been stopped (about €0.5bn in special items).
    finance.yahoo.com
    −2
  • Capital allocation
    Volkswagen is writing down goodwill on Porsche by about €6bn in Q3 (non-cash). Management acknowledges that the group must cut complexity sharply, including in its holdings, after years of overlapping brands, platforms and software investments.
    volkswagen-group.com
    −3

Cracks−13 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • China and profit warning
    On 18/9 Volkswagen cut its margin guidance from 4.0–5.5% to at most 1% because of about €10bn of special items, including about €2bn of restructuring and write-downs linked to the worsening Chinese market. Deliveries in China fell 26% in H1, and the preferred share fell 5.6% on the day.
    ms-aktuell.de
    −6
  • US tariffs
    Volkswagen expects a tariff burden of €4–5bn a year including volume effects, the largest among the German carmakers.
    finance.yahoo.com
    −4
  • Governance
    The preferred share has no voting rights. Porsche SE (the Porsche/Piëch families) holds the majority of the votes, and the state of Lower Saxony has a blocking minority; together with the strong influence of employees, that slows restructuring and plant closures. About 50,000 job cuts are planned.
    finance.yahoo.com
    −3

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The non-voting preference share (VOW3). stockanalysis' net debt of €214bn and EV of €264bn include the finance arm (Volkswagen Financial Services); the automotive business itself has net liquidity of €32.7bn (about 92% of the €35.6bn market value), so EV multiples and ROIC are distorted. The forward P/E rests on adjusted EPS (€17.42 for 2026e) excluding the roughly €10bn of special items VW now expects in 2026; reported earnings will be much lower. Volkswagen has special items almost every year. Cyclical = yes. Q3 comes 29/10.
