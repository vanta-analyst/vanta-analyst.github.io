    Bull case

    A strategic partner or customer secures financing, the annual report is published, and trading resumes. If the 'strong and growing' order book can be turned into revenue of CHF 60–80m with positive gross margin, a battery producer with European production could trade at EV/revenue of 2–3 – but with debt of about 90m and an EV already at about CHF 220m, that gives at most 1–1.5x for the stock, and only if dilution is kept down.

    Bear case

    No financing is available; SIX is delisting the share, and the company goes into restructuring or bankruptcy, where the debt (c. CHF 90m) and the main shareholder take the value. The current shareholders may end up with zero and with no opportunity to sell along the way.
