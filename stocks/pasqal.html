    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin
      H1 2026 revenue was EUR 4.9m, and the operating loss EUR 59.2m (incl. EUR 27.3m of share-based pay and EUR 10.2m of transaction costs). The operating margin is about -1,200%. The net loss was EUR 92.4m in 2025, and no gross margin is disclosed. The loss counts here and not again under ROIC.
      finviz.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Growth has stalled: H1 +14% (EUR 4.3m to 4.9m), and analysts expect only EUR 18.7m in 2026 vs EUR 16.4m in 2025. Revenue is lumpy sales of systems and QPU upgrades (e.g. EUR 0.8m from the Jülich contract) to state-backed research centres. The order book of EUR 70.4m also includes grants and tax credits.
      finviz.com
      −6
    • Technology and moat
      Neutral atoms: 1,024 defect-free atoms and a register of over 1,000 physical qubits, but Pasqal discloses no number of logical qubits and no timeline for fault tolerance. The delivered machines (Orion, 140–200 qubits) are mostly used in analogue mode. The competitors QuEra and Atom Computing (with Microsoft) are working with the same technology.
      finviz.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Share-based pay of EUR 27.3m in H1 is 5.6 times revenue (EUR 1.0m the year before). The SPAC structure was financed with expensive convertible bonds (see T3).
      rttnews.com
      −3
