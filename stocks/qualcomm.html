    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −10 of −30
    • Margin and growth
      TTM revenue of $44.1bn is the same as in FY22 ($44.2bn), while the operating margin has fallen from 33.5% to 23.7% and ROIC from 62% to 26%. Analysts expect a decline in revenue (-3%) and EPS (-13%) in FY26 and only about 2% annual EPS growth over three years: neither bigger nor stronger.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      Buybacks of $10.4bn and dividends of $3.8bn (TTM) exceed FCF of $10.4bn by about $3.8bn, and net debt has risen from $2.1bn (FY24) to $7.0bn. Stock-based compensation of $3.2bn (31% of FCF) eats a large part of the buyback.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Future segment
      The data center push (CPUs, AI accelerators) has no documented revenue yet; Meta is the first customer for the Dragonfly C1000 CPU. QCT gross margin has fallen below the historical range of 48-50% due to rising costs for wafers, packaging and memory.
      itiger.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −12 of −35
    • Loss of customer (Apple)
      Qualcomm's share of modems in the new iPhones is below 20% and falling faster than expected; revenue from Apple is expected to fall about 50% from the September to the December quarter, and FY27 Apple revenue will come in below the earlier estimate of just over $2bn.
      marketbeat.com
      −6
    • Weak guidance and handset decline
      Handset revenue fell 20% to $5.1bn in Q3 FY26 because high memory prices push consumers towards mid-range phones. Q4 guidance for adjusted EPS of $2.05-2.25 was about 9% below consensus of $2.36-2.38, and the stock fell 7%.
      itiger.com
      −4
    • Litigation (Arm)
      The jury in Delaware found on 13 Oct 2026 that Arm breached the licence agreement, but awarded only $2 in damages; the royalties question lies with the judge, and Arm's appeal of the first case (Nuvia) is still pending. The conflict with Qualcomm's most important IP supplier is not over.
      thestar.com.my
      −1

      Consensus is 'Hold' among 37 analysts, and several have price targets below the price (JLPascual $160, Bernstein $165 in Sep 2026).

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Q4 FY26 (the September quarter) reports on 4 Nov 2026 and is not included. FY25 net income ($5.5bn) is depressed by a one-off tax charge; forward P/E is therefore used in T2 instead of trailing. The QTL licensing business, the Chinese authorities' investigations (SAMR) and insider trading have not been examined; the score is an upper bound on those points. Management's target of $40bn non-handset revenue in FY29 is a target, not guidance, and earns no points. 13F purchases earn no points.
