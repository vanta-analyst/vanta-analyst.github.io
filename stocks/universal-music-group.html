    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −9 of −30
    • Margin
      Adjusted EBITDA margin fell 1.3 pp to 21.5% in Q2 (excl. Downtown) because of an unfavourable mix, higher legal costs and losses in merchandise (−10.7%). The gross margin has fallen from 45.8% (2021) to 41.7% TTM.
      investing.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Organic growth 6.4% in Q2, but subscription growth fell to 6.7% from 7.9% in Q1, and market share loss cost 1.5 pp – growth is driven by price increases (Streaming 2.0) and the Downtown acquisition (13.3% reported).
      marketscreener.com
      −3
    • ROIC and capital allocation
      ROIC fell from 21.5–31.5% (2021–25) to 16.2% TTM. Downtown (EUR 806m in investment outlays) contributed EUR 288m in revenue, but only EUR 13m adjusted EBITDA in H1 (about 4.5% margin).
      prnewswire.com

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −7 of −35
    • Debt and debt-funded payouts
      Financial net debt rose from EUR 2.39bn to 4.13bn in six months (Downtown, buybacks of 734m, dividends of 514m), while H1 FCF was only EUR 24m (−85%). Current ratio 0.61, quick ratio 0.41 and Altman Z 0.24; buybacks are partly funded by selling Spotify shares.
      prnewswire.com
      −4
    • AI and legal costs
      Generative AI music is a structural threat to catalogue values; UMG is betting on licensed AI deals (the Spotify collaboration), but needs a 'critical mass' of artists, and legal costs have risen and squeeze the margin.
      investing.com
      −2
    • Governance
      Insiders/major shareholders (the Bolloré sphere) own about 19%, and the free float is 1.26bn of 1.81bn shares; analysts deeply disagree (targets from EUR 12.10 to 39), and the share has fallen 41% in a year.
      digitalmusicnews.com
      −1

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Reported net profit fell 84.5% to EUR 222m in H1 due to fair value adjustments of listed equity holdings (incl. Price targets were cut after the 25% share price fall on 31/7 (e.g. Morgan Stanley 31→25, Goldman 19→15, HSBC 'reduce' 12.10), but several have not been updated – price targets are only included via the formulas.
