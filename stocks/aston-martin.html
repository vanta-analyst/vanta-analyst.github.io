    Bull case

    The gross margin reaches 'high 30s', adjusted EBIT heads towards zero, and free cash flow turns positive in 2027. At EV/Sales of 1.6 (historical level) on GBP 1.6bn in revenue and net debt of 1.5bn, the equity value becomes about GBP 1.1bn – about 3x the current market cap. An acquirer (Geely/PIF) could also be a catalyst.

    Bear case

    The Valhalla effect is fading, tariffs/China weigh on demand, and a cash burn of 300–400m a year continues against liquidity of 340m. Then the liquidity covenant is breached in 2027, and a new issue at e.g. 10–15 pence or a debt conversion could mean −60–90% for current shareholders.
