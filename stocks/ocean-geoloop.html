    Bull case

    COOL gets a pilot customer among data centres, the theme gives the stock a hype lift (daily turnover is already high), and Energi Teknikk delivers a small profit. With an EV of only about NOK 120–140m, a credible COOL contract could give a market cap of NOK 400–600m, i.e. 3–4x, if the financing happens at higher prices.

    Bear case

    COOL remains an idea with no customers, the cash runs out in H1 2027, and the conflict with the main shareholder makes it hard to raise money. Then comes another issue at NOK 0.2–0.5 with 50–100% dilution. Energi Teknikk could be squeezed by the resource rent tax and liquidity.
