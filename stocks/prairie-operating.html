    Bull case

    Production holds at 25–27,000 boe/d, oil stays above 60 USD, and H2 capex falls as guided, so FCF is used to reduce debt. At 3.5x EBITDA of 185m USD, EV becomes approx. 650m; minus approx. 480m in debt and preferred shares gives equity of approx. 170m USD – approx. 3–4 times today before dilution from warrants.

    Bear case

    A lower oil price or production decline triggers a lower borrowing base and covenant breach; with USD 21,000 in cash and a 125m working capital deficit it ends in an emergency issue at USD 0.2-0.3 or a restructuring in which shareholders are wiped out. Alternatively delisting to OTC.
