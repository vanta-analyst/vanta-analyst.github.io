Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Insurance: stockanalysis' FCF (−2.4bn) is not meaningful because it includes insurance and investment flows; NN's own free cash flow (holding level, H1 922m, target above 1.8bn in 2028) and payouts are measured against net profit. Stockanalysis' revenue forecast (−5%) and EPS history are affected by IFRS 17 and the sale/separation of NN Bank. Solvency II of 224% is lifted by NN Bank being taken out of the group solvency calculation. Interest-rate risk in the Dutch life book and the transition to the new pension system (Wtp) have not been examined separately. The unit-linked mis-selling case has been settled (about EUR 300m) and does not count. One price target (ING, EUR 74) is below the price. Next report 10 Feb 2027.
