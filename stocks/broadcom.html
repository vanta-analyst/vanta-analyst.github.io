Cracks−23 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • Customer concentration
    The AI business rests on six XPU customers led by Google, Anthropic and OpenAI. The customers are spreading their purchases: Google also works with MediaTek, and Anthropic is looking at chips from Samsung. The previously cited top-5 share of about 50% was not found again in the 10-Q.
    simplywall.st
    −8
  • Customer financing and guarantees
    Together with Apollo and Blackstone, Broadcom has set up a platform that finances Anthropic's and OpenAI's roll-out. Broadcom guarantees up to about $29bn of one customer's lease payments over five years: on default Broadcom is liable for 85% of the remaining debt minus what the racks can be sold for. BofA estimates the maximum residual-value exposure at about $370bn around 2030.
    fool.com
    −6
  • Customers are shifting
    Anthropic has pushed to slow the pace of the roll-out. Hock Tan said on 14/9 that the AI targets are unchanged.
    cnbc.com
    −2

    Debt of $61.1bn in principal and net debt of $35.4bn, but net debt/EBITDA has fallen from 2.3 (FY24) to 0.7, and $10.5bn was repaid in FY26. The old figure of $66bn is outdated.
