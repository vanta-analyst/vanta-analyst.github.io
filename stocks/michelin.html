Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Reported EPS is 2.25 € TTM (burdened by about 220 m € in one-off costs for the closure of Tuscaloosa), while the analysts' 2.74 € for 2026 is adjusted; forward P/E 11.3 is based on adjusted figures. Segment operating income (Michelin's key metric) is 1.45 bn € reported / 1.555 bn € before currency in H1. Cyclical set to no, because about three quarters of sales are replacement tyres, but the margin is noticeably affected by rubber and oil prices (raw materials contributed +199 m € in H1). Net debt (4.6 bn € in June) is seasonal and increased by three acquisitions. The company form (commandite par actions) gives the managing partners great control – no points given. Q3 revenue about 22/10; insider trading not investigated.
