    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −16 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      Germany accounted for EUR 44.0m of 56.3m in revenue (78%) in FY 2025/26, and the Bundeswehr is the dominant customer via framework agreements (e.g. SmG), which are called off in uneven lots. Delayed call-offs can shift revenue between years.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Inventory and working capital
      Inventory is EUR 18.8m (about 1/3 of annual revenue), and inventory turnover is only 1.6. Quick ratio is 0.94. The company tied up EUR 15m in inventory in FY 2023/24 ahead of large orders, and the risk of write-downs rises if the call-offs are delayed.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Liquidity and size
      The market cap is approx. EUR 78m, trading is thin (approx. DKK 176,000 a day on Nordnet and very few shares in Frankfurt), and the founding families Günther/Hemer own approx. 28%. Large orders or news can cause large price swings.
      ceotronics.com
      −3
    • Sector/political risk
      The price has fallen 29% over a year after the German defense euphoria faded. Earnings depend on German procurement processes and budgets, which have historically been slow and politically sensitive.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    CeoTronics is NOT in restructuring and is not a classic penny stock: the company makes money, pays a dividend and has a small net cash position. The normal calibration is used. stockanalysis only has a Frankfurt page (quote/fra/CEK); the Xetra path (quote/etr/CEK) gives 404. The financial year runs from June to May (FY 2025/26 ended 31/5-2026). Market cap is calculated at EUR 77.9m (7.98m shares × EUR 9.76, about DKK 581m) and roughly matches Nordnet's DKK 589m. The Bundeswehr's share of revenue is not stated directly; Germany accounted for 78% in FY 2025/26. The founding families' share (27.9%) is from an older analysis and not verified for 2026. Insider trades and short interest have not been examined. Quarterly burn and runway make no sense for a company with positive free cash flow (runway is set to 99 = not relevant).
