      Hydro's own adjusted RoaCE (12 months) is 10.9% – even with rising aluminium prices. Stockanalysis' ROIC of 22.6% overstates the return.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has been flat at around NOK 194–208bn since 2022. The Q2 lift comes from Aluminium Metal (EBITDA +165% on a higher aluminium price), while Bauxite & Alumina fell 66%, Energy 53% and Metal Markets 88%. That is price, not a stronger company.
      hydro.com

      Hydro is a price taker on LME aluminium but has a relative advantage: a cost position in the 16th percentile and low-carbon hydropower-based aluminium that earns product premiums.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −10 of −35
    • Temporary aluminium price
      The aluminium price has been lifted by supply disruptions in the Middle East (including EGA's Al Taweelah smelter). The price fell when the supply outlook improved, and the share price has fallen from NOK 120 to about 82. Analysts expect EPS −5% in 2027.
      alcircle.com
      −5
    • Alunorte and gas supply
      Alunorte, the world's largest alumina refinery outside China, had to cut to 50% of capacity in August because of interrupted gas supply from the New Fortress company CELBA. The Q3 impact is USD 75–100m, and there is dependence on one gas supplier.
      mining.com
      −3
    • Hydrology and energy
      Nordic reservoirs were at 61.9% vs a normal 67.9%. Energy EBITDA fell 53% because of lower production and losses on price-area differences, and the stronger NOK also weighed.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis shows an operating margin of 20% and ROIC of 22.6%, but these are distorted: profit before tax is only 7.4% of revenue, and Hydro's own adjusted RoaCE (12 months) is 10.9%. The company's figures are used. Forward P/E 8.6 rests on EPS of 8.70 in 2026 at a high aluminium price. The price has fallen from the 52-week high of 120.40 to about 82. Peers (Alcoa, Century, Rio Tinto Aluminium) and US aluminium tariffs (Section 232) have not been examined. The Investor Day on 25 Nov. 2026 may change the strategy.
