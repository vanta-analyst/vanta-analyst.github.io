    Bull case

    The retort in Brazil is running efficiently again, sales are reaching production (about 4,150 boe/d), and HCCO saves part of the roughly 15% of production that today goes to own consumption. With Canada (1,500 boe/d), EBITDA can reach C$70–90m. At EV/EBITDA of 4 and after the Brazil debt, that corresponds to equity of C$200–300m (1.5–2.2x). A 3–5x increase also requires a settlement or compensation in Quebec, but that value is now shared with the QGAS preference shares.

    Bear case

    Low oil price, customers who still fail to lift, and retort problems mean PX cannot refinance US$80m (plus PIK interest) in April 2028. Then the bondholders may take over PX, and Questerre is left with 1,500 boe/d in Canada, a blocked Quebec asset and a working capital deficit: -50-70%.
