    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −10 of −30
    • Margin and quality of earnings
      Operating margin 20.0% (TTM), but the net margin of 30.7% is due to a tax benefit of $43m and royalties. The operating margin was only 5.3–15.5% in 2021–2025, and according to an analyst the product gross margin fell a couple of hundred basis points in Q2 when royalties are stripped out.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue was flat at $359-408m in 2021-2025, and growth has only come now. Q2 revenue ($143.4m) included $15m from a single licence agreement, which falls to $5m in Q3; the year before included $45m from a patent settlement. Part of the 'growth' is thus lumpy licence payments.
      marketbeat.com
      −3
    • Free cash flow
      FCF fell 47% to $51m (TTM; FCF margin 10.8% versus 29.2% in 2025) because working capital and capex rose. Inventory rose 10% in Q2 to $104.5m with only 2.1 turns a year.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Dilution
      Share count has risen 2.4% over a year; shares were issued for 81.6m $ (options) against buybacks of only 17.6m $ (TTM), and stock-based compensation is 16.8m $ (3.5% of revenue).
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −13 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      Analysts asked on the Q2 call whether Cerebras was a customer above 10% and mentioned an estimate of 25-30% of revenue next year; management referred to the 10-Q and declined to comment on Google, AMD and Broadcom. Licensing revenue comes from a number of OEMs and one hyperscaler.
      marketbeat.com
      −4
    • Dependence on patent cases
      New licence agreements are not expected until the second ITC case receives its final ruling in 2027. Two-year licence agreements must be renegotiated when they expire, and Q2's operating costs rose due to results-dependent legal fees. Part of earnings therefore rests on litigation.
      marketbeat.com
      −3
    • Capacity and execution
      Fab-1 in Andover is approaching full utilisation. On 11/9 Vicor bought two plots in New Hampshire for Fab-2 and Fab-3 (almost 1 million square feet) with about a year's lead time to first operation. It is a large build-out for a company with $474m in revenue and risk of bottlenecks and delays.
      macaubusiness.com
      −2
    • Insider trading
      Founder and CEO Patrizio Vinciarelli has sold several times since December 2025, including 50,000 shares at about USD 167 in March 2026 and 20,000 shares in July, in total about USD 46.5m in insider sales. No insider purchases found; insiders still own 44%, however.
      marketbeat.com
      −2
    • Volatility and sentiment
      Beta 2.38, the share price is +417% over 52 weeks, and 7.95% of the free float is sold short. The free float is only 25.7m shares.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
