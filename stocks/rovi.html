Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The TTM result is lifted by a one-off income (badwill) of EUR 62.4m from the purchase of the BMS plant in Phoenix: reported H1 net profit +113%, but adjusted EBITDA −10% to EUR 58.8m. Trailing P/E 16.6 is therefore too low; without badwill it is about 25. Analysts expect EPS of EUR 2.47 in 2026 (−10%). TTM FCF (EUR 179m) is helped by inventory reduction (53m). Share purchases and sales (66m vs. 64m) are a liquidity agreement/treasury shares; the share count is unchanged, so no plus for buybacks. Price targets were cut in May (e.g. 83.5 → EUR 64.2), but several have not been updated since. Not investigated: the remaining volume and duration of the Moderna contract.
