    Bull case

    Vulvodynia data at the turn of the year shows clear pain relief. It is a condition with no approved treatment and many patients, and women's health attracts partners. A licence agreement with payment on signing funds the company, and the ED programme comes along as a bonus. Then the market cap could go from approx. SEK 140m to 0.5–1bn (3–7x).

    Bear case

    The vulvodynia study shows no effect with single doses. Then only the ED programme without a partner is left, cash runs out in 2027, and a MAC loan of up to approx. DKK 21m must be repaid. An emergency issue at DKK 0.5–1 could halve or cut the share price to a third.
