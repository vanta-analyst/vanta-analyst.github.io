Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The company steers by non-IFRS (operating margin guidance 32.2–32.6%, EPS 1.30–1.34 €), while the IFRS operating margin is 25% TTM and IFRS EPS 0.97 € (trailing P/E 21.7 vs forward 15.4); the difference is mainly amortisation of acquired intangibles and share-based pay. Net cash of 2.28 bn € at 30/6 falls to about 0.7 bn € when the ArisGlobal purchase (1.8 bn USD cash) closes in Q3/Q4. The family holding Groupe Industriel Marcel Dassault controls about 40% of capital and the majority of votes via double voting rights (float about 51%) – mentioned, not scored. Q3 results 28/10.
