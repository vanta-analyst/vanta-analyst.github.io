    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 2 findings    −11 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Growth is price-driven: gross margin went from 2.7% (FY23) and 39.8% (FY25) to 87.0% (non-GAAP) in Q4 FY26, and all four business units now have 83-90% gross margin. ROIC is 93% versus -7.4% to 19.4% in FY22-25. The new take-or-pay agreements with minimum prices dampen the cycle, but memory is still a commodity with three major suppliers, and peak prices are not a new normal.
      stockanalysis.com
      −8
    • Quality of earnings (capital intensity)
      Capex was 27.4bn $ in FY26 (8.4bn $ in FY24) and is expected at approx. 11.5bn $ in Q1 FY27, approx. 25bn $ in H1 and over 50bn $ for the whole of FY27. Adjusted FCF of 33.2bn $ in Q4 includes customer deposits (CFO expected approx. 10bn $ in Q4). Share count is +1.6% over a year, even though 0.65bn $ was bought back.
      biz.heraldcorp.com
      −3

    Price

    Today's multiples against the company's own history and its peers. · 1 finding    −16 of −35
    • Multiples vs own history
      Forward P/E 5.8 (FY27 EPS $176) and trailing P/E 13.8 look cheap, but memory stocks always trade at a low P/E on peak earnings (forward P/E 6.5 in mid-2022 just before the downturn). P/B is 8.4 versus 1.3-2.5 in FY22-25 and P/S 8.7 versus 2.1-4.9. EV/EBITDA is 10.1 (within 3.5-12.3 excluding the trough year FY23) and P/FCF 19.8 (about 23 excluding the deposit). The multiples have fallen after the results, but the price still assumes that peak prices hold for several years.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −13 of −35
    • Competition and supply
      Samsung and SK Hynix are both approved as HBM4 suppliers for Nvidia's Rubin. Micron had 21% of the HBM market versus SK Hynix's 58% in Q1 2026, and Samsung is expected to gain share in 2027. New capacity arrives in 2027 (Micron's own fabs in Singapore, Idaho and Taiwan), and all three are raising capex sharply. This is the classic sign that prices may turn from 2027-28.
      siliconanalysts.com
      −7
    • Insider trading
      CEO Sanjay Mehrotra sold 40,000 shares for approx. 37m $ in July and 40,000 shares for 38.8m $ on 21/8 via a 10b5-1 plan. The CFO and the chief people officer also sold in July. No insider purchases found, and insiders own only 0.21%.
      247wallst.com
      −2
    • Macro and sentiment
      The stock is +424% over 52 weeks, and beta is 2.23. Price targets range from 361 $ (-65%) to 3,000 $ (+192%). Despite a beat of approx. 2.7bn $ and strong guidance, the stock closed at 1,029 $ on 9/10 versus 1,065 $ before the results. This suggests the market is pricing in the peak. Gross margin in the Q1 guidance (86.25%) is slightly lower than in Q4 (87.0%).
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • China and regulation
      Micron acknowledges that Chinese producers (CXMT and others) are making progress, but most of their output is sold in China. Chinese DRAM capacity is a risk to commodity DRAM prices in the medium term, and US export rules are an uncertainty.
      trendforce.com

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-scored 11 Oct 2026 after the Q4 FY26 report (30 Sep). The memory cycle is at peak prices, and forward P/E of 5.8 is calculated on peak earnings (cycle trap); P/B is the better cycle measure. Q4 FCF is inflated by customer deposits (approx. 10bn $ expected in Q4, 18bn $ in total). Customer concentration (share from Nvidia/hyperscalers) is not quantified. The status of the Netlist patent case (445m $ judgment from 2024, immaterial in size) and export rules to China have only been examined superficially. No insider trades have been found since the report.
