    Bull case

    A sale of the company or a partner buying the commercial platform: EV is USD 165m (1.1x revenue). If a buyer pays 1.5-2x sales (EV 225-300m), equity becomes USD 120-195m = 2-3x the price. If generic CINVANTI is delayed, and ZYNRELEF/APONVIE keep growing 35-70%, the company can reach positive EBITDA and avoid dilution.

    Bear case

    Generic CINVANTI takes 40–60% of sales (about 35–50 m USD a year), covenants are breached, and the debt of 148 m – larger than the entire market cap – leads to a restructuring in which the creditors take the company. At the same time a Nasdaq delisting could send the stock to OTC. The stock could go toward zero.
