  • Acquisition of MPS
    Exchange offer for all of Monte dei Paschi at EUR 30.6bn (16 Intesa shares + EUR 1 per MPS share): about 4.9bn new shares (+28%) and about EUR 3bn in cash, about 30% of own market value. Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (stockanalysis' FCF swings between −40 and +102bn due to balance sheet movements); valuation on P/B, P/TBV and P/E, and the payout is held against net profit. The share count is −2.9% over a year, but +1.36% in the latest quarter (issuance of EUR 1.7bn TTM, probably an employee plan), so net buyback is smaller than the gross figure. The share price is affected by the ongoing exchange offer for MPS (16 Intesa shares + EUR 10 in cash per 10 MPS shares); the outcome depends on Antitrust, the ECB and Delfin (17.5% of MPS). CET1, bank tax/IRAP surcharge from the Italian budget law and exposure to Italian government bonds have not been investigated. Q3 results 30 Oct.
