    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −10 of −35
    • Regulation (China export controls)
      The US MATCH Act bill (April 2026) would ban both sales and servicing of DUV equipment to SMIC, Hua Hong, Huawei, CXMT and YMTC. Quilter Cheviot estimates it would hit 10–15% of ASML's sales; the stock fell on the news.
      cnbc.com
      −4
    • Customer concentration
      System sales in Q2 2026 went 43% to South Korea and 30% to Taiwan (up from 23% in Q1), i.e. effectively a few customers such as Samsung, SK Hynix and TSMC. China accounted for 14% of system sales and about 20% of total sales.
      trendforce.com
      −3
    • Macro and cycle
      Growth rests on AI-driven investment in memory and leading-edge logic; management mentions demand swings for 2027–2028 and ongoing capacity adjustment, while at the same time increasing EUV and DUV capacity by 30% to 2027. The share is +96% in a year, and the lowest price target is EUR 1,291 (−20%).

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Share price is +96% over 52 weeks (about EUR 1,620, market cap EUR 615bn). stockanalysis' consensus for 2026 (EUR 42.9bn) is below ASML's own guidance of EUR 43–45bn, so the estimates are partly outdated. FCF includes customer prepayments (unearned revenue EUR 19.4bn at end-2025); 'other operating assets' drew EUR 3.1bn out of cash flow TTM, and analysts expect only EUR 9.2bn FCF in 2026 vs EUR 14.8bn net income. The fall in share count (−1.4%) is consistent with buybacks of EUR 3.95bn TTM. Insider trading, the Mistral investment and order intake (no longer reported quarterly) are not examined.
