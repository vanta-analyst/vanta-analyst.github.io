    Penny deep dive. Maltese B2B platform provider for online casino and sports betting (spun out of Gaming Innovation Group in 2024), listed with SDRs in Stockholm. The accounts are in EUR; the penny amounts are converted to SEK at EUR/SEK 11.30 (7.4627/0.6604). Price SEK 2.27 (9/10-2026). Stockanalysis shows 156.13m shares, but after the directed issue in August (conditional on 888AFRICA, completed 1/10-2026) there are 176.9m shares, so market cap is about SEK 402m (DKK 265m) versus Nordnet's DKK 230m. Stockanalysis' FCF is before capitalized development (EUR 15.3m TTM); including it, the company burns about EUR 3.9m per quarter. Pro forma cash (about EUR 5.9m) is my estimate: 3.5m at 30/6 + 8.5m raised - 6.1m paid on the takeover, before 888AFRICA's own cash and Q3 burn. The remaining EUR 10.3m for 888AFRICA falls due over 10 months. Industry: 'Software, cloud & internet' is closest, but with 888AFRICA the company is now also a B2C gaming operator.
