    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Industrial: no category fits pool equipment (pumps, filters, robots, chemicals); nearest is "Machinery & automation" – demand also follows housing construction. Cyclical set to false because the aftermarket carries revenue, but the industry had a clear inventory cycle in 2023 (revenue −14%). Guidance and analysts' EPS are adjusted (excl. PPA amortisation from the Zodiac merger): adjusted EPS 2025 was about 1.30 vs reported 0.93, so forward P/E 12.7 is on adjusted EPS, while trailing P/E 20.7 is reported. Buybacks (about EUR 116m a year) only keep the share count flat (+0.02%) and therefore give no credit. Price targets last updated 31 July 2026. Insider trading and the ownership base (founding families/Rhône) have not been examined.
