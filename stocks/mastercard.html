    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −10 of −35
    • Regulation: Credit Card Competition Act
      Trump backed the CCCA on 13/1/2026, and Visa and Mastercard fell at least 4% on the day. The act requires at least two independent networks on credit cards from large banks. It failed in March, but the Senate majority leader has promised a vote in this session of Congress.
      paymentsdive.com
      −3
    • Fee settlement with merchants
      The $38bn settlement received preliminary approval on 9/6/2026. It cuts effective credit card interchange by 10 bp for five years and gives merchants the right to refuse expensive cards and add a surcharge. NRF and other trade groups will continue to challenge it.
      pymnts.com
      −2
    • Litigation in the UK
      In June 2025 the UK Competition Appeal Tribunal (CAT) ruled that Visa's and Mastercard's unregulated interchange fees are an infringement of Art. 101 by themselves. It was the first of at least three trials in the combined merchant claims; the damages phase is outstanding.
      crowdfundinsider.com
      −2
    • Cash flow
      FCF rose only 0.9% TTM to $16.7bn, while net income rose 22%. FCF margin fell from 52.3% (2025) to 47.6%, with −$2.6bn in other operating items. Cash flow is not following earnings.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

      CEO Michael Miebach sold 16,628 shares at $567.68 on 31/7 and 15,372 shares at $575 on 5/8/2026 (about $18m) under a 10b5-1 plan and owns 93,163 shares directly. There are no insider purchases.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
