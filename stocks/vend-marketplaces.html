    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −10 of −30
    • Moat (Blocket migration)
      Blocket's move to the FINN platform in November 2025 caused a sharp drop in traffic and private car listings (−35% right after the switch, still −9% in Q2) and lost customers to Tradera. In April 2026 the company had to lower its revenue outlook for Mobility, and the Mobility EBITDA margin fell from 57% to 53%.
      vend.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Group revenue is flat (NOK 1,696m in Q2, +2% at constant currency) and 6.34bn TTM vs 6.39bn in 2024. The four verticals are growing 10%, but largely through price increases (ARPA), while volume in Jobs and cars in Sweden is falling.
      vend.com
      −3
    • Margin
      An EBITDA margin of 40% in Q2 and an operating margin of 28% TTM are a clear improvement from 13–16% in 2023–2024, but still far below the best classifieds platforms (60–70%), and part of the lift comes from cost cuts (about 100 FTEs).
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

    • AI disruption
      Fears that AI assistants and search will bypass classifieds portals have pressed the stock (−38% over 52 weeks); concern about AI weighs on sentiment. Vend has set up an AI unit (April 2026), but the business model has not been tested against AI-based search.
      norwaystocks.substack.com
      −5
    • The Adevinta stake
      The illiquid 14% stake in PE-owned Adevinta (Aurelia) was written down by a net NOK 5.8bn in Q1 2026 to 7.2bn because of 25% lower peer multiples. The stake is about 17% of market value, with no control over exit or timing.
      vend.com
      −3

      Weaker volume in Jobs and a sluggish Swedish car market; Mobility Sweden is still challenged after the migration, while growth in Norway is carried by price increases.

      The Tinius Foundation via Blommenholm, which bought the media business from the company, remains the dominant shareholder; the A/B share structure and the sale to a related party give minority shareholders limited influence.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Formerly Schibsted ASA: the media were sold to the Tinius Trust via Blommenholm (agreed 2024, completed 2025), and the marketplace company (FINN, Blocket, DBA, Tori, Oikotie) was renamed Vend Marketplaces. Tickers now VEND (A) and VENDB (B); the stockanalysis path quote/osl/SCHB redirects to VEND. Historical multiples before 2025 are distorted by media and the Adevinta stake and are not used. The TTM net loss (−NOK 7.5bn) is due to a non-cash impairment of the Adevinta stake. The EV calculation deducts the Adevinta stake (NOK 7.2bn). Competition from Hjem.no (Norwegian property portal) has not been examined.
