  • Moat (Blocket migration)
    Blocket's move to the FINN platform in November 2025 caused a sharp drop in traffic and private car listings (−35% right after the switch, still −9% in Q2) and lost customers to Tradera. In April 2026 the company had to lower its revenue outlook for Mobility, and the Mobility EBITDA margin fell from 57% to 53%.

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Formerly Schibsted ASA: the media were sold to the Tinius Trust via Blommenholm (agreed 2024, completed 2025), and the marketplace company (FINN, Blocket, DBA, Tori, Oikotie) was renamed Vend Marketplaces. Tickers now VEND (A) and VENDB (B); the stockanalysis path quote/osl/SCHB redirects to VEND. Historical multiples before 2025 are distorted by media and the Adevinta stake and are not used. The TTM net loss (−NOK 7.5bn) is due to a non-cash impairment of the Adevinta stake. The EV calculation deducts the Adevinta stake (NOK 7.2bn). Competition from Hjem.no (Norwegian property portal) has not been examined.
