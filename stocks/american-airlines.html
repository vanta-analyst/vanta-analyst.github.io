    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Q3 2026 has NOT been reported yet (22 Oct); figures run to 30 Jun 2026, and the company itself has guided a Q3 loss (−USD 0.70 to −0.10 per share) on fuel at USD 3.75/gallon, while jet fuel was at about USD 4.34 in October. Negative equity (−USD 4.0bn) makes P/E and P/B meaningless; T2 is based on EV/EBITDA and P/FCF. The fall in the share count (−4.4%) is not due to buybacks and gives no plus. The S&P rating B+ comes from a secondary source. Industry: airline = 'Transport & logistics' (closest). Collective agreements have not been investigated. 13F purchases give no points.
