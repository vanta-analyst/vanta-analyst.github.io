Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Industry: the list has no chemicals category. 'Commodities & uranium' was chosen as the closest because the largest division (Basic Chemicals: soda ash from natural trona and peroxides) is a commodity-price business, and earnings follow the soda ash price; hence also cyclical. Rare earths (La Rochelle) are a small part of the group, so the share is only a very diluted exposure to the magnet theme. History before 2024 includes Syensqo (spun off Dec. 2023) and is only partly comparable; TTM net income (€4m) is distorted by one-offs and a 92% tax rate. Pension liabilities and credit rating have not been examined.
