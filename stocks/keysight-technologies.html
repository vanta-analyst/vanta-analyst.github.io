    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Fiscal year ends 31 Oct; Q4 FY26 reports on 23 Nov 2026 and is not included. Spirent (closed Oct 2025) and the acquired Synopsys/Ansys businesses mean that GAAP margins and ROIC from FY25 are depressed by amortisation of intangible assets; 'core' growth (+52% in orders) excludes acquisitions under one year. China's share of revenue and the specific effect of export controls were not found; the score is an upper bound on that point. The industry 'Tech hardware & optics' was chosen as the closest for test and measurement equipment. Insider sales (CEO, small 10b5-1 sales) are judged immaterial. 13F purchases earn no points.
