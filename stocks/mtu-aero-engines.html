    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −6 of −30
    • Margin and mix
      The adjusted EBIT margin fell from 15.9% to 14.8% in H1 2026 (adjusted EBIT +5% vs revenue +13%) because of start-up costs and mix. Growth comes from low-margin maintenance (MRO +21%), whose EBIT margin fell to 8.0% from 9.1% in Q2; MRO is now about 71% of revenue vs 64% in 2021.

    • Moat
      MTU is a junior partner in the engine programmes (including 18% of the GTF) and controls neither design nor quality; the powder-metal case at Pratt & Whitney shows that the partner's mistakes hit MTU in full. The GTF makes up about 46% of civil maintenance in H1.
      globalbankingandfinance.com
      −2

      ROIC fell from 22.6% (2025) to 17.3% TTM and ROCE from 16.3% to 15.0%; still clearly above WACC (8.5%), but the direction is down while capital tied up rises (working capital −€781m TTM, capex €448m).

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The dollar rate and hedging have not been examined (most revenue is in USD).
