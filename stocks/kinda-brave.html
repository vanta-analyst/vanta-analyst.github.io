    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −26 of −30
    • Margin
      Almost no revenue: SEK 0.45m in 2025 and SEK 0.07m in H1 2026. EBITDA was -SEK 19.7m in 2025 and -7.6m in H1 2026, and that is even flattered by capitalized development work of SEK 22.1m in 2025. Net result was -SEK 37.0m in 2025 incl. SEK 6.5m write-downs and 9.1m interest expenses.
      mb.cision.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth and concentration
      The whole company rests on one game, HELA: Of Mice & Magic. Revenue from earlier titles is almost gone (Q2: SEK 28k), and SEK 72.7m of the balance sheet is capitalised development of HELA. The company has four studios, but only Windup Games is developing something that could matter for revenue.
      mb.cision.com
      −6
    • Capital allocation and dilution
      The share count has risen from 35.6m (30/6-2025) to 56.4m (+58%) through directed issues, warrant exercise and settlement of about SEK 5.95m to the loan guarantors (including board member and CFO Hans Isoz) in shares. In addition there are 5.47m TO5 warrants at SEK 3.20 in June 2027.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Business model and moat
      Kinda Brave keeps only part of the HELA sales: Knights Peak (MY.GAMES) is the publisher and has prepaid the development, and Jinderman Publishing has given SEK 25m in royalty financing against a share of net revenue. Indie games are a hit-driven market without a moat.

    • Debt and financing
      The loans from Avida Finans (SEK 16.5m facility, about SEK 14.5m drawn, personally guaranteed by major shareholder Love Carlsson, Hans Isoz and Alexander Benitez) mature 31/12-2026, only a month after the launch. Financial liabilities were SEK 18.5m at 30/6 vs about SEK 17m in cash at 18/8, and burn before prepayments is about SEK 9m per quarter.

      −8
    • Launch risk
      HELA launches 1/12-2026 on all platforms and simultaneously on Xbox Game Pass from day one. That gives reach but cannibalises sales on Xbox, and the launch falls in the most crowded window of the year. Development has already slipped from 2025/H1 2026 to Q4 2026.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Accounting risk
      SEK 72.7m in capitalized development of HELA is on the balance sheet against equity of SEK 24m. If sales disappoint, an impairment could wipe out the equity. In 2025, SEK 6.5m was already written down.

      Top management invoices through their own companies (CEO SEK 1.01m in H1 2026), and insiders' loan guarantees have been paid with shares. The insider purchase in September (SEK 0.6m at SEK 2.49) was a purchase from CCO Alexander Benitez, i.e. insiders trading among themselves, not new money.
