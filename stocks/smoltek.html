    Bull case

    A Taiwanese component producer (e.g. in the Yageo circle) or an electrolyser producer signs a licence or development agreement, and the Heraeus test shows that the iridium amount can be reduced markedly. Then the market could price Smoltek as a platform of SEK 1–2bn (3–6x), and TO 9 is exercised at SEK 2.50, so financing is secured into 2028.

    Bear case

    No customer agreement before February 2027, TO 9 is not exercised, and a new issue at DKK 0.5–0.8 becomes necessary. At the same time the convertible loan can be converted at DKK 0.33 and sold in the market. With a 20-year history without a commercial breakthrough, the share price could fall 50–70%.
