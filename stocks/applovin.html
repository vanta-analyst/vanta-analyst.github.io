    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −16 of −35
    • Data practices, SEC and platform rules
      According to Bloomberg (Oct 2025), the SEC's Cyber and Emerging Technologies Unit is examining whether AppLovin breached Apple/Google partner agreements through fingerprinting to target ads; the probe stemmed from a whistleblower and the short reports, and no conclusion has been reported. The entire business runs in apps from the App Store and Google Play - in 2021 Apple rejected apps with Adjust's code for the same practice. A platform intervention would hit the core.
      natlawreview.com
      −7
    • Children and ad content
      On 5 Oct 2026 San Diego County sued AppLovin for targeting obscene ads at children and collecting data from minors; the complaint builds on the short reports from Fuzzy Panda, Culper Research and Muddy Waters.
      yahoo.com
      −3
    • Class action
      New securities lawsuit (class period 12 Feb-5 Aug 2026) over misleading statements about progress on the AI models and the video tool, after the stock fell about 20% on Q2; in addition, the older Quiero lawsuit (2023-25) over use of Meta data and inflated install numbers is pending.
      rgrdlaw.com
      −3
    • Insider trading
      Sales only: according to the lawsuit, CEO Adam Foroughi sold about $101.6m during the class period (including 90,704 shares at $460-466 in March and several sales at about $480-500 in June), director Eduardo Vivas about $74m and CTO Vasily Shikin about $30m in March - all close to the peak and before the miss.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock has fallen 56% over a year (277 $ on 9/10 versus a peak of 745.61 $) after the Q2 revenue miss and a new class action. The figures are clean continuing operations: the app/games business was sold to Tripledot in 2025 (407m $ in proceeds in the cash flow), so 2024-26 growth is platform growth. The SEC investigation into data practices (reported Oct. 2025) is neither confirmed nor closed, and the status of the state-level preliminary inquiries is unknown. The S&P 500 inclusion (Sept. 2025) and KKR's earlier sale have not been examined further. Q3 results 4/11-2026. Lead plaintiff deadline in the new lawsuit 16/11-2026.
