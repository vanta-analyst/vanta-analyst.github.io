Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Industry set to 'Construction & materials': heat pumps, water heaters and ventilation (Climate Solutions, about 2/3 of revenue) are sold for home renovation and new construction and follow building activity and subsidy schemes – like peers Carrier, Trane and Lennox, which are classified as building products – not the power grid. Element (heating elements, about 28%) could fit under 'Grid & electrical equipment'. stockanalysis lacks TTM EPS and share count; EPS figures are from the annual reports. Insiders (the Lindquist family and others) own about 19% and control the votes via A shares.
