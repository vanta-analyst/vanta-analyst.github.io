    Bull case

    Market cap of SEK 160m vs expected cash of SEK 225m at year-end. If retogatein is out-licensed for prevention (5-year data) with upfront and royalties, and the Umeå plant is sold, the value could reach 2–3x cash, i.e. SEK 450–700m = SEK 3–4.5 per share (3–4x). Alternatively, a capital return above the current price.

    Bear case

    The cash is spent on a reverse takeover of an unproven company with a large non-cash share issue, or burned through 2027 without a transaction. Then the cash discount disappears, and the price could halve to SEK 0.5 or lower with further dilution.
