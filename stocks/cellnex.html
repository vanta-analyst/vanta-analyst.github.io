    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Cellnex is managed on RLFCF (recurring FCF after interest and leases, before expansion capex): guidance EUR 1.90–2.00bn for 2026, i.e. about 8x market value. The full FCF after all capex is far lower: the company's guidance is EUR 600–700m for 2026, and stockanalysis shows EUR 807m TTM before leases and only EUR 181m after leases (EUR 625m) – the full FCF is used in the valuation. Net debt EUR 20.3bn including lease liabilities (stockanalysis). P/E cannot be used (loss); EPS forecasts are adjusted. Price targets (average EUR 35.11) are partly outdated after the fall to EUR 23.35; only Goldman (EUR 26) and UBS (EUR 29.50) updated in September. The reason for the price fall in September (about −10%) is not documented beyond Goldman's downgrade. The CNMC's review of the TDT market (broadcast) and insider trading have not been investigated. Industry: no telecom category exists; towers are placed under 'Software, cloud & internet' like the other telecom shares (Orange, Deutsche Telekom, Telenor).
