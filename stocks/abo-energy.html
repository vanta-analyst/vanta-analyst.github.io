The restructuring calibration has therefore been used. The stockanalysis figures stop at 30/6-2025 and are outdated; the 2025 annual report has not been published (delayed to Q3 2026). The 2026 half-year report (planned 1/9-2026) was not found. Market value is calculated at EUR 26.0m (9.22m shares × EUR 2.82, about DKK 194m) vs Nordnet's DKK 209m.
