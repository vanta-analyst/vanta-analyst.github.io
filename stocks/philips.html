Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
The forward P/E and analysts' EPS are adjusted (EUR 1.56 in 2025 vs reported 0.93). Q2 figures and the raised 2026 guidance (adjusted EBITA margin 13.5–14.0%, FCF EUR 1.5–1.7bn) are lifted by a one-off refund of US tariffs of EUR 186m (about 1 percentage point on the full-year margin); TTM FCF (1.99bn) and P/FCF of 10.7 are therefore flattered. The share count has risen 3.7% in a year because the dividend is largely paid in shares (only EUR 354m of cash dividends TTM) – checked against the cash flow. Exor owns about a sixth of the shares. Any remaining US investigations (DOJ) and lawsuits over Respironics, and insider trading, have not been examined in detail. Q3 report at the end of October.
