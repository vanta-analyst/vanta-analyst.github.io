    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Q3 2026 has been reported (9 Oct); TTM figures run to 30 Sep 2026. Revenue is inflated by refinery sales to third parties (+76%) and fuel-driven fares, so revenue growth of 14–16% is not real volume (capacity was flat). 2025 net income included a gain on investments of USD 1.2bn, so trailing P/E for 2025 (9.0) is understated. Industry: airline = 'Transport & logistics' (closest). The fuel price swings with the Iran conflict (since Feb 2026); Q4 guidance assumes about USD 4.25/gallon. Labour agreements and the terms of the Amex deal have not been investigated in detail. 13F purchases give no points.
