    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' FCF (EUR 2.14bn, yield 9.8%) does not deduct capitalised development (EUR 695m TTM under intangible assets) nor leasing (EUR 47m); real FCF is about EUR 1.40bn, or about 6.4% of market cap. Guidance and EPS growth are on adjusted EPS (excl. PPA amortisation); reported EPS of 3.03 rose only 2% TTM. The −4% share count matches buybacks of EUR 1.44bn in the cash flow. Deutsche Bank's upgrade (16 Sep) came after the price fall; several price targets from before the downgrade in July have not been updated. The AI threat is a market fear with no named competitor in the source. Insider trading not investigated.
