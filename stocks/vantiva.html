    Bull case

    Memory supply normalises, the DOCSIS 4.0 and Wi-Fi 7 replacement at US cable operators lifts Broadband, and EBITDA returns to EUR 120m (2025 level). With EV/EBITDA 5, EV becomes 600m, and after net debt of approx. 500m equity could be 100m – approx. 2.5-3x now. The high debt makes the share a leveraged option.

    Bear case

    Video disappears faster than Broadband grows, EBITDA stays in the 80–90m range, PIK interest increases the debt, and the convertible bond is issued at a low price. Then the equity is effectively worthless (EV of 5x 85m = 425m below net debt), and the share price could fall 50–90%.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Penny analysis (Under DKK 3 – Europe). French supplier of broadband gateways and TV boxes (formerly Technicolor) with about 59% of revenue in the US; bought CommScope's Home Networks in 2024. Has been through restructurings in 2020 (safeguard, 1:27 reverse split) and 2022–2023 (spin-off of Technicolor Creative Studios, debt restructuring with creditors as new owners). The debt was refinanced in H1 2026 with a four-year term (Barclays 295m, Angelo Gordon 154m incl. PIK, Wells Fargo USD 64m; weighted interest rate 7.6%). The general meeting on 23/9-2026 approved a move from Euronext Paris to Euronext Growth (earliest about 2 months later). Market cap calculated myself: 490.3m shares × EUR 0.0764 = EUR 37.5m (about DKK 280m; Nordnet shows 283). The company's FCF (+30m in H1 2026) differs from stockanalysis' TTM FCF (−13m); the company's figure is used. The precise stakes of the largest owners are not verified; insider trades not investigated. Customers are large cable and telecom operators, but the concentration is not quantified.
