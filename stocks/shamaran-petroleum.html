    Bull case

    The war subsides, Atrush restarts at over 40,000 bopd gross, and Sarsang is repaired. Analysts expect revenue could rise to about US$252m in 2027 (+134%). With an EBITDA margin of about 65% that gives about US$160m; at EV/EBITDA of 4 and net debt of about US$120m, equity is about US$520m vs a market cap of just over 400m (about 1.3x). A 3x also requires an oil price above US$90, payment of receivables and a dividend.

    Bear case

    The shutdown lasts into 2027, the export agreements expire without replacement, and KRG is withholding payments. The company is then running with negative cash flow and bond debt of US$143.8m, and the share could fall 50–70% as during the export stop in 2023.
