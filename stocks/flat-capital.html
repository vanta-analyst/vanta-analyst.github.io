    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company (2 employees) that is in practice a leveraged Klarna position: Klarna made up approx. 88% of net asset value at the half-year. 'Finance, data & insurance brokerage' was chosen as the closest industry. Stockanalysis' revenue/earnings are value changes and cannot be used for multiples, and there are no analysts (forecast 404). The net asset value is calculated by me: 25.34m Klarna shares × 14.06 USD (9 Oct 2026) × 9.969 SEK/USD = 3,552m SEK plus approx. 570m SEK in other unlisted holdings less net debt (H1 figure 665m minus the Klarna purchase of 95m in August) ≈ 4,120m SEK = approx. 8.27 SEK per share (498.08m shares incl. 240,457 new B shares from the option programme). The price of 7.965 therefore corresponds to a discount of only approx. 4% – P/B of 0.72 on stockanalysis is based on the outdated H1 value (11.14). The unlisted holdings have not been reviewed individually. The maturity of the credit facility (approx. 100m USD) and whether the Klarna shares are pledged were not found. Short register not checked. Market cap 498.08m × 7.965 = 3,967m SEK ≈ 2,620m DKK (Nordnet 2,604).
