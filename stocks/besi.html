    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Full name BE Semiconductor Industries. Stockanalysis shows −2.5% in share count in a year, but buybacks were only EUR 70m TTM (about 0.5% of shares), and the share count rose 1.9% in the latest quarter while debt fell from EUR 521m to 358m without a matching cash repayment (conversion of bonds). No plus has therefore been given for a falling share count. China's share (23–45% per quarter in 2025) is from a secondary source, and the largest customers' share has not been examined. The price includes takeover speculation (Applied Materials owns about 9%; rumours about Lam/Applied in March 2026). Morgan Stanley cut its target to EUR 220 in September. The new long-term targets (EUR 1.7–2.2bn of revenue, 45–55% operating margin) have no timeframe. Q3 report on 22/10.
