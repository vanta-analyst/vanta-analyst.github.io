  • CO2 regulation (pricing power)
    The investment case rests on the phase-out of free CO2 allowances and CBAM lifting cement prices. When Chancellor Merz said in February 2026 that the EU should reconsider or postpone the phase-out, the stock fell 11%; a fund manager warned that the sector loses pricing power if the policy is rolled back.

    RCO guidance for 2026 lowered to 3.40–3.65 bn € from up to 3.75 bn due to rising energy costs after the tensions in the Middle East; Goldman Sachs and Berenberg lowered their price targets to 210 and 215 €.

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
The share is −25% over 52 weeks and near the 52-week low (EUR 142.60) after a guidance cut in July and uncertainty about EU CO2 allowances; price targets (average EUR 210) have only been partly cut. Heidelberg reports semi-annually, so TTM figures are from 30/6-2026, and net debt is seasonally higher in mid-year. The major shareholder Spohn Cement (Merckle) has issued put options on 300,000 shares expiring in 2027; the significance is unclear, and no points have been given for it. Credit rating and litigation have not been investigated.
