    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    US ISR producer in Wyoming (Lost Creek plus the satellite Shirley Basin), listed on NYSE American (URG) and TSX (URE); both listings are available on Nordnet (search API 11/10-2026). The company is an 'exploration stage issuer' without proven reserves, so wellfield expenditure is expensed. That is why the GAAP gross margin is −164%, even though the cash-basis product profit is positive (cash cost USD 40.20/lb in Q2). The total size of the contract book and price mechanisms for 2026 were not found; the latest figures (5.7m lb, 2024–2030) are from 2024. Lost Creek's licensed capacity was not looked up. News from September 2026 was not found (Q3 results expected 2/11-2026). Insider trades were not investigated (insiders own 1%).
