    Bull case

    Morfiadakis is selling non-core assets, the Sveafastigheter merger lifts earnings 20%, and the 2027–2028 maturities are refinanced without an issue. If the discount to NAV (SEK 7.94) closes from 0.35x to 0.7–0.8x, that gives SEK 5.5–6.5 = 2–2.3x. A 3x also requires falling interest rates and rising property values, so that the leverage works in the right direction.

    Bear case

    The leverage works both ways: with EV 48.6 bn and market cap 5.5 bn SEK, a fall in property values of about 10% wipes out most of the equity ahead of the B share (after hybrids and preference shares). If the bond market closes in 2027, a debt conversion or directed issue could dilute B shareholders by 50–90%.
