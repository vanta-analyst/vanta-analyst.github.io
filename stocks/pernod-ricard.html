    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The fiscal year ends 30/6; FY26 = July 2025–June 2026. Reported EPS of 4.77 EUR is below adjusted EPS (about 5.85), and forward P/E 10.3 is based on adjusted figures; the reported decline (−14.2%) is stronger than the organic one (−3.9%) due to currency and the sale of Imperial Blue. The company's own net debt/EBITDA is 3.7x, stockanalysis shows 4.1x. The Ricard family (about 14% of shares, more votes via double voting rights) and Alexandre Ricard as CEO are mentioned without points; the family took the final dividend in shares. Credit rating (S&P/Moody's) and insider trading have not been investigated. Several price targets (e.g. Bernstein 110 EUR) are far above the share price and are not a finding.
