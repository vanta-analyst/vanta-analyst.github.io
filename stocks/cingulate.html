    • Quality of growth
      All the value depends on one product, CTx-1301 (dexmethylphenidate in a three-part tablet), which is a new formulation of an old, generic ADHD drug. CTx-1302 awaits capital, and CTx-2103 (anxiety) is at the formulation stage.
      sec.gov
      −6
    • Moat
      The ADHD market is full of cheap generics (dexmethylphenidate ER, lisdexamfetamine) and branded drugs such as Azstarys, Jornay PM and Qelbree. Insurers typically restrict expensive new formulations of generic substances. A new patent (US 12,653,791, until December 2042) protects the formulation, but not the substance itself.
      sec.gov
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The price has fallen from USD 662 (adjusted for reverse splits, 2021) to 4.76. Financing has come via expensive instruments: the Lincoln Park equity line, an Avondale debt note with 9% interest and a monitoring fee paid in shares, and repeated ATM programs.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
