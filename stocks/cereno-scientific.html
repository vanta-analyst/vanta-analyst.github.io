    Bull case

    A large PAH partner (the sector paid USD 11.5bn for Acceleron/sotatercept) licenses CS1 with an upfront that removes the funding need, or the 2028 phase 2b data is clearly positive. The value could then reach SEK 3–5bn = about SEK 10–15 per share (2.5–4x), in line with Stifel's SEK 20 target in the best case.

    Bear case

    No partner before 2027: new share issues at a low price to fund phase 2b (several hundred million SEK) while handling the 175m convertible. At a price of SEK 2 and 50% dilution the share halves again; a negative phase 2b in 2028 could send it towards SEK 0.5.
