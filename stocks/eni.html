    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Forward P/E and analysts' EPS (€2.72 for 2026, +73%) are adjusted figures; reported EPS TTM is €1.66. Earnings for 2026 are lifted by high oil and gas prices after the closure of the Strait of Hormuz; Eni's own guidance is based on Brent at USD 85. Analysts expect FCF of €8.7bn in 2026 (about 12% of the market cap), which contrasts with FCF TTM of €3.65bn – the FCF yield is therefore not given a deduction. Dividend plus buybacks (€5.6bn TTM) exceed FCF TTM, so the buyback does not earn a plus despite a fall in the share count of about 18% since 2021; the payout is partly funded by selling minority stakes in Plenitude and Enilive (minority interests up from 0.5 to 4.9bn). The Italian state is the largest shareholder via CDP and MEF. Credit rating, Venezuela receivables and the Italian special tax have not been investigated.
