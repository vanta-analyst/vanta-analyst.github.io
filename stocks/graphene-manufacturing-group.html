    Penny deep dive (the price is above DKK 3). Australian company (Brisbane) listed on TSX Venture that makes graphene with its own plasma process and sells THERMAL-XR coating for HVAC/cooling, G-lubricant and develops graphene-aluminium-ion batteries (G+AI) with the University of Queensland and Rio Tinto. No industry on the list fits; 'Commodities & uranium' is chosen as the closest (materials producer), and cyclical = no, because earnings do not follow a commodity price. Fiscal year July–June; the annual report for FY2026 (as of 30/6-2026) is not yet published, so the latest quarterly figures are as of 31/3-2026. Stockanalysis' figures are converted to CAD from Australian dollars and are mutually inconsistent (the statistics page shows cash 9.8m, the quarterly page net cash 14.0m); cash is an estimate. Share count after the placement on 28/9-2026: about 140.2m (133.77 + 6.47). Price CAD 2.31 from stockanalysis 2/10-2026. The large net income swing (−21m in the Dec quarter) is probably due to revaluation of warrants, not operations. Short data and insider trades have not been investigated.
