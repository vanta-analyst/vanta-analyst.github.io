    Bull case

    The compressor and workovers lift Cascadura, the WD-4 and Baraka wells add oil, and production reaches 5,500-6,000 boe/d. With a netback of around US$25/boe that gives EBITDA of about C$45-55m; at EV/EBITDA of 4 and falling debt, the equity could be worth C$100-130m beyond the debt - market cap C$150-250m, i.e. 2-3x. More requires higher gas prices in Trinidad.

    Bear case

    Cascadura pressure falls faster than expected, the banks refuse a waiver, and the company must raise money again at C$0.08–0.12 with heavy dilution. With net debt above the market cap and a going concern note, shareholders could in the worst case be left with very little (−60–90%).
