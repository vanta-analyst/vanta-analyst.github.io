    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Listed 4/6 2026 (IPO at $60), so there is no own multiple history: T2 is based on peers (IonQ, D-Wave, Rigetti, IQM, Infleqtion) and absolute level. Up-C structure: 36m A shares + 227m B shares/units; the market cap ($10.9bn) is calculated on all 263m shares, but only about 32m are free float. GAAP figures are distorted by IPO-related share-based pay ($447.5m in H1) and warrant revaluation; adjusted EBITDA −$68m in Q2. Honeywell's exact ownership share (about 48–49% of the votes after the IPO; economic share higher) and the lock-up date in December have not been checked in the prospectus. Quantinuum is regarded as the leader on fidelity (ion traps, Helios), which is why there is no deduction for technology. Customer concentration is not disclosed.
