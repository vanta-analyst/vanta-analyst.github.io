Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Silicones was sold to Bluestar as of 30 April 2026 in exchange for redemption of Bluestar's 52.9%. Historical figures are partly restated (2023+ without Silicones), so TTM mixes before and after. stockanalysis' EV/EBITDA of 5.5 rests on TTM EBITDA of 3.3bn, which does not fit the new Elkem; annualised Q2 EBITDA (523m) gives about 8.9x. The forward P/E of 14 is apparently for 2027; on 2026 EPS (1.06) the P/E is about 31. Industry: 'Commodities & uranium' was chosen as the closest for silicon/ferrosilicon and carbon materials. Norwegian power prices and the value of the retained minority stake in Silicones have not been examined.
