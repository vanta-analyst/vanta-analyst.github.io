    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −23 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin 3.9% and net margin −5.5% (TTM) vs 26% and 21% in 2022. The EBITDA margin in Q2 2026 was 14% (17% a year earlier), and Silicon Products, the core of the new Elkem, earned only 11%.
      investing.com
      −7
    • ROIC
      ROIC 1.7% (TTM), 2.9% in 2025 and 4.1% in 2024 vs 32% in 2022. Returns have been far below the cost of capital for three years.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue from continuing operations fell from NOK 21.1bn (2023) to 16.1bn (TTM), and analysts expect −6% in 2026. Sales volume in Silicon Products rose 9% in Q2 but was eaten by lower prices.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Future segment
      Elkem has sold most of the Silicones division, the most value-added part of the chain, and is left with silicon, ferroalloys and carbon. These are commodity-like products in direct competition with Chinese overcapacity.
      elkem.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Negative FCF in 2023–2025 (−2.2, −1.4 and −1.2bn NOK) after large investments, which ended in an emergency capital raise of NOK 1.8bn in 2026 at a low share price. The share count rose about 20% (301m to 363m).
      stockanalysis.com
      −4

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −11 of −35
    • Debt and covenants
      Leverage was 5.0x net interest-bearing debt/EBITDA in Q1 vs a covenant of at most 4.75x, which forced the capital raise. It is now 3.2x with interest cover of only 1.4x, and the covenant limit tightens to 4.25x from Q2 2027.
      investing.com
      −5
    • Chinese overcapacity and prices
      Low silicon and ferrosilicon prices pulled Q2 EBITDA down to NOK 523m (645m a year earlier). According to Elkem, the EU safeguard on ferroalloys from November 2025 has had a limited effect on prices.
      inderes.fi
      −4
    • Customer industries
      Carbon Solutions volume fell 4% and revenue 13% in Q2 because of closed capacity at customers (smelters), i.e. weak demand downstream.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Silicones was sold to Bluestar as of 30 April 2026 in exchange for redemption of Bluestar's 52.9%. Historical figures are partly restated (2023+ without Silicones), so TTM mixes before and after. stockanalysis' EV/EBITDA of 5.5 rests on TTM EBITDA of 3.3bn, which does not fit the new Elkem; annualised Q2 EBITDA (523m) gives about 8.9x. The forward P/E of 14 is apparently for 2027; on 2026 EPS (1.06) the P/E is about 31. Industry: 'Commodities & uranium' was chosen as the closest for silicon/ferrosilicon and carbon materials. Norwegian power prices and the value of the retained minority stake in Silicones have not been examined.
