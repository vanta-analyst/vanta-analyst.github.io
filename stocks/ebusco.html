    Bull case

    The EUR 30m LC facility closes, Ebusco 3.0 from Golden Dragon is delivered on time, and the UAE opportunity (230 buses) and the tender pipeline of 173 buses give new orders. With a gross margin of about 28% and revenue of EUR 100-150m, EBITDA could turn positive; at EV/revenue 0.8, EV becomes 80-120m, minus about 30m net debt = EUR 50-90m equity, i.e. 1-2x today - and only 3x+ if a strategic buyer pays a premium for the whole company.

    Bear case

    The LC facility or the transaction fails to materialize, cash (about EUR 8m) runs out around Q1 2027, and the company has to do a deeply discounted issue or a WHOA restructuring in which the debt (38.7m) is converted and the old shareholders are wiped out. A distressed sale of the bus division could leave an empty shell.
