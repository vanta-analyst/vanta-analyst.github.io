      Revenue has fallen from SEK 2,162m (2022) to 1,504m TTM due to divestments and weak demand. Organic growth was 9.2% in H1 2026, but reported growth was only 1.1%, and the project-based business continues to have problems.

      Acquisitions for over SEK 3.7bn in cash in 2021–22 have been followed by write-downs of 749m (2023) and SEK 1,043m (2025) as well as divestments. Accumulated deficit is −SEK 6.1bn vs paid-in capital of 7.6bn. A great deal of capital has been destroyed.
