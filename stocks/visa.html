    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −5 of −30
    • Future segment
      Account-to-account transfers and banks' own solutions are gaining ground: EPI's Wero is live in e-commerce in Germany and Belgium (March 2026), and France, Luxembourg and the Netherlands follow in 2026, with a merchant fee of about 0.7%. Visa is still growing (payment volume +10%, value-added services +34% in Q3), but in the longer term account-to-account transfers pressure the card networks in Europe.

      GAAP operating expenses rose 19% in Q3 FY2026 vs 14% revenue growth, mainly personnel costs. On top came a round of layoffs (mostly tech roles) with a one-off charge of $563m. Operating margin (66.9%) is flat, not rising.
      tikr.com
      −2

      Buybacks (USD 21.7bn) and dividend (USD 5.0bn) totalling USD 26.7bn TTM exceed FCF of USD 21.0bn. Net debt has risen from USD 1.2bn (FY2023) to USD 10.1bn. The share count is falling 2.9% a year, but that is partly debt-financed.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −12 of −35
    • Litigation: DOJ monopoly case (debit)
      The Department of Justice's case on an illegal debit card monopoly (Sherman Act §1 and §2) was not dismissed in 2025. Discovery has been extended to 29/1/2027, and a trial is expected in 2028 with no settlement. Debit is a large part of Visa's US volume, and a settlement could force discounts and exclusivity agreements away.
      paymentsdive.com
      −4
    • Regulation: Credit Card Competition Act
      Trump backed the CCCA on 13/1/2026, and Visa and Mastercard fell at least 4% on the day. The act requires at least two independent networks on credit cards from banks over USD 100bn. The attempt to attach it to a housing bill failed in March, but the Senate majority leader has promised a vote in this session of Congress.
      paymentsdive.com
      −3
    • Fee settlement with merchants
      The revised $38bn settlement received preliminary approval on 9/6/2026. It cuts effective credit card interchange by 10 bp for five years, lets merchants refuse expensive cards and add a surcharge, and caps standard cards at 1.25% for eight years. NRF and other trade groups oppose it, and Visa still makes provisions for the MDL case ($237m in Q3).
      pymnts.com
      −2
    • Cash flow
      FCF fell 4.8% TTM to $21.0bn, while net income rose about 13%. FCF margin has gone from 60% (FY2023) to 47% (TTM), driven by larger customer incentives and other operating items (−$18.3bn TTM). Earnings and cash flow are moving in different directions.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Insider selling
      CEO Ryan McInerney sells continuously under a 10b5-1 plan: 31,455 shares in April, about 31,000 in June–July and 5,875 shares monthly in August–October 2026. He owns only 15,174 shares directly (265,168 via a family trust). There are no insider purchases.
      sec.gov
      −1
