    Price USD 2.28 (2/10-2026); above DKK 3 is fine according to the brief. 75.99m shares (20/7-2026) give a market cap of about USD 173m (about DKK 1,141m), in line with Nordnet's DKK 1,161m. Nordnet calls the stock 'Comstock Mining', but the mining operations were sold to Mackay Gold & Silver on 24/8-2026 (USD 20m cash at closing, 2m Mackay shares, 7m within 18 months, 10m contingent and a 1.5% NSR royalty). The company is now a solar panel recycler (Comstock Metals) plus Bioleum (biofuel) and real estate/water rights via 47.6% of Sierra Springs Opportunity Fund. Cash of 31.4m at 30/6 plus 20m from the mine sale = about 51m pro forma; no interest-bearing debt according to the 10-Q (stockanalysis' 19.4m of debt is leases and obligations). No going concern warning. ATM with Titan Partners (94.2m remaining capacity). No analyst coverage (the forecast page returns 404). Industry: solar panel recycling and metal recovery do not fit exactly; the closest is 'Commodities & uranium'. Cyclical = false, because revenue is mainly recycling fees and the mining operations have been sold.
