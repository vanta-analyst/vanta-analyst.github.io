    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Cyclical = yes (tyres/car parts, consistent with Michelin, Renault and Stellantis), even though about 82% of sales are High Value tyres, mainly for the replacement market. Adjusted EBIT margin 16% (company metric) vs reported operating margin 13.1% TTM; analysts' EPS is adjusted. Stockanalysis shows a share count increase of 4.6% over a year, but there is no share issuance in the cash flow – probably conversion of a convertible bond; no points given. The share price (EUR 6.59) is close to the EUR 6.50 that Lumina Crown (Michal Strnad/CSG) paid for 14% from Sinochem in July. Price targets were last updated 18/8. Insider trading and Russia exposure not investigated in detail.
