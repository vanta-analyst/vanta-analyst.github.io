Cracks−18 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • Salmon price and macro
    The export price for fresh salmon was NOK 71.4/kg in week 38 of 2026 and reached NOK 48–50/kg in summer 2025, the lowest in four years. Higher supply and a strong NOK could squeeze price achievement again.
    ssb.no
    −4
  • Resource rent tax and regulation
    Almost the whole harvest is Norwegian and pays resource rent tax (effectively 25%) on top of 22% corporate tax; only the five largest farmers are above the NOK 70m basic deduction. The Icelandic subsidiary pays a farming levy 3–4 times the Norwegian one and faces an unresolved new aquaculture law.
    sticos.no
    −5
  • Debt
    Net interest-bearing debt NOK 19.1bn (NIBD/EBITDA 2.6x according to the company); total debt has risen from NOK 6.4bn (2021) to 21.9bn, and interest cover is only 3.4x. Pre-tax profit was NOK 565m in Q2 vs operational EBIT of 1.24bn.
    stockanalysis.com
    −4
  • Competition law (EU cartel case)
    SalMar is among the six Norwegian farmers that the European Commission accused in January 2024 of illegally exchanging price and volume data in 2011–2019; a fine of up to 10% of revenue is possible.
    salmonbusiness.com
    −3
  • Biology
    Biological problems left Central Norway with operating profit of only NOK 7m in Q2 2025; results can swing sharply from year to year with the biology.
    investing.com
    −2

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The Witzøe family controls the company via Kverva, although stockanalysis only shows 2% insider ownership. The EU cartel case is undecided according to our research. The reason for the rise in share count (+2.5%) has not been examined.
