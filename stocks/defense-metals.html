    Bull case

    The West keeps paying USD 110–130/kg for non-Chinese NdPr, NRCan grants up to CAD 32m, and a strategic partner (carmaker, magnet maker or defence) takes a stake in Wicheeda. If the market prices the project at just 0.15–0.2x after-tax NPV (CAD 150–200m) vs approx. CAD 51m today, that is 3–4x – more if the state takes part of the capex.

    Bear case

    NdPr prices fall back towards USD 60–80/kg, the DFS drags on, and the company must raise C$10–20m a year at C$0.08–0.12 (+30–50% shares annually). With capex of C$1.4bn and no partner the share price could halve again.
