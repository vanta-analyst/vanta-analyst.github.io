Business−12 of −30

Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger?

  • Margin
    The operating margin is 10.4% TTM vs 14.6% in 2023 and 13.0% in 2024, and the gross margin has fallen to 24.4% from 26.4%. The adjusted Q2 margin of 11.7% is better, but still clearly below the peak.
    stockanalysis.com
    −3
  • ROIC
    ROIC has fallen from 19.1% (2023) and 14.5% (2024) to 9.4% TTM; ROE from 28.8% to 20.9%. Volvo's own ROCE for the industrial operations is 26.8%, but the trend is the same: lower returns than two years ago.
    stockanalysis.com
    −3
  • Quality of growth
    Neither bigger nor stronger: revenue fell 4.6% in 2024 and 9.0% in 2025 and is SEK 471.5bn TTM vs 552bn in 2023; EPS is 17.62 vs 24.78 in 2024. Construction equipment has fallen from SEK 105bn to 78bn since 2023.
    stockanalysis.com
    −3
  • Future segment (electric trucks)
    Volvo's share of the EU29 market for battery-electric heavy trucks has fallen to 16.3% from 33.8%, while its overall share is 19.6%. Deliveries of electric vehicles fell 2% (12 months), so competitors are catching up in the segment that has to carry the 2030 requirements.
    investing.com
    −3

Cracks−10 of −35

  • US tariffs
    Tariffs cost about SEK 1.1bn in the truck division in Q2. Q3 is expected to be offset by IEEPA refunds (a one-off), while Section 232 credits for heavy vehicles are not yet booked and await the authorities.

    The stock has fallen about 8% from its August peak on weaker order intake for heavy trucks in the US and uncertainty about the transition to the EPA27 emissions rules (Sept 2026). Management must also accelerate deliveries sharply in H2 to reach its market forecast of 265,000 units.

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The B share (VOLV.B). Cyclical = yes, because demand for trucks and construction equipment follows the freight and replacement cycle (revenue has fallen from SEK 552bn to 472bn since 2023). stockanalysis' net debt of SEK 213bn and EV multiples include Volvo Financial Services' loan book; the industrial business has net cash.
