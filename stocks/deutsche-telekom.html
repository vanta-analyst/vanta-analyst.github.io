    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −10 of −35
    • Competition in the US
      The TMUS share has fallen 31% over a year: Verizon's 'Value Pivot' with price cuts and a three-year price guarantee presses price growth of 2.5–3% per account, and TMUS has stopped reporting postpaid phone additions. The main asset is under real competitive pressure.
      lightreading.com
      −4
    • Merger plans and capital allocation
      CEO Höttges has since April 2026 been working on a full merger with T-Mobile US, which requires backing from the minority and the German state, and US authorities are expected to require that the money stays in the US. Elliott has bought a stake and opposes the plan in favour of larger buybacks; unresolved strategy and risk of value-destroying share issuance.
      finance.yahoo.com
      −4
    • Debt
      Net debt EUR 141bn including leases, debt/EBITDA 3.1x, interest cover 4.0x and Altman Z-score 1.13; heavy balance sheet if interest rates rise or TMUS has to finance further spectrum and fibre purchases.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
