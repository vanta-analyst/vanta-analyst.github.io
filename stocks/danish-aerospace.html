    Bull case

    Commercial space stations (Axiom, Starlab and others) and ESA order E4D and medical equipment, and the defence track brings the first contracts, so revenue reaches DKK 40-50m with positive EBITDA. At 2x sales the market cap would be DKK 80-100m, about 2x today; more requires a major defence breakthrough.

    Bear case

    Orders keep failing to materialise, cash is empty, and the company has to do yet another guaranteed issue with a 10% commission far below DKK 2.40. With revenue under DKK 20m and a loss, the price could halve again.
