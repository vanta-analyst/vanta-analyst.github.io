    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −9 of −35
    • Regulation: credit card interest-rate cap
      President Trump demanded in January 2026 a 10% cap on credit card interest rates for a year; the stock had its biggest one-day fall in nine months. A cap probably requires Congress, but the political risk hits the core of the business (credit cards are by far the largest segment).
      barchart.com
      −2
    • Credit quality and provisions
      The loss rate on US cards was 4.71% in Q2 (improved), but the quarter was lifted by a USD 662m reserve release, and coverage was lowered to 5.02%; in a consumer slowdown that effect reverses.
      marketbeat.com
      −2
    • Legal legacy from Discover
      10-Q for Q2 2026: SEC investigation into Discover's card misclassification, an amended shareholder lawsuit (motion to dismiss filed 30 Apr 2026) and the interchange litigation with Visa and Mastercard; the FDIC previously ordered at least USD 1.225bn in repayments.
      sec.gov
      −2
    • Integration risk
      The bank is 14 of 24 months into the Discover integration (only about a third of cost synergies realized) and has also integrated Brex; CET1 fell 70 bp to 13.7% in Q2 due to buybacks, Brex (about 40 bp) and higher risk-weighted assets.
      marketbeat.com
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Q3 report is due on 20 Oct 2026 (after the close) and is not included; data as of 16 Oct 2026. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/TBV and P/E. Discover was acquired in May 2025 (share-funded), so the 2025 figures are distorted by merger costs and day-1 provisions, and the P/B history before 2025 is not directly comparable (goodwill). P/TBV history before the merger has not been retrieved. Brex (USD 5.15bn) closed in Q2 2026. The status of the proposal for a 10% cap on credit card interest rates is not clarified in the sources. Insider trading has not been investigated.
