    • Margin and ROIC

      In Q2, $44.4m of $57.0m adjusted EBITDA (78%) came from Rare Metals at record prices for hafnium, gallium and tantalum. The magnet business Magnequench, which is the magnet story itself, contributed only $10.5m.

      The magnet plant in Narva (Estonia, 2,000 t/year) opened in September 2025 but still ships only qualification samples; 2–3 customer programmes are to enter commercial production in 2026, and the Bosch deal is only an MOU.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Price in CAD, accounts in USD (stockanalysis' statistics page converts to CAD, financials are in USD). TTM net income is almost zero despite operating profit of $82m due to tax and one-offs from 2025 (including the sale of the Chinese separation plants); the forward P/E rests on adjusted EPS. The company is not a pure commodity producer: Magnequench partly works with pass-through pricing, but 78% of EBITDA now comes from hafnium, gallium and tantalum at record prices, hence cyclical. 'Commodities & uranium' was chosen as the closest industry for an integrated processor of rare earths and specialty metals. Insider trading has not been examined.
