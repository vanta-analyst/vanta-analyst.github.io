    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −9 of −30
    • Margin
      The operating margin has fallen from 13.0% (2024/25) to 11.3% TTM, and the adjusted EBIT margin was 12.5% in Q3 vs 15.2% and 14.5% in Q1 and Q2. A 60% gross margin turns into a low operating margin for medtech (Stryker: 23.8%).
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • ROIC
      ROIC is 9.0% TTM, below a WACC of 11.7% and down from 11.2% in 2024/25. It was 2.0–8.9% in 2021/22–2023/24, so the company has never consistently earned its cost of capital.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Endoscopy (64% of revenue) grew 16% organically in Q3, but anaesthesia and patient monitoring are only +1.3% over 12 months. Reported growth in DKK was only 4.7% TTM because of the weak dollar.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Quality of earnings
      A full-year margin at the upper end of 12–14% partly rests on tariff refunds: DKK 85m received after Q3, and another 70m may come. Management expects an ongoing tariff cost of about 1 percentage point of margin before refunds next year.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −12 of −35
    • Guidance
      Organic growth guidance for 2025/26 has been cut for the second time this year (Ritzau): on 25/8 from 10–12% (set 6/5) to about 10%, because anaesthesia and patient monitoring are now only expected to grow at a low single-digit rate. (The old list said 'three times'.)
      dknyt.dk
      −4
    • Customers are shifting
      After the price increases Ambu lost volume at selected anaesthesia customers, cf. the Q2 report. The effect now shows in the 12-month figures. Management says no further customers were lost in Q3.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Competition (urology)
      Competition in urology has intensified. Ambu has given price concessions on aScope 4 Cysto at selected customers and ambulatory surgery centres (strategic pricing below the earlier level). Management will not quantify the effect on gross margin, and analysts are asking about higher commercial investment.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Currency and tariffs
      According to the CFO, the weak dollar is the biggest external headwind for the margin. Goods from Malaysia face a 10% US tariff, and contracts typically have no automatic price adjustment.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
