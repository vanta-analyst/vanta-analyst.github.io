    Bull case

    Churchrock gets the discharge permit, and the NRC licence is renewed without intervention, so the company can become a new ISR producer in the USA around 2029. At the same time Queensland opens for uranium. The total PEA NPV of about US$1bn (about C$1.4bn) at 0.25–0.4x NAV gives C$350–550m vs a market cap of about C$135m, i.e. 2.5–4x. Boss Energy is an obvious takeover candidate.

    Bear case

    The NRC lets New Mexico intervene, and the renewal drags on for years while Navajo opposition blocks in practice. Queensland keeps the ban, and cash runs out in 2027, so money must be raised at C$0.30-0.40 (+15-25% shares) again and again. The stock could halve but hardly go to zero because the assets have strategic value.
