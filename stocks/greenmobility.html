      Growth has slowed sharply: +70% in 2024, +20% in 2025, +4% in H1 2026 and +1% in Q2 2026. Revenue guidance for 2026 was cut from 8–12% to 5–9%.

    • Moat and competition
      The business is concentrated on Copenhagen, where more taxis increase supply, which Inderes calls 'potentially more structural'. Shared cars have low switching costs, and driverless robotaxis could in time undermine the model. Dependence on political support for reserved parking spaces is a risk.
