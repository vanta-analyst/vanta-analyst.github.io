    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −10 of −35
    • Unlisted valuation (Nscale)
      Nscale has become Aker's second-largest asset (NOK 32bn, about 26% of gross assets of NOK 121bn), valued at the latest private funding round. The value rests on a few large contracts (Microsoft, OpenAI/Stargate Norway, Anthropic agreement in Aug. 2026) and an enormous capital need for GPU data centers; a lower next round or a turn in AI investment could hit NAV directly.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Oil price risk (Aker BP)
      Aker BP is the largest holding and contributed about NOK 15bn of the NAV increase in Q1; listed investments of NOK 65.9bn are dominated by oil and offshore companies (Aker BP, Aker Solutions, Solstad, Akastor/HMH), so a fall in the oil price or investments on the Norwegian shelf hits NAV directly.
      inderes.fi
      −3
    • Governance and control
      Free float is only 23.6 of 74.3 m shares; Kjell Inge Røkke's TRG controls about two-thirds. The minority has no real influence, and related-party transactions (e.g. the offer to take Aker BioMarine off the exchange) are decided by the main shareholder.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1
    • Debt in the group
      Consolidated debt of kr 45.8bn vs kr 3.1bn in cash (net debt kr 42.7bn, up from 25.3bn at the end of 2024), Altman Z 2.58. Most of it sits in subsidiaries such as Solstad, but it increases the financial leverage behind NAV.
      stockanalysis.com

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company: stockanalysis' earnings figures (P/E 3.7, net income NOK 27.9bn TTM) are distorted by revaluations and the consolidation of Solstad Maritime among others, so the valuation is based on net asset value (NAV) from the company's own reports. NAV per share for Q3 will only be published on 4 Nov 2026; the discount is calculated against Q2 NAV of NOK 1,429. Industry set to 'Finance, data & insurance brokerage' like Investor AB and Exor, even though the portfolio is dominated by oil (Aker BP) and AI infrastructure (Nscale). Not flagged cyclical, as oil exposure is under half of gross assets. The historical NAV discount (approx. 15–20%) is documented only for end of 2025 and Q2 2026, not a full 10-year series.
