    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Insurance: payouts are measured against net profit and Aegon's own free cash flow (holding level); stockanalysis' FCF (1.74bn, P/FCF 6.6) includes insurance and investment flows and is not meaningful. The revenue forecast (−25% in 2026) is an IFRS 17/restructuring artefact. ROIC (3.7%) is not meaningful for an insurer. The trailing P/E of 12.2 is on reported EPS of 0.62; the forward P/E of 8.7 is on adjusted EPS (0.86). The ASR stake (about 24%, worth about EUR 3.5bn at ASR's market value of 14.7bn = about 30% of Aegon's market value) is equity-accounted: Aegon's share of ASR's net profit (about 0.27bn) is about 29% of Aegon's net profit, but only the dividend (about 0.17bn) comes in as cash. ASR's own findings (including a record valuation, disability, combined ratio) are NOT counted again; only the effect on Aegon's earnings quality. That Aegon can sell ASR shares to fund buybacks is flexibility but earns no plus. Share count −4.7% in a year matches buybacks of 625m (about 80m shares at about EUR 7.5). The price target (8.11) is only 4% above the price. The move to the US (EGM 8 Oct 2026, renaming to Transamerica Inc., US GAAP from the 2027 accounts) could lead to exits from European indices and forced selling – not quantified. The sale of Aegon UK has been announced, but price and timing have not been examined. Next report 18 Feb 2027.
