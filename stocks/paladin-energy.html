    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The primary listing is ASX (cannot be traded on Nordnet), so the analysis follows the TSX listing in CAD, which Nordnet has (search API 11/10-2026: PDN, CAD, TSX). Stockanalysis has no forecast page for the TSX listing (404); the 15 analysts (consensus Buy, price target AUD 13.49) are on quote/asx/PDN, so formula points for upside and revision may end up empty. The accounts are in USD with a July–June financial year (FY26 = July 2025–June 2026). Langer Heinrich is 75% owned (CNNC 25%). The PLS figures (capex USD 1,226m, NPV USD 1,325m at USD 90/lb) are from the FEED update in August 2025; FEED is not complete, and the investor day 2/9-2026 gave no new figures that I could find. The contract book (volumes, floors and ceilings) was not found. The status of the class actions after mid-2025 is not confirmed. Insider trading has not been investigated (insiders own 0.4%). Q1 FY27 report expected 14/10-2026.
