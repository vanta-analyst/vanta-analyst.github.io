    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −15 of −30
    • Future segment
      Losing market share in obesity to Lilly (Zepbound/Mounjaro), and its share outside the US is also falling. CagriSema disappointed with 20.4% weight loss vs an expected about 25%. The Wegovy pill (over 5m prescriptions since January) is a bright spot, but Lilly's orforglipron is coming as a competitor.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue grew 25% in 2024 but only 6.4% in 2025, and consensus expects −2.9% in 2026 and +1.3% in 2027. EPS is expected to fall from DKK 23.03 to 21.66. Guidance is 0 to −6% adjusted sales; growth must come from volume while prices fall about 10% a year.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • ROIC and margin
      ROIC has fallen from 88.6% (2023) to 68.2% (2024), 42.6% (2025) and 39.2% TTM after the Catalent plants and heavy capex (DKK 56bn TTM). The gross margin has gone from 85.0% to 82.0% and is expected at 79.8% in 2026. Still world-class (operating margin 49%), but weaker, not stronger.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      Impairments of DKK 6.3bn on pipeline assets in Q2 2026, including DKK 4.0bn on monlunabant, on top of the capacity build-up that now meets falling prices. Debt rose from DKK 27bn (2023) to 140bn to fund Catalent and acquisitions.
      qz.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −25 of −35
    • Customers are shifting
      US prescriptions for Wegovy injections fell 22%, and self-pay patients now make up 35% of volume at lower prices. Patients are switching to Lilly's tirzepatide.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Price pressure and regulation
      Novo has accepted the Medicare price deal on obesity drugs and the IRA's negotiated maximum prices on semaglutide from January 2027; Medicare negotiated a 71% discount on Wegovy. On top come the MFN deal and Medicaid.
      seekingalpha.com
      −6
    • Patent expiry
      The semaglutide patent expired in Canada, Brazil and China in 2026, with copies following, and the US/EU follow in the early 2030s. The capital markets day gave no clear answer on how the loss will be covered.
      ad-hoc-news.de
      −4
    • Pipeline execution
      The ZEUS trial of ziltivekimab failed, monlunabant was written down by DKK 4.0bn, and CagriSema did not meet expectations.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Guidance and targets
      The capital markets day on 21/9/2026 offered only growth 'in line with the industry' and a broadly stable margin towards 2030, with no concrete figures. The stock fell 7.7% on the day, and Danske Bank called it disappointing.
      ugebrev.dk
      −2
    • Lawsuits
      In July 2026 a US judge allowed parts of the shareholder lawsuit over misleading statements ahead of the CagriSema data to proceed. There is also a class action over an artificially sustained semaglutide shortage and 3,763 injury cases in the GLP-1 MDL (June 2026).

      −2
