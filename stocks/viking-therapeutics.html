    • Moat and future segment
      Obesity is the segment of the future, but VK2735 is a GLP-1/GIP dual agonist – the same mechanism as Lilly's tirzepatide, which already sells for about 49 bn USD a year. The oral version gave 12.2% weight loss in 13 weeks, but about 20% dropped out due to side effects, and the stock fell 41% on the day (August 2025). The whole company rests on one molecule; VK2809 (MASH) is stalled without a partner.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −17 of −35
    • Competition and price pressure
      By the time VK2735 can launch at the earliest (about 2028-29), there will be tirzepatide at about USD 346/month via TrumpRx, oral pills from Lilly (Foundayo) and Novo (Wegovy pill) and monthly agents from Amgen (MariTide) and Pfizer/Metsera. Viking has to compete as no. 5-6 on price against giants with their own production and sales force.
      marketbeat.com
      −6
    • Funding and dilution
      Two days after maintenance data (22/9-2026) Viking raised about USD 548m net: 9.0m new shares (+7.7%) at about a 16% discount and USD 259m in 2% convertible bonds (2032, conversion USD 50.75 = up to 5.1m shares). Cash covers phase 3, but hardly an own launch – more capital, a partner or a sale of the company is needed.
      sec.gov
      −4
    • Binary data risk and timeline
      Topline from VANQUISH-1 (approx. 4,500 participants) and VANQUISH-2 (approx. 1,000 with type 2 diabetes), both 78 weeks, is not expected until 2027; so no decisive phase 3 data will come in 2026. The maintenance figures of 97% and 90% come from individual dose groups with approx. 12 participants (pooled 90% and 85%).
      marketbeat.com
      −4

      19.4% of the shares are sold short (12 days to cover). The price rose 36% to USD 40.85 on data 22/9 and has since fallen to USD 28.51 (−30% in two weeks); −12% over 52 weeks.
