    Bull case

    Ceasefire in Ukraine, VAT refunds resume, and production returns to 6–10m tonnes (2021: 11m tonnes, USD 2.5bn revenue, EBITDA over 1bn). Just 6m tonnes at USD 120/t with a 30% EBITDA margin gives approx. USD 215m EBITDA; at 4x EV/EBITDA equity becomes approx. USD 0.9bn – approx. 2–2.5x the current market cap. 3–10x requires both peace and the state dropping the cases against FPM.

    Bear case

    The state wins: FPM is declared bankrupt or nationalised (49.5% has already been transferred to ARMA), or the damages claim of USD 3.5bn – ten times the market cap – is maintained. Then the share price is close to zero. Even without that, continued power attacks and missing VAT could force a new issue below 16.5 pence.
