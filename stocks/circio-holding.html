    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −27 of −30
    • Margin
      No revenue. The operating loss is NOK 63m TTM vs 41m in 2025 and 42m in 2024, and costs are rising: 16 employees vs 11 recently, target of 20–25 in 2027, a 50% larger laboratory and a new facility in Stockholm.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Everything is preclinical. The heart programme (Danon) aims for IND in H2 2028 and clinic in 2029, and eye data are expected later in 2026. In in vivo CAR-T the company has not yet confirmed that the spleen signal comes from T cells, and management is still investigating whether high or prolonged expression could cause toxicity.
      investing.com
      −8
    • Moat
      Patented circVec vector, but big pharma now owns several circRNA companies (Lilly bought ORNA, BMS bought Orbital), and management itself mentions the competition. The moat is not clinically proven.
      investornytt.no
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Four capital raises in 2026 for a total of 820 m NOK, and the share count has risen from about 98 m to 291.5 m (about +200%). The 15/9 issue went to new investors at a 9% discount, and the repair issue to existing shareholders was cancelled on 23/9. The money is to be used, among other things, for in-licensing or acquisition of LNP and vector technology.
      investegate.co.uk
      −5
