    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Industry: the list has no chemicals category; 'Commodities & uranium' was chosen as the closest (like Solvay and Yara), and earnings follow the chemicals cycle, hence cyclical. TTM net income (€5.8bn) and the trailing P/E of 21 are lifted by a one-off after-tax gain of about €3.5bn from the sale of Coatings; underlying EPS is expected at about €3.0 in 2026. The sale proceeds (€5.8bn before tax) fund both debt reduction and buybacks. Insider trading, litigation (PFAS etc.) and credit rating have not been examined.
