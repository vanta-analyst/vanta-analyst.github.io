Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Thales manages on adjusted EBIT and adjusted EPS (purchase price allocation stripped out); reported EPS TTM is €7.26 vs. analysts' adjusted 2026E of €10.8, so trailing P/E 31 overstates the price. TTM FCF of €4.0bn (FCF yield 8.6%) is inflated by prepayments and working capital in H1 (€1.8–1.9bn vs. €0.5bn); analysts expect about €2.4bn FCF in 2026. The French state (about 26.6%) and Dassault Aviation (about 26.6%) together control over half of the shares (float about 44%) – mentioned, not scoring. The space cooperation with Airbus and Leonardo (Bromo) and the Exail acquisition have not been assessed. Q3 revenue comes 23/10.
