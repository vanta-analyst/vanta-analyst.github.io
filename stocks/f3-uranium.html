    Bull case

    The Tetra zone or JR's extension gives new high-grade intercepts, and the resource grows from 11.8 to 25–30m lb. McElroy has previously sold a neighbouring discovery (Fission/Triple R) to Paladin. In a takeover at CAD 10–15/lb for 25–30m lb, the company would be worth CAD 250–450m vs a market cap of about CAD 76m, i.e. 3–6x.

    Bear case

    The summer and winter drilling disappoints, and after the reverse split money must be raised at a far lower valuation. The CAD 15m bond (about 20% of market cap) must be refinanced by 2028 at the latest. A price around CAD 0.05 (pre-consolidation) is realistic if the resource does not grow.

Has a small but very high-grade NI 43-101 resource (JR Zone, 11.8m lb indicated), but no PEA. Fiscal year July–June; the latest published figures are as of 31/3-2026 – the annual report at 30/6-2026 comes about 28/10-2026, so cash today is lower than the C$21.9m used here. Share count: the company reports 631.8m shares (24/9-2026), stockanalysis 661m. A 1:10 consolidation has been approved by shareholders, but awaits TSXV. Insider trades and the largest shareholders have not been investigated systematically. Compared with CanAlaska (same basin), F3 has a resource, but also convertible debt and a large warrant/option overhang.
