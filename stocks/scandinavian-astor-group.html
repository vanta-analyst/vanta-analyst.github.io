    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −17 of −30
    • Margin
      Adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 19.2% in 2025 to 12.8% in H1 2026 and only 6.2% in Q2 (adjusted EBITDA 10.6m on revenue of 171m), because large deliveries were pushed to H2 and the product mix was weak. Reported EBITDA of 141.6m in Q2 is due to a revaluation gain of 131.9m.
      inderes.dk
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 82% in H1 2026, but according to the CEO most of it is due to acquisitions; organic growth is not stated for H1 (2025: organic 42%). The strategy is buy & build (NSG, PBH Teknik etc.), so growth is primarily acquired.
      placera.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      NSG was bought for SEK 467.8m, paid with cash, 8.8m new shares at SEK 19.64 (12.4% dilution) and promissory notes, partly financed with an acquisition loan of 257.5m (STIBOR + 2.25%). PBH Teknik (88.2m) followed in July. Operating cash flow was only 14.6m in H1 (-25.9m in Q2), so the acquisitions are financed with shares and debt, not own cash flow.
      placera.se
      −5
    • Moat
      Astor is a collection of small Swedish niche producers in protection/safety (Astor Protect) and advanced manufacturing (Astor Industry). There are real customer relationships in the defence industry, but few signs of a technological or pricing moat beyond being an approved supplier.
      inderes.dk
      −2

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −18 of −35
    • Management change
      CEO Mattias Hjorth stepped down in April 2026, and board member Martin Elovsson took over in May. CFO Wictor Billström left in March (interim CFO, new CFO Niclas Lundin from 1/9). A full replacement of top management in the middle of a major acquisition phase.
      news.cision.com
      −5
    • Debt and financing
      The acquisition loan of SEK 257.5m (3 years) plus promissory notes and earn-outs to the NSG sellers and the PBH purchase price give an estimated net debt of approx. 400m, approx. 3x pro forma EBITDA. The equity ratio was 38.3% (45.6% after the issue). With negative operating cash flow in Q2 there is limited room for new cash acquisitions without more shares.
      inderes.dk
      −5
    • Execution and customer concentration
      Q2 disappointed: large deliveries under 'a larger customer contract' were postponed to H2, adjusted EBITDA fell to 10.6m, and the CEO wrote that he is not satisfied with the quarter. A single large contract can move an entire quarter, and the customers are not named.
      placera.se
      −6

      Listed on NGM with lower requirements than Nasdaq. The stock has fallen 51% in a year and 23% in three months, and the directed issue to the NSG sellers was at SEK 19.64, 29% above the current price.
