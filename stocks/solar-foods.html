    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −25 of −30
    • Margin
      Revenue is EUR 0.16m TTM vs an operating loss of -12.4m and a net loss of -13.6m. H1 2026: revenue 0.1m and operating loss -6.8m. The company so far sells only sample and test quantities from Factory 01.
      stockanalysis.com
      −12
    • Quality of growth
      The order book fell from EUR 1.5m to 0.2m in a year. The only concrete consumer launch is a protein powder (Planta from Ambrosia Collective) in the USA in May 2026. The volume agreements are non-binding LOIs and MoUs, and sales in the EU require novel food approval, which has not yet been granted.
      investors.solarfoods.com
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      Operations are financed with issues (EUR 8.8m in 2024 and EUR 25m in January 2026 with 5.15m new shares, about +21%) and public grants. The share count has risen from 20.5m (2023) to 30.1m. Grant financing is however cheaper for shareholders than pure equity.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat
      Solein (microbial protein from CO2, hydrogen and electricity) is patent-protected, and the company is early, but gas fermentation is also pursued by Calysta, Air Protein, NovoNutrients and Unibio, and precision fermentation and cheap plant protein are strong alternatives. The price (EUR 17/kg in the company's illustration) is high vs whey and plant protein.
      investors.solarfoods.com
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −22 of −35
    • Financing of Factory 02
      The first phase costs 134 m EUR (both phases 182 m), and the plan requires 65–75 m in debt, about 95 m from strategic partners and 110 m in IPCEI grants. In June Business Finland granted only 39.6 m in grants plus a loan of 38.1 m, and about 21 m under the IPCEI notification awaits a decision. The investment decision is promised in 2026 and has not yet been made with three months left.
      proteinproductiontechnology.com
      −10
    • Regulation
      The novel food approval in the EU is still in process, and the company has not received a No Questions Letter from the FDA (Solein is sold in the US on self-affirmed GRAS and in Singapore). Without EU approval, Factory 02's output cannot be sold in the home market.
      investors.solarfoods.com
      −5
    • Commercial validation
      The largest deal is a non-binding LOI for 500–1,650 tonnes a year with an unnamed sports nutrition brand (July 2025). Together with two MoUs it could cover 50–60% of Factory 02's capacity, but only if the agreements become binding. The order book is EUR 0.2m.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Scaling and technology risk
      Factory 01 is a demonstration plant; the first phase of Factory 02 is to produce 3,200 tons a year and the whole plant up to 12,800 tons. The production price of EUR 6.5/kg is a plan, not documented at large scale, and depends on cheap power and hydrogen (energy agreement with Fortum under development).
      investors.solarfoods.com
      −3
