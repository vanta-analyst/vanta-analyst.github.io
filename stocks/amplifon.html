    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen every year from 9.5% (2021) to 6.3% TTM and is now below the WACC of 7.0%; ROE has fallen from 18.9% to 5.7%.

      The purchase of GN Hearing for €2.3bn (about 14x EBITDA 2025) is financed with up to €1bn of debt, a share issue of €453m at €10 per share (about 20% of capital) and 56m shares to GN; at the same time own shares were bought for €108m in 2025 at far higher prices. Barclays expects about 3% EPS dilution in 2027.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −11 of −35
    • Suppliers become competitors
      With GN Hearing, Amplifon becomes a manufacturer itself and competes with its largest suppliers: Sonova and Demant supply about 20% and 15% of the products. The retail business has so far been supplier-neutral.
      ca.investing.com
      −4
    • Integration and debt
      Pro forma debt/EBITDA rises to about 2.7x after closing; net debt incl. leases is already 1.07 bn € and Altman Z 1.6. Integrating a manufacturer with its own sales channel is a new business area, and closing awaits regulatory approval.
      ca.investing.com
      −4
    • Execution and guidance
      Q4 2025 disappointed, and 2026 guidance was withdrawn on 5/3 2026 (stock −13%); the share price has fallen over 75% from its peak above 34 € in 2024, and the Fit4Growth programme is closing about 250 clinics (−3.5% on revenue in Q2).

      −3

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Hearing aid chain (clinic and retail network), placed under Medicine & medtech like Demant and GN; Health insurance & services was the alternative. Stockanalysis' share count change (+2.5%) is misleading: 45.3m new shares were issued in May 2026 (about +20%), and 56m more shares go to GN Store Nord at closing (general meeting 29/10) – in total about 45% more shares than in 2025. Market cap (€3.2bn) and EV include neither the GN shares nor up to €1bn of new debt. Forward P/E 15.2 is based on adjusted EPS; reported EPS is €0.31 TTM (trailing P/E 38.8). FCF after leasing (€230m TTM) is used, as the clinics are leased. S&P's rating (BB+ stable according to news coverage) is not confirmed in a primary source. The GN deal still requires competition approval. Q3 comes 29/10.
