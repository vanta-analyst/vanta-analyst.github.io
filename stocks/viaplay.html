    Bull case

    The Netherlands sale and Allente synergies (SEK 300–400m) lift EBITDA to about SEK 2bn (double-digit margin) in 2028; at 7x EBITDA and net debt of about 3.8bn that gives equity of about SEK 10bn = about SEK 2.2 per share (about 2x). A tripling requires a margin above 12% or a bid from Canal+/PPF with a large premium.

    Bear case

    Rising sports rights prices or the loss of several rights (such as the NHL) put pressure on subscriptions and margin, FCF stays negative, and the 2028 maturity of SEK 6.9bn requires a new recapitalisation – in 2024 that gave about 98% dilution.
