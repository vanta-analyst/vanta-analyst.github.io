    Bull case

    The subscription model drives growth in Anti-Piracy from 2027, EBITDA rises to USD 6–8m on USD 45–50m in revenue, and the lower interest rate lifts FCF to USD 4–5m. At 8x EBITDA and falling debt, equity value could be EUR 50–60m, i.e. about 2x. 3–10x requires an acquisition by a larger player in content protection.

    Bear case

    Pay-TV customers are cutting back, licences keep falling 20%+, and EBITDA is disappearing. With USD 17m in net debt, equity can quickly become insignificant, and an issue at EUR 0.15–0.20 or a debt conversion could halve the share price.
