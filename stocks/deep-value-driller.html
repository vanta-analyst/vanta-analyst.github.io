    Penny deep dive. The decline of -90% is NOT a collapse: the company sold its only asset, the drillship Deep Value Driller, to Eldorado Drilling for USD 300m (completed 10/9-2026), repaid the loan and is paying an extraordinary dividend of NOK 20.60 per share (about NOK 1,926m, ex-date 8/10, payment about 16/10-2026). The company is changing its name to 3030 DVD AS (registration about 8/10-2026), so the Nordnet name may change. A price of NOK 1.93 (9/10-2026, after the ex-date) and 93.51m shares give a market cap of about NOK 180m (DKK 124m); Nordnet's DKK 1,410m is from before the dividend. The board expects residual cash of NOK 0.80-1.00 per share after the dividend, transaction and liquidation costs, so the stock trades at about 2x the guided cash. Stockanalysis' EV (263m) is based on the 30/6 balance sheet with loans that have now been repaid. The residual inventory has unknown value.
