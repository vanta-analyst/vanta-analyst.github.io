    • Governance and ownership battle
      MPS (via Mediobanca, about 13%), Delfin (about 10%), Caltagirone and UniCredit are fighting for influence; the Milan prosecutor's office is investigating Caltagirone and Delfin for acting in concert over control of Generali via Mediobanca. The strategy risks being steered by major shareholders' banking interests.
      en.ilsole24ore.com
      −3
    • Banca Generali and cross-ownership
      MPS has bid EUR 8.7bn in shares for Banca Generali (Generali's fastest-growing and high-yielding subsidiary), and Generali's board will 'assess' the offer. Acceptance turns a controlled asset manager into a minority stake in MPS, which is itself Generali's largest shareholder (cross-ownership and conflict of interest).

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The share price may be supported by the ownership fight (MPS-Mediobanca about 13%, Delfin about 10%, Caltagirone about 7%, UniCredit) rather than fundamentals.
