    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −21 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin in the automotive segment was 2.3% in Q2 2026 (5.4% a year earlier) and 3.6% in H1, and full-year guidance is 1–3% vs BMW's own target of 8–10% and 9.8% in 2023. The group gross margin has fallen from 18.0% (2023) to 12.4% TTM.
      bmwgroup-werke.com
      −9
    • ROIC
      ROIC is 2.9% TTM, and ROE 6.6% vs 16–18% in 2022–23. BMW itself guides automotive ROCE to only 1–5% for 2026.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue is €128.0bn TTM vs 155.5bn in 2023, and automotive revenue fell 7.7% in Q2 on 4.9% fewer cars delivered. EPS is expected to fall 44% to €6.64 in 2026 (€17.68 in 2023).
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Future segment (EVs)
      The EV share rose to 19.8% in Q2 (31% in Europe), and the Neue Klasse (iX3) promises 40–50% lower drivetrain costs. But in China the EV share is only 9.7%, and sales of both electric and petrol cars to Chinese customers are falling in favour of local brands.
      investing.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      The 2025 dividend (€4.40 per share, about €2.6bn) and the third €2bn buyback programme together exceed the automotive FCF guidance of more than €2.5bn for 2026. Payouts are therefore partly funded from the balance sheet while earnings halve.
      investing.com
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −11 of −35
    • China and profit warning
      Retail sales in China fell 30.2% in Q2 (from 169,000 to 118,000 cars). That triggered a profit warning in June: automotive margin guidance was cut from 4–6% to 1–3%, and group pre-tax profit is now expected to fall markedly. The stock fell to its lowest level since autumn 2020.
      press.bmwgroup.com
      −6
    • US tariffs
      Tariffs take about 1.25 percentage points off the automotive EBIT margin in 2026, a clearly bigger burden than the year before.
      press.bmwgroup.com
      −3
    • Macro and currency
      BMW cites the Middle East conflict, high energy prices and weak consumer confidence in several markets as reasons for the downgrade, and currency cost €400m of EBIT in Q2 alone.
      boerse-online.de
      −2
