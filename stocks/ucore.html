    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Pre-revenue company on TSX Venture. The Nordnet API shows 'Ucore Rare Metals Inc' (UCU, Canada) as tradable (slug ucore-rare-metals-inc-ucu-xtsx). The 141.8m shares include the private placement of 24.6m shares at C$2.80 in August; cash at 30/6 (C$19.9m) is from before the C$69m gross placement. According to the company, the Orca debt was repaid on 9/9 (not verified). The DoD funds (about $27m in total) are grants that need not be repaid. The company has not published an NPV for the Louisiana plant, so EV vs NPV cannot be calculated. The earlier plan of 2,000 t/year in 2026 appears to have slipped (Machine A only in H1 2027), but this has not been confirmed directly by the company.
