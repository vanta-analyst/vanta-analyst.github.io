    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −13 of −35
    • Overcapacity
      A record order book of 13–14m TEU (about 42% of the fleet); deliveries rise to 2.8m TEU in 2027 and 3.5m TEU in 2028, and supply is expected to grow faster than demand in 2027 regardless of the outcome in the Strait of Hormuz.

    • Temporary earnings
      The guidance lift is due to unbalanced trade flows and rerouting because of disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, where spot rates rose sharply. Earnings are carried by a geopolitical event that could reverse quickly; analysts expect EPS −74% in 2027.

      A.P. Møller Holding owns about 41.5% of the capital and about 51.5% of the votes via the A shares; B shareholders have no real influence.
