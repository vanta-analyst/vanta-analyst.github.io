    Bull case

    Creditors approve the composition, the convertible loans of 100m are paid out, the dispute with Infiniteria is settled with a licence agreement, and the licence intentions in Brazil and North America become real contracts. Even then about SEK 180m of financing must be converted at a discount, so a 3x price rise requires the market cap to reach about SEK 600-700m after a doubling of the share count. That is very unlikely.

    Bear case

    Creditors reject the plan, or Infiniteria wins the arbitration, and the restructuring ends in bankruptcy. The stock goes toward zero. Even if the plan is approved, ongoing conversions at 90-95% of VWAP push the price down (a classic 'death spiral').
