    Bull case

    The refinancing succeeds without a new large issue, the gold price holds, and Tuvatu lifts production towards 6–8,000 oz per quarter. Then EBITDA could reach 50+ m C$, and an EV of 5x gives equity of approx. 200m C$ (fully diluted approx. 0.35 C$) – just under a doubling; 3x+ requires an acquisition or a marked resource upgrade.

    Bear case

    The refinancing fails or happens via a deep issue at 0.08–0.10 C$, or Nebari takes over the asset. With approx. 43m C$ of senior debt ahead of shareholders and secured convertible holders, the stock could fall 50–90%.
