    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (stockanalysis shows negative FCF due to lending movements); valued on P/B and P/E, and payouts are measured against net income. Dividends paid over the last 12 months (SEK 33.5bn) exceed net income (31.2bn), so there is no payout plus; the policy is 60–70%. The share is +47% over one year and 8% above the average analyst price target. The Riksbank (1.75%) signals rate hikes that may lift NII; Q3 results in October. Baltic credit risk and property exposure have not been examined separately.
