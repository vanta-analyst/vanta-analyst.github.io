    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' FCF (DKK 4.8bn, FCF yield 37%) is greatly overstated: it includes prepaid tickets (customer funds) and places lease payments under financing (IFRS 16), so no credit has been given for the FCF yield. The Q2 figures are affected by a one-off loss of DKK 733m related to EU ETS (Supreme Court ruling on 2020 allowances). The NLTG acquisition (Ving, Spies, Tjäreborg, Sunclass) has not been completed; EU approval is pending, and the figures are from before the acquisition. Price target and analyst count (6, consensus 'Hold') come from the formulas. September traffic: load factor 86.6% (+1.4 pp.), ASK +2%. Labour relations (strike risk) not examined.
