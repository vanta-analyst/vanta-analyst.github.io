Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Stockanalysis has no statistics page for the Madrid listing (quote/bme/IAG/statistics gives 404), so the path is the London listing (quote/lon/IAG) with the price in pence (GBX); Nordnet also trades the share in Madrid in EUR. The accounts are in EUR, the market cap in GBP (about EUR 22bn at about 1.15 EUR/GBP). Stockanalysis' FCF (EUR 3.95bn TTM) is before leasing; after leasing (1.38bn) it is EUR 2.57bn. The −8.6% share count change is more than the buybacks in the cash flow (EUR 1.14bn TTM ≈ 5–6%) explain; it may be due to timing differences, so buyback points are given only for what is documented. The EPS decline is on adjusted EPS (−10.9% in H1). Earnings are close to the cycle peak (margin −35% in 2021, 6% in 2022), so low multiples must be read accordingly.
