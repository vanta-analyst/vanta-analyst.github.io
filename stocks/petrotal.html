    Bull case

    Eight new wells and better water handling lift production from approx. 12,300 to 20,000 barrels/day in 2027. At Brent $70–75 that gives EBITDA of approx. CAD 250–300m; with EV/EBITDA 2.5–3 and net cash the market value could reach CAD 800–1,000m (approx. CAD 0.9–1.1/share, 1.5–2x), plus a reinstated dividend. A 3x also requires a higher oil price.

    Bear case

    The drilling programme disappoints, erosion or low water in the Marañón stops operations and exports, or Peru politics/PetroPerú cause problems. At 11,000 barrels/day and Brent $55, EBITDA is close to the guidance level (US$30m), cash is eaten by capex, and the stock could fall to C$0.25–0.30 (−50%). Zero is unlikely with net cash and production.
