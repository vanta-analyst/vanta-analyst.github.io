    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Q3 2026 is reported on 29 Oct; TTM figures run to 30 Jun 2026. The stock is effectively an option on AkzoNobel: each Axalta share is exchanged for 0.6539 AkzoNobel shares at completion (expected late 2026/early 2027), so the price follows the AkzoNobel stock. Industry: coatings and paint = 'Construction & materials' (closest; no chemicals category). GAAP EPS is depressed by merger costs, so forward P/E (adjusted) is used. Insider trading and customer concentration among the carmakers have not been investigated. 13F purchases give no points.
