    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −14 of −35
    • AI disintermediation
      The stock fell 13% on 9/2/2026, the biggest drop among the brokers, when OpenAI approved an insurance app in ChatGPT (Tuio) that gives quotes and soon sales without an intermediary. The threat mainly applies to less complex volume work.
      finance.yahoo.com
      −3
    • Falling insurance prices
      Commercial insurance prices are softening in almost all markets; growth will have to come from exposure and new business rather than price increases. Brown & Brown is already at negative organic growth.
      marshberry.com
      −2
    • Recurring restructuring
      After the Transformation programme (2021–24) now comes Propel: about $625m of cash costs and $25m non-cash, kept out of the adjusted figures, with staff cuts. On top come transaction and integration costs of $102m in H1. Restructuring is effectively a recurring cost.
      sec.gov
      −4
    • Customers are shifting
      Clients are postponing discretionary projects because of a weaker labour market; Career project work fell almost 50% in the Middle East, and commissions in the individual marketplace fell outside enrolment periods.
      finance.yahoo.com
      −2
    • Debt
      Net debt has risen from $3.74bn to 5.44bn in six months (net debt/EBITDA 1.42 → 1.93); net borrowing of $1.22bn TTM funds buybacks and Newfront. Altman Z-score 1.39.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

      A law firm is investigating a possible securities lawsuit after the Q1 share price fall; no filed case found.
