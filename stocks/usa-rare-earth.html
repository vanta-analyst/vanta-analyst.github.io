Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Industry set to 'Commodities & uranium' (not 'Grid & electrical equipment'): after the Serra Verde acquisition (closed 3/9) most assets and expected revenue come from mining and oxides in Brazil, and the magnet price follows NdPr/Dy/Tb – hence cyclical. stockanalysis' market value ($3.53bn) uses 244.7m shares from before closing; with 126.8m Serra Verde shares it is in reality about $5.4bn. The net loss ($311m TTM) is distorted by revaluation of warrants/earnout. The Commerce package ($277m grant + $1.3bn loan) is an LOI with milestone-based disbursements. Insider trading is mixed and modest (director Blitzer bought $2.1m in January; minor sales in May–June) – no deduction given. Serra Verde's debt is not quantified in the material.
