    • ROIC and margin
      The company has only just become profitable: non-IFRS operating margin about 27% in Q2 2026 and gross margin 91%. But the IFRS operating profit of EUR 220m in Q2 includes EUR 158m from the sale of a priority review voucher; without it the operating margin was about 18%. 2021-25 produced only losses, so profitability has not been proven over a cycle.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Reported earnings are inflated by one-offs: sale of a priority review voucher (EUR 158m in Q2 2026) and recognition of a deferred tax asset (about USD 736m tax income TTM). Trailing P/E 16.9 is therefore misleading, and the PEG fallback gives −6. The stock switched from ADS to ordinary shares on Nasdaq on 20/4/2026, so stockanalysis lacks 52-week and 200-day data. Insider trades, US price regulation (IRA/MFN) and patent situation are not examined. No formal 2026 guidance found.
