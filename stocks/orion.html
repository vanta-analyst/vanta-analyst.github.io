    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −7 of −30
    • Concentration and quality of growth
      Almost all growth comes from one product, sold by partner Bayer: royalty income has risen from EUR 24m (2021) to EUR 554m (TTM) plus EUR 183m in milestone payments, while product sales have grown only from EUR 1,014m to 1,321m over five years (about 5% a year). Orion controls neither the pricing nor the marketing of Nubeqa.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Capital conversion
      In 2025 FCF was only EUR 219m against net income of EUR 500m, because receivables rose by EUR 337m (payments from Bayer). TTM FCF is EUR 393m against EUR 587m in earnings – an FCF margin of 19% against an operating margin of 36%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Future segment
      The pipeline after Nubeqa is early: ODM-212 has only just entered phase 1b/2, and no significant milestone payments are expected in 2026 (2025 had approx. 180m EUR). The generics and consumer business is growing only slowly.
      biospace.com
      −1

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −11 of −35
    • Patents and generics
      Bayer and Orion are pursuing a patent case in Delaware against Alembic, which wants to launch generic darolutamide; the first compound patent expires in October 2030, while later patents run to 2036–2038. A lost patent defence would hit the bulk of Orion's profit.
      news.bloomberglaw.com
      −4
    • Competition
      Patent protection on Astellas' Xtandi (enzalutamide), which is used in largely the same stages of prostate cancer, expires from 2027; cheap generic enzalutamide could pressure Nubeqa's growth and price with payers.
      pharsight.greyb.com
      −3
    • One-off items
      According to Inderes, Q2 growth (+25% in revenue, +69% in operating result) was partly one-off due to the timing of tablet deliveries to Bayer; H2 is expected to be weaker, as analysts expect lower EPS for the full year than TTM.
      inderes.dk
      −2
    • Currency and tariffs
      A significant part of the Nubeqa income comes from the US, so a weak dollar and American drug tariffs/pricing policy hit operating profit directly; the company itself cites tariff uncertainty as a risk.
      biospace.com
      −2
