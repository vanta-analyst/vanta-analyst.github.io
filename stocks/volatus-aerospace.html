    Bull case

    Canada's defence budget and the DDI marketplace provide a stream of drone orders, the options on 4,900 systems are exercised, and the Mirabel factory lifts revenue to C$80–100m in 2028 with gross margin above 35%. At EV/Sales 8 that equates to a market cap of C$650–800m – only about 1.6–2x today's C$397m. A 3x+ requires the company to become a real NATO supplier or acquisition target.

    Bear case

    Revenue keeps standing still around C$30-40m, the loss of about C$20m a year continues, and the multiple falls back toward the 2024 level (EV/Sales about 3). That corresponds to a market cap around C$100-130m - a fall of 65-75%. The risk of zero is low in the short term, though, because of the cash.
