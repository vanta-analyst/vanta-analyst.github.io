    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' EPS (€0.33 TTM, trailing P/E 16.9) is reported; the company's adjusted net profit is guided at over €1.45bn for 2026 (about €0.43 per share), and forward P/E is based on adjusted figures. FCF TTM of €1.03bn is lifted by about €600m of working capital and was negative in 2023–2024; analysts expect slightly negative FCF in 2026. Snam is a regulated gas grid, so low FCF is partly structural in an investment phase. The 7th regulatory period (WACC, gearing, LNG return) will only be decided in Q1 2027. Insider trading and demand scenarios after 2030 have not been investigated further.
