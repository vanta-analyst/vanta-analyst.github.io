    Bull case

    Coricelli or a Spanish buyer completes at EUR 500m or more, and the minority shareholders get the same price as the funds: about EUR 0.39–0.45 per share (+10–25%). A bidding war that pushes the value to EUR 600m could give about EUR 0.50 (+40%). A 3–10x is not realistic.

    Bear case

    The government blocks, or the deal is structured as a sale of the operating company in which the minority shareholders get no offer. Without a takeover premium and with a normalised olive oil margin, the price could fall back towards 0.18–0.25 EUR (−30–50%).
