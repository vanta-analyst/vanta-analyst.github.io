Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Mixed group (post, BancoPosta, insurance, payments): EV, debt/EBITDA and ROIC on stockanalysis are distorted by customer deposits and insurance liabilities; the payout is held against net profit. All multiples and forecasts are before consolidation of TIM (85.8% after the reopening on 25 Sep); the new group's EPS, debt and share count will not be known until the next results, and the number of new shares (about 0.3bn, about 20% dilution) is an own estimate based on the exchange ratio of 0.218. The remaining TIM minority (about 14%) and the path to delisting have not been clarified. Peers have not been assessed because no listed peer has the same mix. BancoPosta's placement of deposits in government bonds and interest rate sensitivity have not been investigated separately (no deduction without a company-specific source). Insider ownership is shown as n/a. Q3 results 11 Nov.; new business plan with TIM expected in spring 2027.
