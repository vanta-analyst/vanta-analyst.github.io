    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Mixed group (post, BancoPosta, insurance, payments): EV, debt/EBITDA and ROIC on stockanalysis are distorted by customer deposits and insurance liabilities; the payout is held against net profit. All multiples and forecasts are before consolidation of TIM (85.8% after the reopening on 25 Sep); the new group's EPS, debt and share count will not be known until the next results, and the number of new shares (about 0.3bn, about 20% dilution) is an own estimate based on the exchange ratio of 0.218. BancoPosta's placement of deposits in government bonds and interest rate sensitivity have not been investigated separately (no deduction without a company-specific source).
