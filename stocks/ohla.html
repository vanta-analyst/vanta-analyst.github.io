    Bull case

    The construction margin holds at 7%+, revenue grows 6% a year to about EUR 3.9bn, and refinancing lowers the interest rate from about 9.75% to 6%. Net income could then reach EUR 70–90m; at P/E 10 the equity value is EUR 0.7–0.9bn, i.e. about 1.5–1.8x today's 0.49bn. Analyst price targets of EUR 0.59–0.70 (+70–100%) point the same way. A 3–10x requires unrealistically high margins for a contractor.

    Bear case

    New arbitration losses (Qatar, USA and others) and a loss-making major project eat the cash, bank guarantees tighten, and the bonds go into default again. Then a new recapitalization at EUR 0.10-0.20 with heavy dilution (-50-70% for shareholders) is needed. Debt and guarantees of over EUR 600m put shareholders last in line.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Penny analysis (Under DKK 3 – Europe). Market cap checked myself: about 1,395m shares x EUR 0.3502 = about EUR 489m = about DKK 3,645m (Nordnet DKK 3,673m fits). Stockanalysis' gross margin of 43% is misleading for a contractor (subcontracts are booked outside cost of goods); EBIT margin and the company's own figures are used. The company's 'liquidity with recourse' of EUR 711m includes customer advances and funds tied up in joint ventures and projects, so net cash is not freely available. The creditors' waiver after the Qatar ruling is supported by over 75% of bondholders according to the press; the formal confirmation and the banks' waiver have not been read in the company's own announcement. Largest owners are not verified in the CNMV register. No formal restructuring is under way, but the 2025 recapitalisation and the 2026 waiver are close.
