    Bull case

    Phase 2 scales to 14,600 t HOG in 2027 at a cost of about NOK 60/kg, and the salmon price returns to NOK 85-90/kg: that gives an EBITDA of NOK 300-400m. With NOK 2bn in net debt, equity could then be worth NOK 3-4bn (about NOK 6-7 per share, 2x). A 3x also requires phase 3 to be financed without large dilution.

    Bear case

    Biological problems in the new tanks, the salmon price ends up around NOK 70/kg, and the pre-grower tanks plus repayments from 2028 require new equity far below NOK 3. With NOK 2.3bn in debt and negative cash flow, the stock could fall 50–80%.
