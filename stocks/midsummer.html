    Bull case

    The defence customer or new customers order more DUO lines (e.g. SEK 200m+ a year), Bari scales its own panels for European tenders requiring non-Chinese supply chains, and revenue reaches SEK 400–500m with a 15% EBITDA margin. At 3x sales the market value would be SEK 1.2–1.5bn – about 2x; a tripling requires repeat defence/equipment orders.

    Bear case

    No new equipment order after 2026: revenue falls below SEK 100m, the deficit returns, and yet another share issue dilutes 50%+ again. The price could fall 50–70%.
