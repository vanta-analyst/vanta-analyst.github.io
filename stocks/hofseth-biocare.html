    Bull case

    The price increases of over 100% come through, B2B Consumer Health and Boots UK/network marketing scale, and revenue grows to 400m NOK with a 15% EBITDA margin (60m NOK). Combined with a positive NT-II result or a partnership in AecorBio, market cap could go from approx. 0.7 to 2bn NOK (3x).

    Bear case

    The price increases cost volume, revenue keeps falling, and the 121.5m NOK of short-term debt can only be refinanced via a new issue well below 1.30 NOK or a debt conversion to the major owners. With negative equity the stock could fall 50–80%.
