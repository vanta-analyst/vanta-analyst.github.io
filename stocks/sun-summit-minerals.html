    Bull case

    The 2026 assays extend the Creek zone with more intervals such as 81 m at 4.8 g/t, and the resource estimate in Q1 2027 lands at 1–1.5m oz of gold. With gold at a high level and juniors trading at C$30–60/oz in the ground, JD could be worth C$50–90m extra, and a takeover by a larger producer in the district would give 2–4x from C$49m.

    Bear case

    The assays show scattered, narrow zones, the resource estimate ends up small (under 0.5m oz), and the company has to raise C$8–10m in 2027 at C$0.07–0.10 (+30–40% shares). The price could fall 50–70%.
