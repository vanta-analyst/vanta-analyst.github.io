    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −22 of −35
    • Cash flow
      FCF has fallen from $7.59bn (FY23) to 5.62bn TTM while revenue rose 16%. Capex has quadrupled since FY21 ($0.67bn → 2.77bn), and receivables tied up $1.9bn over the last year. The FCF margin is 18% vs 28–29% in FY23–24, and FCF is only 61% of net profit.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Customer concentration
      Two customers accounted for 19% and 15% of revenue in FY25 (annual report). Normal for the industry, but a handful of chipmakers control the order book.
      sec.gov
      −4
    • Governance and export-control case
      A $252.5m settlement with BIS (Feb 2026) over illegal re-exports to SMIC in 2020–22, paid in Q2. The DOJ and SEC have closed their investigations without action, but the settlement requires internal audits of export controls.
      ir.appliedmaterials.com
      −3
    • Insider trading
      CEO Gary Dickerson sold 83,000 shares on 15–16/6 (about $590–600) and 78,321 shares for $55.6m on 29–30/6 ($700–736), about $105m in total near the top. No insider buying found; insiders own 0.26%.
      marketscreener.com
      −4
    • Macro and export controls
      China was 28% of sales in Q3 (35% a year earlier), and new US export rules cost about $0.6bn of revenue in FY26. The stock follows the AI capex cycle and has a beta of 1.6.
      tikr.com
      −5
    • Sentiment and analysts
      The stock has fallen about 34% from its $740 peak (30/6) after a sector-wide downturn and Q4 guidance that did not meet high expectations. Morgan Stanley has a Hold and cut its target from $642 to 563 on 28/9.
