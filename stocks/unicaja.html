Cracks−3 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • Governance and merger blocking
    Fundación Unicaja owns over 30% and would be diluted to 9.8% in a merger with Sabadell; sources around the foundation call it 'very difficult' to accept. Santa Lucía raised its stake to about 10% in May 2026 to deter Sabadell, and the CEO sees 'no catalysts'. A bloc of about 40% can block a bid, so minority shareholders are unlikely to get a takeover premium, and Citi estimates that the narrow valuation gap gives Sabadell only about a 1% EPS uplift (little room for a premium).

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B, P/TBV and P/E, and the payout is held against net profit. EPS and ROE for 2021 are distorted by badwill from the Liberbank merger, so history is measured from 2022. The share price (+53% over a year) is probably partly driven by merger speculation about Banco Sabadell; this has not been counted as fundamental, and the merger has been counted only once (governance in T3). Sabadell's approach to Unicaja's investors was in May 2025 and failed; Citi's analysis from Sep 2026 is an analyst calculation, not a bid. Consensus is 'Sell' with a median price target of EUR 3.00 (updated Aug–Sep 2026) below the price of 3.54, which is captured by formula points and not counted here.
