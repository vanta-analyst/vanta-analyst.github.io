    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 2 findings    −3 of −35
    • Governance and merger blocking
      Fundación Unicaja owns over 30% and would be diluted to 9.8% in a merger with Sabadell; sources around the foundation call it 'very difficult' to accept. Santa Lucía raised its stake to about 10% in May 2026 to deter Sabadell, and the CEO sees 'no catalysts'. A bloc of about 40% can block a bid, so minority shareholders are unlikely to get a takeover premium, and Citi estimates that the narrow valuation gap gives Sabadell only about a 1% EPS uplift (little room for a premium).
      merca2.es
      −2

      The bank intends to distribute about 95% of the 2026 result (and close to 100% in 2026–2027), so almost nothing is reinvested in a business whose lending grows only 3.3%; the dividend depends fully on earnings holding up.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B, P/TBV and P/E, and the payout is held against net profit. EPS and ROE for 2021 are distorted by badwill from the Liberbank merger, so history is measured from 2022. The share price (+53% over a year) is probably partly driven by merger speculation about Banco Sabadell; this has not been counted as fundamental, and the merger has been counted only once (governance in T3). Sabadell's approach to Unicaja's investors was in May 2025 and failed; Citi's analysis from Sep 2026 is an analyst calculation, not a bid. Consensus is 'Sell' with a median price target of EUR 3.00 (updated Aug–Sep 2026) below the price of 3.54, which is captured by formula points and not counted here. Bank tax (IMIC) for Unicaja has not been found and not counted. Insider ownership is shown as n/a at stockanalysis; Fundación Unicaja owns over 30%. Q3 figures 30 Oct 2026.
