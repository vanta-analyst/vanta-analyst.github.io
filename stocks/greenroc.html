    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Now called GreenRoc Strategic Materials Plc (formerly GreenRoc Mining, renamed July 2024), AIM: GROC, price 4.5 pence. Microcap (£12.6m) with 6 employees. Amitsoq only has a PEA (post-tax NPV $179m, capex $131m); the PFS starts in late 2026. The plan for an anode plant (FS: capex $321m) comes on top. The fiscal year ends 30/11, and the latest figures are the half-year to May 2026. Since then a further €2m has been drawn on the EIFO loan, so debt is now higher than the £1.76m in the figures. No analysts. The graphite price, China's dominance in graphite and anode material, and insider trading have not been examined. The share is not found in Nordnet's search.
