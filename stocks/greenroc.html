Now called GreenRoc Strategic Materials Plc (formerly GreenRoc Mining, renamed July 2024), AIM: GROC, price 4.5 pence. Microcap (£12.6m) with 6 employees. Amitsoq only has a PEA (post-tax NPV $179m, capex $131m); the PFS starts in late 2026. The plan for an anode plant (FS: capex $321m) comes on top. No analysts. The graphite price, China's dominance in graphite and anode material, and insider trading have not been examined. The share is not found in Nordnet's search.
