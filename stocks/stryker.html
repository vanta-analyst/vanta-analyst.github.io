Cracks−18 of −35

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers.

  • Execution (cyberattack and Inari supply)
    An Iran-linked cyberattack on 11/3 wiped about 80,000 devices and paralysed orders and production for weeks. The after-effects hit the Inari plant: supply drags growth down 70–80 bp in Q2 and Q3 and now also in Q4. The stock fell 8.8% on 8/9 when the CFO disclosed it.
    medtechdive.com
    −7
  • Customers are shifting
    Hip sales disappointed on weaker demand in Europe. The CFO says patients are postponing procedures for financial reasons (inflation and higher deductibles), and that Stryker lacks a competitive hip impactor product.
    medtechdive.com
    −3
  • Insider trading
    Director Ronda Stryker sold 310,000 shares ($96.8m) on 26/5 and 350,000 shares ($117.8m) on 18/8, about $215m in total. The general counsel, HR director and a group executive have also sold. There are no open-market purchases in 2026.
    secform4.com
    −4
  • Lawsuit and governance
    On 23/9 the law firm Hagens Berman began investigating whether Stryker disclosed enough about the ongoing problems in peripheral vascular. So far it is only an investigation, not a class action.
    globenewswire.com
    −2

    TD Cowen cut its target from $355 to 298 on 22/9 (Hold), citing the margin, and Canaccord cut from $400 to 385. Guidance of 8.3–9.3% organic growth is maintained, but several expect it will not be met.
