    Bull case

    Sales in the US keep doubling every year (from 9m to SEK 40–50m in 2028), and patch sales provide recurring revenue with an 84% gross margin. Broader reimbursement and break-even at around 60m in revenue can lift the value to SEK 600–800m (2.5–3x), but only after further dilution.

    Bear case

    Growth from the small base is flattening out, and a new issue at DKK 0.10–0.15 with 30–50% dilution is necessary within half a year. With an EV/Sales of 23 and continued losses, the share price could halve without anything going wrong in operations.
