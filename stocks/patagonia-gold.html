    Bull case

    Calcatreu quickly reaches stable production of several tens of thousands of ounces a year at gold above US$4,000, repays BRM's US$40m and reduces debt, and the Cap-Oeste underground project adds a second mine. EV/revenue of 19× could then fall to a normal level through growth, and the share price might double; 5x+ requires an acquisition.

    Bear case

    The scale-up is slow (1,960 oz in the first quarter), debt keeps growing, and Miguens converts loans to shares at a low price or takes the company private. With P/B 6.6, EV/revenue 19 × and only 60% ownership of Calcatreu, the price could fall 50–70%, as before 2025.

The upside requires a flawless scale-up; the downside is a dilutive debt conversion.
