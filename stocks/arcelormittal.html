    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Amsterdam listing (quote/ams/MT) is used; the company is also listed in Paris, Luxembourg, Madrid and New York. Accounts in USD, share price in EUR. Forward P/E and estimates are adjusted (non-GAAP); 2025 net profit (USD 3.2bn) was lifted by non-operating items, while the operating margin was only 2.7%. Analysts expect EPS +74% in 2027 – that is formula points, not a plus. Targets range from €48.59 to 81.67; Barclays cut to €55 on 25/9, while several other targets have not been updated after the fall from about €63 to 60. The status of the European DRI/decarbonisation projects and CO2 allowance costs have not been examined. The Mittal family's exact stake has not been verified (free float about 55% according to stockanalysis).
