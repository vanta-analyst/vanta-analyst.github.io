    Bull case

    The assays confirm Anatacau as a new major discovery 20 km from Rio Tinto's Galaxy project, the Mirage resource grows towards 80–100 Mt, and the lithium price keeps rising. Then the market cap could go from about C$69m to C$200–300m (3–4x) – the level at which James Bay peers with similar resources have been traded or acquired.

    Bear case

    The assays disappoint, the lithium price falls again, and the company has to raise C$5–8m at C$0.12–0.18 (+15–25% shares) every year. The price could then fall back towards the 2024 level (C$0.16) or lower; real zero risk is low in the short term because the resource can be sold or optioned.
