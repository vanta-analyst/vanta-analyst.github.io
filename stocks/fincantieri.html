Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Industrial: no category fits exactly – cruise ships are over half of revenue, naval vessels and underwater systems the rest; 'Defence, aerospace & space' was chosen as the nearest. Stockanalysis' gross debt (€2.5bn) includes construction loans; the company's own adjusted net debt was €756m at 30/6 (1.0x EBITDA) after the capital increase, and customer prepayments on cruise ships have not been deducted from cash – so no credit has been given for the balance sheet. The share count has in fact risen (capital increases of about €389m in 2024 and about €485m in February 2026 are visible in the cash flow); stockanalysis' −41% in 2024–25 is distorted by a reverse split. Forward P/E is based on adjusted EPS (2026E €0.55, company guidance €140–180m net result). State-owned CDP controls the majority (float about 41%) – mentioned, not scored. Analysts' price targets (median €18) were last updated in July, before the September drop. Q3 comes 12/11.
