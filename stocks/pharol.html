    Bull case

    The Rioforte estate distributes more than the 5.79% that Pharol has booked. At a 10–15% recovery (EUR 90–135m) plus the cash of EUR 42.5m, NAV becomes EUR 130–175m, i.e. 1.8–2.4x the current market cap, and Davidson Kempner is pushing for a distribution to shareholders. 3x+ requires over 20% recovery.

    Bear case

    Rioforte distributes nothing for many years or less than book value, and the new management spends the cash on poor investments in distressed companies. The stock falls back to P/B 0.4–0.5 (EUR 0.04–0.05), i.e. −45–55%. That it goes towards zero is unlikely with EUR 42.5m in cash.
