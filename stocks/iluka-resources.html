    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Today Iluka is a mineral sands business (zircon, rutile, synthetic rutile); rare earths only generate revenue once the Eneabba refinery is commissioned in 2027. The TTM loss is marked by about A$565m of impairments and inventory items after the suspension of Cataby and SR2. Group net debt (A$1.23bn) includes government loans for Eneabba, which are non-recourse; mineral sands net debt is A$273m. Only 2 analysts in stockanalysis' estimates. Not traded on Nordnet DK (ASX only); the OTC lines ILKAF/ILKAY are not in Nordnet's search API. Insider trading has not been examined.
