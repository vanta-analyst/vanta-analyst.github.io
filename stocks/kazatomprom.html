    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −8 of −30
    • Earnings development
      EPS fell 35% in 2025 and 11% TTM, and analysts expect -10% in 2026, even though the spot price rose 24% in H1. The realized price (USD 67.88/lb) lags spot (USD 85.98/lb) because part of the long-term contracts have fixed price components and price ceilings. Attributable EBITDA fell 12% in H1.
      thedeepdive.ca
      −3
    • Costs and margin
      Attributable C1 cost rose 37% to USD 24.48/lb and AISC 25% to USD 38.45/lb in H1, partly because the mineral extraction tax (MET) rose from 9% to 12.4%. Full-year guidance for C1 (25.50–27.00) and AISC (USD 39.00–40.50/lb) has been raised, and capex guidance has been lifted to KZT 435–450bn.
      quartr.com
      −3
    • FCF and capital allocation
      FCF fell to KZT 289bn TTM from 581bn in 2025 (FCF margin 15.5% vs 32%), while capex has risen from 108bn (2023) to KZT 227bn. The dividend of KZT 328bn TTM is larger than FCF. ROIC is still high (22%), so the deduction applies only to the direction.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −14 of −35
    • State control and jurisdiction
      The state fund Samruk-Kazyna owns 75%, and production is steered by the state's priorities: nominal 2026 production has been cut from 32,777 to 29,697 tU (100% basis) after 'value over volume'. The state can change taxes, subsoil licences and JV terms, and minority shareholders have limited influence.
      wnn.world-nuclear.org
      −5
    • Sulphuric acid and production risk
      Commissioning of the sulphuric acid plant TQZ is delayed 6–12 months to between Q3 2027 and Q1 2028. 2026 production (27,500–29,000 tU) depends on the acid supply, and 2027 guidance is outstanding.
      kalkine.co.uk
      −3
    • Geopolitics and customers
      Growing dependence on Chinese state buyers: a long contract with CNNC (November 2025) and a spot contract with SNURDC approved in October 2026, both with secret prices and volumes. Rosatom's Uranium One has sold its JV stakes (Zarechnoye, Khorasan-U, Kyzylkum) to Chinese state companies, and exports go via China and Russia, creating sanctions and transit risk.
      thedeepdive.ca
      −4
    • Currency and inventory
      The stronger tenge has forced a downgrade of revenue guidance to KZT 2,100–2,200bn, and currency losses pulled net income down 9% in H1. The company's own sales fell 13%, while inventory rose 23% to 8,245 tU.
      quartr.com
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The world's largest uranium producer (approx. 20% of global primary supply). Listed as GDR in London (LSE: KAP, USD, 1 GDR = 1 share), on AIX and on KASE (KZAP). Nordnet's search API gave no hits for 'Kazatomprom', 'KAP' or 'National Atomic' (11/10-2026), so the share is set to 'Not on Nordnet'. Stockanalysis' London page (quote/lon/KAP) only has statistics and no financials or forecast page, so the path is the main listing quote/kase/KZAP in KZT. Net income is after minority interests in the JVs (combined EBITDA margin 50%, net margin 28%). The share of the contract book with fixed prices and price caps is not quantified. The source for the TQZ delay (Kalkine) blocks bots, but is a public page. Insider trades have not been examined (state-owned). Only 3 analysts on the KASE listing.
