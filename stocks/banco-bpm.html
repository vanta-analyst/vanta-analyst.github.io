    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B, P/TBV and P/E, and the payout is held against net profit. The share price (EUR 16.39) is affected by the takeover game: MPS' exchange offer (1.567 MPS shares, EUR 16.73, no premium) and Crédit Agricole's 29.3%, and it is above analysts' average price target (15.88). The payout TTM (dividend 1.53 + buyback 0.54 = EUR 2.07bn) was above net profit (1.93bn), so no plus has been given for payout. The H1 result is adjusted (EUR 1.08bn); reported from continuing operations 1.1bn. The Anima acquisition did not receive Danish Compromise capital treatment; the capital effect has not been investigated in depth. Bank tax and government bonds have not been investigated.
