    Bull case

    La Parrilla (2,000 t/d combined capacity, historically 34m oz produced) ramps up to full operation in 2027 at silver above $80/oz, and the San Diego project provides an additional large resource. With annual production of 3-4m oz AgEq and low restart costs the company could earn C$60-100m in EBITDA, which could justify C$1-1.5bn - about 2-3x today's value.

    Bear case

    The start-up is dragging out (the repayment holiday has already been extended), cash lasts only a few months, and a new issue at a lower price becomes necessary. Without reserves, grades and recovery could disappoint, and a fall in the silver price could make the plant unprofitable. At P/B 7.8 there is plenty of room for the price to fall, even without the company going bankrupt.
