    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −12 of −35
    • Competition in HBM
      Samsung has passed Nvidia's HBM4 qualification, and TrendForce expects SK hynix's share of HBM bit production to fall from 59% in 2025 to 50% in 2026, while Samsung's rises from 20% to 28%. This pressures both share and prices in the segment that carries the premium.
      fintool.com
      −5
    • Cycle and sentiment
      The stock fell more than 15% in Seoul the day after the Nasdaq debut in July (largest drop in almost 20 years) on fears of a cycle peak and has been approx. 38% below its peak. Q2 operating profit (60.5tn KRW) missed consensus (64.1tn), and the ADR has fallen 12.7% in the past week.
      advisorperspectives.com
      −4
    • Customer concentration
      According to industry sources, SK hynix gets approx. two thirds (close to 70%) of Nvidia's HBM4 needs for Vera Rubin. The HBM business is therefore heavily dependent on one customer that is actively qualifying Samsung and Micron as alternatives.
      trendforce.com
      −3

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    SKHY is an ADR (10 ADRs = 1 ordinary share, ISIN US78392B2060), listed on Nasdaq on 10 Jul 2026 at USD 149 in an offering of about USD 26.5bn; the primary listing is Seoul (000660, KRW). Nordnet DK has it as 'SK hynix ADR' (tradable, slug sk-hynix-adr-skhy-xnas, checked via Nordnet's search on 11 Oct). T2 uses stockanalysis's history, which is based on the KRW primary listing 2021-2025. Nordnet shows P/B of 7.7 versus stockanalysis's 5.4 - a possible ADR premium relative to Seoul has not been investigated. TTM net income includes an investment gain of about KRW 73tn, so trailing P/E is too low. China exposure (DRAM fab in Wuxi, US export rules), insider trading and capex guidance for 2026-27 have not been investigated. The Q3 report is expected on 27 Oct (stockanalysis says 13 Nov).
