Bull case

The EIB and Faroese/Icelandic customers finance a first Dragon Farm of 10+ MW in 2027, and Minesto gets its first commercial order of, say, SEK 300–500m. The market could then price the company as a 'first mover' in tidal power – a market value of SEK 1–1.5bn (3–5x) requires the funding to come without heavy dilution, though.

Bear case

No order before the cash runs out around year-end; a new share issue far below SEK 1.15, with low take-up as in 2025, dilutes heavily. On top, the SEK 570m of capitalised development costs could be written down. The price could halve again, as after the 2025 share issue (SEK 0.74 at end-2025).
