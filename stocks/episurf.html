    Bull case

    The portfolio reaches 5.9–10bn SEK with 496m in rental income and 372m in pro forma net operating income. If the financing can be refinanced more cheaply and the stock trades up to 0.9–1.0x NAV, the price could rise 30–50%; more than 2x requires NAV per share to grow despite the share issues.

    Bear case

    Leverage and expensive loans in a selective credit market, more in-kind issues below NAV and conflicts of interest (SBB-like structure) press NAV per share. With a fall in property values of 10% and 42% loan-to-value, equity falls about 17%, and the stock could trade at 0.4–0.5x NAV.
