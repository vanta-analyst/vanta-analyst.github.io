    Bull case

    The new sales force gets sales back to +20–30% a year (over SEK 200m in 2027), and the gross margin comes back above 95%. The company then approaches break-even. At EV/Sales 4–5, as in 2023–2025, the stock is worth DKK 8–11 (2.5–3x), and an acquisition by a large orthopaedics group could give more.

    Bear case

    Sales stay flat around SEK 150m, key customers are lost, and the loss of about 50m a year eats the cash in 2–3 years. In the end it requires an issue. The downside is however partly covered by net cash (DKK 1.37 per share).
