    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −23 of −30
    • Margin
      The operating loss is SEK 27m on revenue of 73m TTM (−37%), and net result was −SEK 13.4m in Q2 2026 alone. There has been a loss every year since the IPO (−20 to −51m). Only Q3 2025 happened to be just above zero in EBIT.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has doubled (21.4m in Q2 vs 10.5m) with records four quarters in a row. In return, gross margin has fallen from 60-61% to 50-53% because the volume growth comes from cheaper sales via distributors. Growth is thus buying the margin down.
      inderes.dk
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      There have been repeated issues: 140m (2021), 151m (2024), 50m (2025: 25m directed to Alps Alpine at about SEK 4.56 plus a compensation issue of 25m) and 31.7m directed to Eiffel (Q1 2026). The share count is +21% in a year. The company capitalises about 34m a year in development, about 60% of 2025 sales.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Moat
      The pulsed coherent 60 GHz radar (A1/A121, now A212) is patented and has extremely low power consumption, which is a real niche. Infineon (XENSIV 60 GHz), Texas Instruments (mmWave) and Calterah are, however, far larger with their own fabs and channels, so a pricing moat is hard to defend.
      inderes.se
      −4
