    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' FCF (1.31bn) does not deduct leases (about 0.1bn) and purchases of spectrum/intangible assets (about 0.28bn); KPN's own FCF was about 0.95bn in 2025 and is guided above 0.95bn in 2026, so P/FCF is really about 15–16 and FCF yield about 6.5%, not 8.7%. Forward P/E and EPS forecasts are on adjusted EPS. Share count −1.6% in a year matches buybacks of 250m; stockanalysis' +19% in the quarter is a data artefact and is not used. Q3 2026 is hit by a one-off cost of 45m for a new collective agreement. Citi has cut its price target (4.90), and price targets are not used as findings. The threat from AI agents to the business model (debate in Sept. 2026) and credit rating are not examined separately. Telecom does not fit in the industry list; placed under Software, cloud & internet like Deutsche Telekom, Orange and Telenor. Next report 28 Oct. 2026.
