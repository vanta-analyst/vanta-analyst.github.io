    Bull case

    The recapitalisation is approved, debt falls to about USD 144m, the cost programme holds the Q4 level (operating margin about 6%), and China stabilises. At revenue of 400m and EV/revenue of 0.6–0.7 (historical), EV becomes 240–280m; minus about 90m in net debt, equity becomes 150–190m spread over about twice as many shares = about 2–3x the current price.

    Bear case

    Shareholders vote no, or the order decline continues (product orders −55% in Q4). The covenant pause expires in 2027 with debt of about USD 144–184m against EBITDA near zero; TCW (which is both lender and coming major shareholder) can take over the company in a restructuring. The stock can go toward zero.
