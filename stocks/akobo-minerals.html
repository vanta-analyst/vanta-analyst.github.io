    Bull case

    The shaft lifts production towards 50 kg/month at high grade, the gold price holds, and the refinancing lowers the interest rate from 22% without a new share issue. With EBITDA of NOK 300–400m a year and exploration that extends the resource beyond 69,000 oz, EV/EBITDA of 3–4 could give a market cap of NOK 1–1.5bn – about 3x today's.

    Bear case

    The grade falls, the shaft is delayed, and the refinancing requires a large share issue or conversion; a fall in the gold price or problems with exports/licences in Ethiopia could make the gold loan (9,444 oz) impossible to repay at maturity in July 2027. Shareholders have already been through two restructurings.
