    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is now Honeywell Technologies (pure automation) after the spin-off of Solstice (2025) and Aerospace (HONA, 29/6/2026) and a 1:2 reverse split the same day. Stockanalysis' TTM figures (revenue $38bn, P/E 8 and the gain of $6.6bn on deconsolidation of Quantinuum) still include Aerospace and are misleading. T2 is therefore based on the company's 2026 guidance (revenue $19.8–20.0bn, adjusted EPS $8.05–8.35, FCF about $2.0bn). The debt of $34bn is as of quarter-end before Aerospace was separated in the accounts, so net gearing after the spin-off is unresolved. The industry 'Machinery & automation' is the closest (building and process automation). Insider trading and asbestos liabilities have not been examined.
