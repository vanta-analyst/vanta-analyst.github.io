    Bull case

    The PEA shows a robust open pit at Taurus (e.g. NPV above C$300m at a high gold price), drilling converts inferred to indicated, and the Cassiar South restart (30–60,000 oz/year) provides early cash flow. At US$50–70 per oz – normal for developers with a PEA – 2.3 Moz corresponds to an EV of C$160–225m, i.e. 2–3x; more requires a takeover.

    Bear case

    The PEA disappoints (low grade of 0.95 g/t in northern BC gives high capex), the gold price falls, and the nearly empty cash balance forces an issue at C$0.25–0.35 (+20–30% shares). The share price could fall back to the 2025 level around C$0.25 (−45%).
