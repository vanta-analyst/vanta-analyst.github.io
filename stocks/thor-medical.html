    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −20 of −30
    • Margin
      Revenue was NOK 1.6m (TTM) vs an operating result of −NOK 83m and an FCF of −NOK 237m incl. capex. The company expects negative EBITDA throughout 2026.

    • Moat
      Thor extracts Th-228/Ra-224/Pb-212 from thorium and is one of few independent suppliers in Europe, but Orano Med produces Pb-212 itself, and Eckert & Ziegler, NorthStar and others are building capacity. The price of isotopes may come under pressure as capacity grows.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      AlphaOne has a capacity of about 21,000 patient-dose equivalents after three years, corresponding to about NOK 350m in revenue potential. The Th-228 agreements of about NOK 850m are disclosed without stating whether they are binding minimum-offtake agreements.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Three issues in two years: NOK 114m (2024), 86m at NOK 2.50 (June 2025) and 300m at NOK 4.80 (September 2026). The share count has grown approx. 30% in a year to 422.5m shares. Prices have been rising, though, and the money has gone into a production plant.

      −4

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −22 of −35
    • Dependence on customers' clinical programs
      The customers (Eckert & Ziegler, Minerva Imaging, Node Pharma and others) and their end customers are developing Pb-212 drugs that are still in the clinic. Demand for isotopes depends on these studies succeeding and being approved.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Execution of ramp-up
      AlphaOne has just entered commercial operation, and the first delivery was completed in August 2026. Inventory has been built up from NOK 3m to 42m in half a year. EBITDA break-even is only guided for 2027, and there is no documented full-scale operating history.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Dilution and overhang
      The price (NOK 4.39) is below the September issue price of NOK 4.80. A subsequent repair issue may follow, and the proceeds must fund new downstream plants in the US, which may require more capital.
      inderes.se
      −4
    • Commodities and regulation
      The company is raising money to 'expand and diversify access to feedstock', i.e. access to thorium raw material is a bottleneck. Handling of radioactive material requires permits, and deliveries are time-critical (Pb-212 has a half-life of 10.6 hours).
      inderes.se
      −4
    • Lease obligations
      Lease debt is about NOK 182m (167.5 long-term + 14.6 short-term) for AlphaOne, plus a loan of about NOK 60m. The fixed obligations run no matter how fast sales come.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
