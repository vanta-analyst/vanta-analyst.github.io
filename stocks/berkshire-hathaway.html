    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −8 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has stood still at USD 364-371bn in 2023-2025 (TTM 385bn), and analysts expect broadly unchanged EPS in 2027 (-0.4%); ROE has been only 9.8-14.7% in the last two years. The company is getting bigger via acquisitions, but not stronger.

      Underwriting result fell 19.5% to 7.2 bn USD in 2025, and operating profit in Q4 2025 fell 29.8%; GEICO has lost customers after broad price increases, and reinsurance revenue has fallen from 27.3 to 25.5 bn USD.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −9 of −35
    • Management change
      Greg Abel has taken over after Warren Buffett, and the 2026 letter was the first without Buffett; capital allocation has already changed (large acquisitions, Alphabet purchase), and the market has not yet seen Abel handle a crash or a large insurance loss.
      krdo.com
      −3
    • Litigation (PacifiCorp fires)
      As of 30 June 2026, PacifiCorp has provisioned USD 2.85bn for the wildfires and paid USD 2.28bn in settlements; juries have awarded USD 1.25bn to 201 plaintiffs, and Berkshire itself has estimated that the liability could run to tens of billions. An appeal ruling in April could reduce it.
      sec.gov
      −4
    • Portfolio risk
      Earnings swing sharply with the concentrated equity portfolio: GAAP earnings were USD −22.8bn in 2022 and +96bn in 2023, and equity – on which the P/B assessment is based – thus follows the stock market.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
