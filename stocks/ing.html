Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valued on P/B, P/TBV and P/E, and payouts are measured against net profit. Stockanalysis' net profit (8.7bn TTM, EPS 2.99, trailing P/E 10.6) is distorted and does not match ING's own net profit of 6.7bn over 12 months (EPS about 2.35, P/E about 13.5) or its own forecast table (2025 EPS 2.12); P/B is therefore used as the main multiple. The forward P/E (11.6–12.8) is on adjusted EPS. Share count −6.3% according to stockanalysis; buybacks of 2.8bn TTM equal only about 4%, the rest is earlier programmes.
