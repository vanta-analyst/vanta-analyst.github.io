    Bull case

    The uranium price and the NexGen share rise sharply as Rook I approaches production, and the holding discount (approx. 26%) narrows. If NexGen doubles and the discount disappears, the Mega share could approx. 2.5x; a 3–10x requires a uranium bubble like 2007.

    Bear case

    NexGen falls (budget overruns, new share issue, falling uranium price) – with gearing of C$21m, Mega falls more than NexGen. The share does not go to zero as long as the NexGen stake remains; realistic downside 50–70% in a uranium bear market.
