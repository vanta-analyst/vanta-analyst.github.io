    Bull case

    Johnson Camp proves the Nuton technology at full scale, column tests at Gunnison show good recovery, and a major or DOE funding secures the project. If the stock is re-rated from 0.25x to 0.6–0.8x NAV (US$1.96bn NPV), that corresponds to a market cap of approx. C$1.6–2.2bn before dilution – theoretically 3–5x, but after the necessary issues more like 2–3x. Analysts' targets are C$1.00–1.60.

    Bear case

    Column tests disappoint (low extraction/high acid consumption), the copper price falls, or Rio Tinto deprioritizes Nuton. The company would then have to raise money at a low price to fund drilling and PFS, and the stock could fall to C$0.15-0.25 (-50-70%), as before the copper theme.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Penny analysis (Under DKK 3 – Canada). The accounts are in USD (stockanalysis' statistics page in CAD; converted at US$1 ≈ C$1.418). The reported revenue and the positive result in Q2 2026 include US$9.8m of deferred 'demonstration revenue' from Nuton, and equity is negative – stockanalysis' P/B, EV/EBITDA and margins are therefore misleading. Revenue growth (about 5,800% TTM) is due to the start-up from almost zero. The precise terms of Nuton's repayment (waterfall) are known only from mentions, not from the agreement itself. The Gunnison project is based on a PEA (March 2026), not a PFS/FS. The largest owners after Greenstone's exit and insider trades otherwise have not been investigated. Only 2 analysts.
