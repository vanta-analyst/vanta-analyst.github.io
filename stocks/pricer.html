    Bull case

    The gross margin holds above 27%, and Sobeys, DeCA and the USA more than offset Carrefour, so revenue reaches SEK 2.6–2.8bn with an 8–10% EBIT margin (about SEK 230–280m). At EV/EBIT of 10 the market cap would be SEK 2.3–2.8bn, about 3.5–4x today – in line with the 2021 price.

    Bear case

    The Carrefour volumes disappear, VusionGroup and Hanshow squeeze prices, and the gross margin falls back towards 20%, so Pricer again earns around zero. The share price could then fall 30–40%, but the balance sheet makes a zero risk small.
