      Revenue is flat: EUR 29.7bn in each of 2023, 2024 and 2025 and 30.5bn TTM (−0.6%); analysts expect only +3.8% in 2026. EPS growth comes from costs, lower losses and buybacks, not from the top line.

      ROE 8.9% TTM (5.7–8.8% in 2021–2025) and cost/income 60.9% in Q2 2026 vs about 40–45% at the Nordic large banks. Even with record earnings the bank barely earns its cost of capital.

      Investment Bank accounts for 11.2 of 30.5bn EUR (37%) of revenue, and EPS fell 33% in 2024 (from 2.77 to 1.85) on litigation and capital market swings; earnings are more volatile than at retail-heavy banks.
