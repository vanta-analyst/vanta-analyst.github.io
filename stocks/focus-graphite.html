    Bull case

    Canada/USA prioritise non-Chinese graphite, and a strategic partner (carmaker, battery factory or defence) or government project financing covers a large part of the C$237m capex. If the market prices Lac Knife at 0.4–0.5x NPV (about C$115–140m) vs C$46m today, there is room for 2.5–3x – before further dilution.

    Bear case

    Graphite prices stay low because of Chinese overcapacity, no partner shows up, and the company must keep doing small placements at C$0.20–0.30 to pay general costs. The sharp price rise since 2024 (market cap from C$6m to C$46m) could roll back, so the price falls 50–60%.
