    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −24 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen three years in a row: SEK 7,686m (2022) → 7,576 → 7,223 → 6,094 (2025, -15.6%) and 5,758m TTM (-13.7%). Analysts expect -13.6% in 2026. Organic growth in Q2 2026 was -1%, while the key franchises grew 10%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Capital allocation
      The acquisition wave in 2020–2022 was financed with debt and issues (SEK 1,074m in 2021 and 2,001m in 2022 at far higher prices). Since then 7,057m (2024) and 2,326m (2025) have been written down, in total approx. 2.4 times the current market value.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Moat and future segment
      The portfolio consists of older free-to-play titles (Big Farm, Supremacy, Empire etc.) that depend on paid user acquisition (UA) and on Apple/Google as platforms. The margin improvement in Q2 came mainly from lower UA after the launch of Big Farm: Homestead, not from pricing power.
      stillfront.com
      −5
    • ROIC
      ROIC is 9.0% vs a WACC of 6.8%, but only because the capital base has been written down by over SEK 9bn. ROE is −35.9% TTM.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Margin
      Gross margin 84%, adjusted EBITDAC margin 29% in Q2 and EBIT margin 12.8% TTM (SEK 737m). That is solid for a gaming company, but the reported net income is −2,020m TTM because of write-downs.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
