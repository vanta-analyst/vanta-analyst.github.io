    Bull case

    The restructuring is adopted with an issue price close to the current price, the mine reaches design capacity (DFS: about USD 70m annual FCF), and the rutile/garnet quality is resolved. An asset valued at the 2022 DFS NPV8 of USD 453m spread across a greatly expanded share base can still give a price above today's – but only if the old shareholders are allowed to subscribe at the same price as the new ones.

    Bear case

    The creditors convert the debt and the new capital is subscribed at down to NOK 0.012; existing shareholders are diluted to almost zero. If the restructuring fails, bankruptcy or a sale of the mine follows, with shareholders last in line behind NOK 1.49bn of debt.
