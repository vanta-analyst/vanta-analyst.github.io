    Bull case

    FDA accepts one new phase 3 trial with LSAS as the endpoint, and a partner (e.g. in Asia, where AffaMed and Fuji have shown interest) funds it. The stock could then trade up toward the cash plus a pipeline value: 40–60 m USD market cap = 3–4x. Net cash at 30/6 alone was equal to about 0.70 USD per share, but it has been burned down since.

    Bear case

    FDA requires two new trials, no partner steps forward, and cash is down to 10–15 m in early 2027. Then comes a reverse split and a large issue or ATM sales at a low price, or a wind-down/reverse merger in which shareholders get little. A delisting to OTC makes the stock untradeable on Nordnet.
