    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B, P/TBV and P/E. TSB was sold to Santander (closed April 2026), so reported figures through Q1 2026 include TSB; the H1 result (EUR 971m) includes four months of TSB and one-off items, while the recurring result excluding TSB is 691m. Trailing P/E 20 is on reported EPS, forward 11.9 on adjusted. The dividend yield of 7.2% includes the extra dividend of EUR 0.50 from the TSB sale and will not be repeated. The share count −6.9% is not reconciled against the cash flow. The share price was affected by BBVA's failed bid (Oct 2025) and by merger rumours with Unicaja (Sep 2026); this has not been counted as fundamental.
