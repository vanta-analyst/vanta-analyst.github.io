    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −18 of −30
    • Margin
      The group's operating margin was 5.2% in H1 2026 (6.0% a year earlier) and the automotive division's only 3.0% (€814m); full-year guidance is about 5.5% vs about 7.4% in 2024. Gross margin has fallen from 20.8% (2023–24) to 17.9% TTM.
      investing.com
      −8
    • Capital allocation (Nissan)
      The alliance with Nissan cost an extraordinary write-down of EUR 9.3bn in 2025 and a net loss of EUR 10.9bn – the first loss in five years and more than today's market value.
      largus.fr
      −4
    • ROIC
      ROIC is 3.3% TTM and has been between 0.8% and 4.7% in 2021–25 – below the cost of capital throughout the period.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 9.5% in H1, but registrations fell 0.4% to 1.165m cars and Dacia sales fell 8%; growth comes from mix and less inventory clearance at dealers. Operating profit has fallen from €4.17bn (2024) to €3.49bn TTM despite higher revenue.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −10 of −35
    • Chinese competition and price pressure
      Renault guided in February to a clearly lower margin in 2026 due to price pressure from Chinese rivals (stock fell about 6%); in H1 management still cites price pressure in Europe, and Chinese brands are almost doubling sales to short-term rental.
      boursorama.com
      −5
    • Commodity inflation
      Renault expects a raw materials tailwind of about 600 m € in 2026, of which about 400 m € in H2, and calls it structural; the margin improvement in H2 will therefore be only 'modest'.
      investing.com
      −3
    • Regulation (Euro 6e-bis and CO2)
      The Euro 6e-bis requirements bring extra costs in the B+ segments that are hard to pass on to customers.

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis' net debt of €51bn, EV of €60bn, Debt/EBITDA 11 and ROIC include the loan book of Mobilize Financial Services (RCI Bank); the automotive part itself has net cash of €6.6bn (30/6-2026) vs. a market cap of about €7.2bn. In addition comes the remaining Nissan stake, whose value has not been calculated here. The 2025 net result (−€10.9bn) is mainly due to a €9.3bn impairment on Nissan. Analysts' EPS for 2026 (€6.59) looks high against H1 EPS of €2.39 and a 'modest' improvement in H2 – forward P/E 3.95 may be too low. stockanalysis shows +3.9% in share count over a year, which has not been investigated (possibly technical due to treasury shares/Nissan cross-ownership) and no deduction was made. The French state owns about 15%. Cyclical = no by the prompt's definition (car maker, not a commodity cycle), but earnings are very sensitive to the economic cycle.
