Cracks

  • Chinese competition and price pressure
    Renault guided in February to a clearly lower margin in 2026 due to price pressure from Chinese rivals (stock fell about 6%); in H1 management still cites price pressure in Europe, and Chinese brands are almost doubling sales to short-term rental.
    boursorama.com
    −5
  • Commodity inflation
    Renault expects a raw materials tailwind of about 600 m € in 2026, of which about 400 m € in H2, and calls it structural; the margin improvement in H2 will therefore be only 'modest'.

    −3

    The Euro 6e-bis requirements bring extra costs in the B+ segments that are hard to pass on to customers.

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
stockanalysis' net debt of €51bn, EV of €60bn, Debt/EBITDA 11 and ROIC include the loan book of Mobilize Financial Services (RCI Bank); the automotive part itself has net cash of €6.6bn (30/6-2026) vs. a market cap of about €7.2bn. In addition comes the remaining Nissan stake, whose value has not been calculated here. The 2025 net result (−€10.9bn) is mainly due to a €9.3bn impairment on Nissan. Analysts' EPS for 2026 (€6.59) looks high against H1 EPS of €2.39 and a 'modest' improvement in H2 – forward P/E 3.95 may be too low. stockanalysis shows +3.9% in share count over a year, which has not been investigated (possibly technical due to treasury shares/Nissan cross-ownership) and no deduction was made. The French state owns about 15%. Cyclical = no by the prompt's definition (car maker, not a commodity cycle), but earnings are very sensitive to the economic cycle. The short interest was at a record high in September 2026. Q3 revenue about 20/10; insider trading not investigated.
