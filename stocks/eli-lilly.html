    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −7 of −30
    • Concentration and moat
      Mounjaro (USD 32.5bn) and Zepbound (USD 16.9bn) are the same molecule, tirzepatide, and make up 62% of revenue of USD 79.7bn (TTM). Trulicity is already declining (7.4 → 4.2bn USD). The whole business rests on one molecule in one price-sensitive class.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Net debt has risen from USD 13.2bn (2022) to USD 45.2bn; net borrowed USD 15.2bn TTM. Acquisition wave in Q2 (Orna, Ajax, Centessa, Kelonia, three infectious disease companies, AtaiBeckley) with USD 10.5bn in cash acquisitions TTM and USD 2.8bn in IPR&D expenses in one quarter.
      biospace.com
      −2
    • Quality of growth (FCF conversion)
      FCF USD 18.2bn vs net result USD 26.7bn (68%); capex USD 9.9bn, and receivables and inventory have grown by USD 11.7bn in 2025. Growth is volume (+60%) with falling prices (-13%). On the other hand, the operating margin has risen from 29% (2021) to 50% and ROIC from 28% to 42%: really stronger.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −11 of −35
    • Price pressure and regulation
      The agreement with the Trump administration lowers Zepbound to an average of USD 346/month via TrumpRx (from 1,086) and the Medicare price of GLP-1 to USD 245. In Q2 prices fell 13% overall, 3% in the US and 36% internationally (Mounjaro on China's NRDL list).
      ajmc.com
      −5
    • Competition
      Semaglutide copies were launched in Canada, Brazil and China in 2026, Novo's Wegovy pill competes with Foundayo (orforglipron, only USD 98m in Q2 after the April launch), and many new obesity drugs are in phase 3. That puts pressure on prices internationally.
      americanpharmaceuticalreview.com
      −3
    • Lawsuits
      Lilly is sued together with Novo in the GLP-1 mass litigation (MDL 3094, gastroparesis etc., about 3,600 pending cases in May 2026) and in the NAION cases (vision loss, MDL 3163). Causation and preemption will be decided in 2026; bellwether trials at the earliest late 2026/2027.
      riker.com
      −2
    • Insider trading
      Lilly Endowment (about 10% owner) sold, among other things, 292,148 shares for USD 322m in January 2026 and USD 15.8m in May; no insider buying. Other insiders own only 0.17%.
      frenzycap.com
      −1
