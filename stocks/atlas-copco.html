    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −7 of −30
    • ROIC and earnings trend
      ROIC has fallen from 27.2% (2022) and 27.0% (2023) to 21.1% TTM; EPS fell 11% in 2025 to 5.42 and is 5.46 TTM vs 6.10 in 2024. Still far above a WACC of 9.8%, but returns have not become stronger.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has been flat since 2023 (SEK 172.7bn in 2023, 168.3bn in 2025, 169.9bn TTM); the new lift comes mainly from semiconductor customers in Vacuum Technique (+59% organic order intake in Q2), while Compressor Technique has gone from 78.3bn to 75.8bn since 2024.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Margin
      The operating margin stood still at 20.6% in Q2 despite 26% organic order growth; Vacuum Technique fell to about 21% because of capacity expansion, and Compressor Technique to 24.1% from 25%, partly diluted by acquisitions. The group margin was 22.1% in 2023.
      investing.com
      −2

    • Semiconductor dependence
      Vacuum Technique now makes up 25% of 12-month orders, the customers are a few concentrated OEMs, and some plants must more than double output in a short time. If semiconductor investment turns, Atlas Copco is left with overcapacity and extra inventory.
