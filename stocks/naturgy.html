Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Stockanalysis' −4.6% change in share count was checked against the cash flow: the partial buyback offer in 2025 (€2.58bn) was partly offset by a sale of treasury shares of €1.39bn in H2 2025, intended to increase free float – hence no plus for buybacks. Stockanalysis shows free float of 28%, but the company states over 46% after GIP's and CVC's exit in 2026. Reported and ordinary earnings are not separated in the sources used; impairments are small (−€29m TTM). The forward P/E history is lifted by 2021 (23.5), so the comparison weights 2023–2025. Analysts' 3-year EPS estimate (−1% per year) pre-dates the guidance raise in July and may be outdated. Gas supply contracts (Algeria/Medgaz, LNG) and credit rating have not been investigated in depth.
