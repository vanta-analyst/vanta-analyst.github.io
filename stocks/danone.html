    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Reported EPS (3.02 EUR TTM) is below recurring EPS (about 3.80 for 2025); forward P/E 14.6 is based on adjusted figures, trailing P/E 19.7 on reported. Organic growth (+3.5% in H1) is driven equally by price (+1.8%) and volume/mix (+1.7%). Huel (about 1 bn EUR), Made Group and The Akkermansia Company were bought in 2026, so reported growth gets help from acquisitions. Net debt/EBITDA of 2.0x is moderate; new bonds of 1.5 bn EUR were issued in September. Possible lawsuits following the cereulide recall and insider trading have not been investigated. The stock has fallen about 10% since August (RSI 27) without a single clear news item. Q3 revenue about 26 Oct 2026.
