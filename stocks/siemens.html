Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Industry set to 'Grid & electrical equipment' because Smart Infrastructure (electrification, buildings, data centres) is now the largest profit- and order-driving segment (Q3: EUR 6.4bn revenue at a 20.0% margin vs Digital Industries 4.9bn at 18.7%); factory automation would point to 'Machinery & automation'. stockanalysis' debt (EUR 49.9bn) and ROIC include Siemens Financial Services and consolidate Healthineers (about 67%); industrial net debt/EBITDA is only 0.6x. The Healthineers stake (about 30% of Healthineers) is to be distributed tax-free to shareholders after a vote at the AGM in February 2027; Siemens then keeps about 37% and aims to go below 20% over time, so group figures will change markedly. Q4 FY2026 comes 12/11. Insider trading has not been examined.
