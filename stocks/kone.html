    • The TKE acquisition
      KONE is buying TK Elevator at an EV of EUR 29.4bn: about EUR 5bn in cash and up to 270m new shares (about 50% dilution). Net debt becomes about EUR 13.5bn (about 5x reported and 4x adjusted operating profit) vs net cash today. The deal is expected to face a lengthy competition review in the EU, USA and UK and may require divestments in Europe (see elevatorworld.com).     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The figures apply to KONE alone. The acquisition of TK Elevator (EV EUR 29.4bn, completion no earlier than Q2 2027) fundamentally changes the company: up to 270m new shares and net debt of about EUR 13.5bn. Multiples and ROIC before the acquisition therefore say little about what the combined company will look like. The high ROIC is due to small equity and customer prepayments. Not examined: tariffs and demand for new construction in Europe and North America.
