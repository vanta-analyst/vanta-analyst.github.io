    Bull case

    The acquisition closes with mostly cash and few new shares: KALA gets USD 12.7m in revenue and positive cash flow for a market value of 8m. If the combined business is priced at 1–2x revenue (13–25m), and the listing survives, the stock could rise 2–3x. That requires the target company's unaudited figures to hold.

    Bear case

    The Nasdaq panel delists the share (no cure period), so it goes to OTC and cannot be traded on Nordnet. Alternatively the acquisition is paid for with a large share issue at a low price, so existing shareholders are diluted 50–80%. The history (−99.6% in a year) shows how fast it can go.
