    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Lenses, frames and optical retail chains do not fit the industry list exactly: placed under 'Medicine & medtech' (vision correction, myopia treatment). Reported operating margin (12.8%) and ROIC (5.3%) are depressed by amortisation of intangible assets and goodwill from the 2018 Essilor-Luxottica merger and the GrandVision acquisition; adjusted operating margin is 18.9% (H1 2026, constant exchange rates). Adjusted EPS (EUR 6.79 in 2025) is above reported (4.98); forward P/E 19.2 is based on adjusted figures. About 4 of the 8.7 percentage points of growth in Q2 came from AI glasses; the core business grew 'mid-single digit'. Tariffs gave a net +60 bp on gross margin in H1 due to refunds. Meta is both partner and shareholder. Delfin (the Del Vecchio family, just under a third of the shares) is mentioned under governance. Several price targets (e.g. Jefferies EUR 250) have not been updated after the price decline and are not a finding. Q3 revenue about 16 Oct 2026.
