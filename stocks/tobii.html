    Bull case

    Autosense is sold or gets a strong partner at a price that covers a large part of the net debt of about SEK 510m, and the core business (82% gross margin, about SEK 500–550m revenue) becomes cash-positive after the savings. With EV/sales of 1–1.5 and low debt, equity could be SEK 0.6–0.9bn, i.e. 2–3x today; 5x+ requires a sale at a clear overprice.

    Bear case

    No buyer at a good price, the cash runs out, and a rescue issue or debt conversion happens far below SEK 1 (e.g. a rights issue of SEK 300–500m at SEK 0.3–0.5). If the lenders break with the company, shareholders could lose almost everything.
