    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −6 of −30
    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen from 26.4% (2021) and 24.4% (2023) to 19.9% (TTM) after large debt-financed acquisitions (Shockwave, Intra-Cellular). Still far above WACC (5.1%), but the return on new capital is lower than on the old business.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      Net debt has risen from USD 3.1bn (2021) and 7.5bn (2023) to 28.3bn; 15.1bn (2024) and 17.5bn (2025) were spent on cash acquisitions. Growth has been partly bought.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Growth quality (MedTech)
      MedTech (36% of revenue) is growing more slowly than pharma; cardiovascular (electrophysiology and Abiomed) was a drag in Q2. The operating margin is 27.7% against 27.4% in 2021 - bigger, but not clearly stronger.
      nasdaq.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −14 of −35
    • Litigation (talc)
      More than 70,000 talc lawsuits consolidated in New Jersey; a record verdict of over USD 1.5bn in Baltimore (Dec. 2025), new verdicts in Philadelphia, Los Angeles (32m) and California (40m) in 2026. The bankruptcy solution (USD 9bn) was rejected in 2025, so the cases are now being tried individually.
      businesswire.com
      −6
    • Patent expiry (Stelara/Remicade)
      Stelara fell 55.7% in Q2 due to biosimilars and gave about 760 bp of headwind in Innovative Medicine; Remicade is also eroding. Excluding Stelara, J&J grew at a double-digit rate, but the next big patent expiry (Darzalex) is late in the decade.
      marketbeat.com
      −3
    • Regulation and drug pricing
      J&J has entered an MFN agreement with the Trump administration: discounts via TrumpRx and Medicaid in exchange for a time-limited tariff exemption and 55bn USD in US investments. The extent of the discounts has not been disclosed.
      fiercepharma.com
      −3
    • Execution (separation of orthopaedics)
      The plan to separate DePuy Synthes (Oct. 2025) has turned toward a possible sale for over USD 20bn (including talks with Apollo); no agreement or Form 10 as of August 2026, and the target has been pushed toward mid-2027.
      finviz.com
      −2
