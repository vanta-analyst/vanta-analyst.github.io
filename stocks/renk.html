    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The share has fallen about 53% over 52 weeks and a further 5% today (29/9) to about €37.8 after JPMorgan's Q3 warning; the defence rally has thus already partly reversed, and the multiples are calculated on the low price. Only about 2 years of trading history (listed February 2024), so T2 rests on a short history. Analyst price targets (median €64) have not been updated after the fall and will give a very large upside in the formula – that is an optimistic source, not a finding. Major shareholder sales and insider trades have not been examined.
