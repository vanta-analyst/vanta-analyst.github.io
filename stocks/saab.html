    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −15 of −35
    • Gripen exports
      A string of lost or threatened Gripen campaigns: India chose the Rafale (Feb 2026), Peru is expected to choose the F-16, Canada is leaning towards other aircraft, and Ukraine negotiated for used Mirage 2000s from Qatar; Bank of America downgraded to neutral (March 2026). The Ukraine contract for 16 Gripen E (July) is funded via the Swedish state.
      dagensps.se
      −4
    • Execution (submarines)
      The A26 submarines for Sweden, contracted in 2015 for delivery in 2022–23, have been delayed again to 2031–2035, and the price must be renegotiated. At the same time a SEK 47bn submarine order for Poland was booked in Q2 – a large execution risk for Kockums.
      nyteknik.se
      −4
    • Macro and peace risk
      The case and the multiple rest on NATO rearmament and the war in Ukraine; the sector falls together on peace signals (e.g. −3% on 24/6/2026). The stock is only +7% over 52 weeks despite record orders.
      placera.se
      −4
    • Customer concentration
      The customers are states, and the Swedish state (FMV) is the largest customer and intermediary for, among others, the SEK 24.6bn Ukraine order. The states control orders, timing and prepayments; the share per customer is not disclosed.
      saab.com
      −3
