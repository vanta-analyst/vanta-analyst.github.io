    A string of lost or threatened Gripen campaigns: India chose the Rafale (Feb 2026), Peru is expected to choose the F-16, Canada is leaning towards other aircraft, and Ukraine negotiated for used Mirage 2000s from Qatar; Bank of America downgraded to neutral (March 2026). The Ukraine contract for 16 Gripen E (July) is funded via the Swedish state.

    The A26 submarines for Sweden, contracted in 2015 for delivery in 2022–23, have been delayed again to 2031–2035, and the price must be renegotiated. At the same time a SEK 47bn submarine order for Poland was booked in Q2 – a large execution risk for Kockums.
