    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 2 findings    −8 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      US generics of Entresto, Promacta and Tasigna are eating growth: in Q2 2026 Entresto fell 50% to USD 1.18bn, and in Q1 Promacta fell 66% and Tasigna 59%. 2026 guidance is low-single-digit sales growth and core operating income falling low single digits; consensus expects core EPS to fall 1.5% in 2026. Kisqali (+43%) and Pluvicto carry growth.
      marketbeat.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation and ROIC
      The acquisition of Avidity for about USD 12bn (closed 27/2/2026, bridge loan refinanced with bonds) lifted net debt from USD 23.9bn to USD 41.4bn in half a year, and ROIC has fallen from 24.5% (2025) to 18.8% TTM. Avidity is an RNA platform for rare muscle diseases with no sales today, so the return is several years away.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −13 of −35
    • Patent expiry
      Entresto loses exclusivity in Europe from November 2026 after the US in 2025, and the total generics gap is estimated at about USD 4bn in 2026. Cosentyx, Kesimpta and Kisqali, which are to carry growth, lose patent protection in the early 2030s.
      xtb.com
      −5
    • Price pressure and regulation (MFN and tariffs)
      The 19/12/2025 agreement with the US government gives three years' exemption from Section 232 tariffs in return for MFN pricing and investment commitments in the US. Management itself says the MFN effect will come through over the next 18 months.
      globaltradealert.org
      −3
    • Pipeline execution
      The phase III study Lp(a)HORIZON with pelacarsen (8,323 patients) missed the primary endpoint of fewer cardiovascular events (4/9/2026), even though Lp(a) fell as expected. It was one of the big cardiovascular hopes to replace Entresto.
      hcplive.com
      −4
    • Debt and liquidity
      Debt/EBITDA has risen from 1.5 to 2.1 and net debt/EBITDA from 1.0 to 1.8; current ratio is 0.86. Interest cover is 13x, so it is not critical, but the balance sheet is clearly tighter after Avidity (the acquisition itself is penalised in T1).
      stockanalysis.com
      −1
