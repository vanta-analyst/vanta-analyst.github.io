    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −25 of −30
    • Margin
      The gross margin has fallen every year: 55% (2022), 45%, 30%, 10% (2025) and −19% TTM; Q2 gave a gross loss of $1.2m on $5.6m revenue. The operating result is −$68.8m TTM (margin −700%), and operating costs rose 114% in Q2. The loss counts here and not again under ROIC.
      stockanalysis.com
      −12
    • Quality of growth
      Organic revenue was only $0.68m in 2025; the jump to $9.8m TTM comes from the acquisitions of Luminar Semiconductor, NuCrypt and NHanced (which alone is expected to contribute $7–16m in 2026). It is photonics and chip production, not quantum computers, and 70–80% comes from government contracts, mostly as a subcontractor.
      quantumcomputingreport.com
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      About $180m in cash spent on three acquisitions in H1 2026; goodwill rose from $55.6m to $181.5m, and NHanced ($73.1m) has an earn-out of up to $72m more. The strategy is drifting from 'quantum computing' towards contract manufacturing of photonics.
      sec.gov
      −4
    • Technology and moat
      The company's 'entropy quantum computing' (Dirac-3) is a photonic optimisation machine without gate-based or logical qubits and without a roadmap towards fault tolerance; the class action alleges precisely that the capabilities are exaggerated.
      ktmc.com
      −3
