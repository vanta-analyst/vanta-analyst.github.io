Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The SCAR programme has been stopped (impairment of about $240m on the Space unit), and the BlueHalo acquisition diluted shareholders by +52%. FY27 guidance gives only $10–27m of GAAP profit on $2.1–2.2bn of revenue; adjusted EPS of $3.02–3.34 excludes, among other things, amortisation of acquired intangibles. There is a class action over SCAR. Analysts cut price targets after Q1 (BofA $225→185, Goldman $326→285). Competition from new drone makers has not been examined.
