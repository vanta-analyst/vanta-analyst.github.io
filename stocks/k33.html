    Bull case

    A new crypto bull market lifts bitcoin far above USD 100,000 and brokerage volume, while the MiCA passport gives K33 institutional customers in the Nordics and Germany. If gross profit again reaches SEK 40-60m, and the bitcoin holding rises in value, the market cap can reach SEK 1bn or more, about 3x today.

    Bear case

    Bitcoin keeps falling, brokerage volume and margins fall further, and cash of SEK 3m forces issues at 1–2 öre or a sale of bitcoin at the bottom. The stock has already fallen from SEK 0.16 (2021) to SEK 0.02 with annual dilution of 7–21%.
