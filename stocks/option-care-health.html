    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    CD&R and McKesson agreed on 6 Oct 2026 to buy the company for $32.05 in cash per share (about $5.8bn including debt, 37% premium). The stock trades at $31.20, so the price is effectively locked to the takeover price; completion is expected in H1 2027 and requires shareholder and regulatory approval, after which the stock will be delisted. Guidance has been withdrawn, and no conference call will be held for Q3 (4 Nov). The score applies to the business; the practical investment is a merger arbitrage spread of about 2.7%. Revised guidance from April (amounts) was only found via secondary sources.
