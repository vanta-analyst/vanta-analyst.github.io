    Bull case

    The updated PEA at $60–80/oz silver shows an NPV of over US$1bn, and a major producer (e.g. a mid-sized silver producer) buys the project or comes in as a partner. At 0.5x NPV that equates to a value of C$650–700m – about 3x today's market cap.

    Bear case

    The silver price falls, permits drag on in Mexico, and the company must continue with small issues every six months. Without a partner, capex of US$388m cannot be financed, and the stock trades back towards P/B 1.5–2 (about CAD 0.20–0.25), i.e. half of the price.
