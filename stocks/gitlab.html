    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −11 of −35
    • AI agents versus the seat model
      Core revenue is Premium/Ultimate per developer user, so AI coding agents can reduce the number of seats, and GitHub Copilot (Microsoft) is a strong competitor. GitLab itself calls seat pricing for AI a divide between 'haves' and 'have-nots' and is introducing credits, but that revenue is not expected to take off until 2028.
      about.gitlab.com
      −5
    • Governance (voting control and litigation)
      Co-founder and chairman Sytse Sijbrandij holds B shares with more votes. In April 2026 he and the board were sued for using a $400m buyback programme to push his voting share above 50% and postpone the expiry of the B shares, after he had sold shares for about $64m.
      news.bloomberglaw.com
      −4

      In June 2026, 14% of employees (approx. 350) were cut, and the company is withdrawing from 22 countries (approx. 37% of its geographic footprint). The cost is 30–35m $, and it creates execution risk in sales.
