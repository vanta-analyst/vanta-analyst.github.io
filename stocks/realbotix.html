    Bull case

    The Onconetix deal closes, and the merged Nasdaq company trades at a humanoid premium; the new robot line sells e.g. 300 units a year (approx. USD 6–10m revenue). If the market values the Nasdaq company at USD 150–250m, Realbotix Corp's 75–90% is worth USD 110–225m – approx. 2.5–5x today's approx. USD 44m (before holding discount and dilution).

    Bear case

    The deal drags on or falls through, Onconetix must reverse-split again, and robot sales keep sitting at a couple of hundred thousand USD a quarter. Cash is used up during 2027, and an issue at C$0.10–0.15 dilutes heavily; with negative gross margin and EV/Sales of 49 the price could fall 70–90%.
