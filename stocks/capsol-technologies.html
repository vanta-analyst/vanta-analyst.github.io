    Bull case

    Holcim/Dyckerhoff, E.ON or a US utility moves to a full-scale plant under Capsol's licence, so revenue becomes recurring licence and service income of NOK 150–300m. At 3–4x sales the market value could reach NOK 0.6–1bn, about 2–3x today.

    Bear case

    No large orders, revenue keeps falling, and the cash has to be topped up with a share issue far below NOK 4.3. The DNB facility is scaled down from 2027, and carbon capture remains dependent on political support – the price could halve.
