    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Enapter is NOT in a formal restructuring (no insolvency or StaRUG), but is doing an out-of-court restructuring: the bond debt of about EUR 26m is being settled by transferring the Saerbeck campus to the creditor Patrimonium against EUR 5m in cash and a Besserungsschein of up to EUR 2m, and the 2026 guidance has been withdrawn. The normal penny-stock calibration was used, but the financing risk is weighted heavily in T3. stockanalysis only has a Frankfurt page (quote/fra/H2O); the Xetra path (quote/etr/H2O) returns 404. The share count (32.07m) is from stockanalysis and appears to include part of the conversion of the forced convertible from November 2025; the remaining part and the planned issue of EUR 10-12m are not included. Market cap is calculated at EUR 30.5m (about DKK 227m), slightly below Nordnet's DKK 236m. The strategic investor is not named, and the issue requires regulatory approval. Quarterly burn is an estimate based on H1 EBITDA, capex and interest, because the H1 cash flow statement has not been reviewed line by line. Insider trades and short interest are not verified.
