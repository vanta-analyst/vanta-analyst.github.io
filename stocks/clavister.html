    Bull case

    European rearmament brings more defense contracts, the order book is converted, and revenue reaches SEK 600-700m in 2029 with a 20% operating margin (120-140m EBIT). At P/E 25 that corresponds to SEK 3-3.5bn, about 2x today; a 3x also requires a takeover bid from a defense group.

    Bear case

    Defense deliveries are uneven, ARR grows by only 4–5%, and the margin falls back toward zero. If the multiple falls from P/S 6 to the historical 2–3, the share price falls 50–65% without the company being in danger.
