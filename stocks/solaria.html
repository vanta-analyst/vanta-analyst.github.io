Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Stockanalysis' revenue (€371m TTM) includes other operating income; the company's own operating revenue was €167.2m and other income €56.1m in H1 2026. Production revenue of about €68m (≈€39/MWh) is own calculation (operating revenue minus infrastructure) and may include other items. It has not been clarified whether the 'Powered Land' revenue from data centres is one-off sales of grid-connected land or recurring rental income – hence a deduction for uncertain growth quality, not for one-off items. The share count (+4.2%) was checked against the cash flow: share issuance/sale of treasury shares of €358m vs. buybacks of €125m TTM. The company's leverage (3.9x net debt/EBITDA) is lower than stockanalysis' 4.1–4.7x because the company's EBITDA includes other income. The price target (€22.39, +39%) is probably not fully updated after the price fall of over 40% from the April peak – not used as a finding. Insider trading (the Díaz-Tejeiro family) and the Spanish captured solar price for Solaria specifically have not been investigated. New Investor Day in November 2026.
