    Bull case

    France opens a permanent program in 2027, and ASTRUM 10-10 is approved while Germany normalizes. If sales grow to 30–40 m kr. with 30–35% gross margin and 10% EBITDA margin, the value could become 100–150 m kr. (3–4x).

    Bear case

    The French process drags on, Germany remains closed, and Denmark flattens out. Sales then stagnate around DKK 12–15m with no profit, and an issue under the large authorisations below market price could halve the share price.
