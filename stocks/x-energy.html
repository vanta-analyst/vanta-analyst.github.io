    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Newly listed on 24/4/2026 at $23, so there is no own multiple history. T2 rests on the IPO price and peers (NuScale, Oklo, Nano Nuclear, Centrus). Revenue is almost only DOE reimbursement (88% in Q2), and the first reactor sale is at the earliest in the early 2030s. Industry: 'Utilities & power' was chosen over 'Commodities & uranium' because the business is reactor design and licensing for power and steam customers (Dow, Amazon/Energy Northwest, Centrica). TRISO-X makes fuel elements and is not exposed to the uranium price, and there is no production yet. As a reactor supplier the company in reality sits between equipment and utilities. The TTM net loss (−$449m) is distorted by $114m of fair value losses on warrants in H1; operating profit and adjusted EBITDA are used. Up-C structure: 118.9m class B shares (one vote each, without economic rights) can be exchanged for class A. The $5.86bn market value includes both classes. No insider selling has been found after the lock-up expiry, but Form 4 coverage has not been fully reviewed.
