    Bull case

    Strong phase 2a data in MDS/AML in 2027 or promising early data in combination with daraxonrasib lead to a licence deal for nadunolimab, and Otsuka triggers a CAN10 milestone. EV is only about SEK 0.12bn, so a deal with USD 30–50m upfront could 2–3x the share on its own; 5–10x requires a large deal or convincing randomised data.

    Bear case

    The MDS effect does not hold in phase 2a, the combination study gives no clear added value, and no partner materialises. Cash is burned at about SEK 130m a year, and a new share issue is required in 2028 – the latest was subscribed only 21%. The share price could fall below SEK 1 (−40–60%).
