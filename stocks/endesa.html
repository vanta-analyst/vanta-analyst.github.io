Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
Endesa is about 70% owned by Enel (free float about 29%), so price formation and dividend policy are influenced by the parent. Reported and ordinary net income are close (1,470 vs 1,480 €m in H1), so adjusted vs reported EPS is not an issue here. Stockanalysis' −2.0% in share count was checked against the cash flow: buybacks of €876m TTM, 35m shares bought and 17m cancelled as of June. The share price is up 57% in 52 weeks, and the analyst consensus is 'Sell' with a median price target of €36.60 – some price targets may be outdated after the rally. Spanish grid regulation (CNMC 6.58% for 2026–2031) did not trigger a deduction because Endesa's distribution EBITDA rose 24% and management cites a new royal decree on a higher investment cap as a tailwind. The April 2025 blackout, credit rating and insider trading have not been investigated.
