    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Milan listing (TEN); reports in USD, stockanalysis converts market value and multiples to EUR. Cyclical = yes: demand for pipes (OCTG) follows oil and gas drilling. Industry set to 'Energy: oil & gas' (steel pipes and services for the oil and gas industry). The share price is up 60% over 52 weeks while EPS is largely unchanged, driven among other things by the Hormuz conflict, higher rig activity in the US and 50% US steel tariffs (Section 232); a large part of the multiple expansion may be a geopolitical premium. Stockanalysis' net cash ($2.3bn) excludes long-term investments; the company reports net cash of $3.6bn. The criminal 'Cuadernos' case in Argentina against chairman Paolo Rocca has not been examined.
