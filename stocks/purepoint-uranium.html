    Bull case

    The assay results from Nova confirm high-grade uranium over several metres, and the winter drilling shows the discovery growing along the 1 km long contact. With a market cap of only C$23m, a real Athabasca discovery could lift the company to C$70–150m, i.e. 3–6x – but Purepoint owns only 50% of Nova.

    Bear case

    The assays disappoint, and C$3–5m has to be raised at C$0.15–0.20 (+20–30% shares) to keep the company running. With repeated small issues and very low liquidity, the price could halve again.
