      Operating margin 16.1% (TTM) and 15.9% in 2025 vs 31.4% in 2023 and 23.9% in 2022; 2026 guidance corresponds to about 6% profit before tax of revenue. H1 2026 gave a loss before tax of DKK 54m.

    • ROIC
      ROIC 9.8% (TTM), 8.5% in 2025 and 9.0% in 2024 vs 17.0% in 2023; returns reach a high level only in years with large transfers or the Champions League.

      Revenue growth in H1 2026 (+DKK 134.6m) comes largely from sales of holiday homes in Lalandia; external costs rose DKK 58.8m, mainly construction costs for the homes sold. One-off-style project sales with a low margin – bigger, not stronger.
