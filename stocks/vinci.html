New concessions (A154/A120, India) replace only a fraction.

Cracks

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings−9 of −35
  • Higher infrastructure tax
    The government wants to raise the levy on long transport infrastructure from 4.6% to up to 12.2% of revenue above 120 m € (proceeds 0.6 → 1.4 bn €); RBC estimates 3–4% lower EPS for Vinci. The stock fell 2.3% to 108.50 € near the 52-week low (28–29 Sept 2026).
    tradingsat.com
    −4
  • French corporate tax surcharge
    The special tax surcharge for large French companies costs over EUR 300m in H1 and over EUR 400m in 2026; effective tax rate 33.7% TTM.
    investing.com
    −2
  • Traffic and lowered traffic expectations
    Passenger car traffic on French motorways fell 3.7% in H1 (fuel prices, heatwaves); the airport traffic outlook has been lowered to 'stable' and motorway traffic is now expected to fall slightly; Gatwick and Kansai are weak.
    investing.com
    −2
  • Debt
    Net debt (including leases) has risen from €18.6bn (2023) to €25.8bn (June 2026), and net debt/EBITDA from 1.7 to 2.0.
    stockanalysis.com
    −1

Caveats
