    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −8 of −30
    • Future segment (concessions expiring)
      The French motorway concessions – Vinci Autoroutes has an EBIT margin of 75.5% and accounts for about 35% of the group's EBITDA – expire in 2032 (Escota), 2034 (Cofiroute) and 2036 (ASF), after which about 90% of the network reverts to the state. New concessions (A154/A120, India) replace only a fraction.
      largus.fr
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Organic growth only 1.3% in H1 2026, and revenue growth has slowed from 11.6% (2023) to 1.1% TTM; Vinci Construction is flat (31.5 → 32.0 bn €), and part of the growth in Energies comes from 12 acquisitions in H1.
      investing.com
      −2
    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen from 11.5% (2023) to 10.3% TTM, despite rising margins.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1
    • Capital allocation
      Management acknowledges that acquisition multiples are high, and is buying, among others, the German ERP/IT company All for One – some distance from the core business.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −9 of −35
    • Higher infrastructure tax
      The government wants to raise the levy on long transport infrastructure from 4.6% to up to 12.2% of revenue above 120 m € (proceeds 0.6 → 1.4 bn €); RBC estimates 3–4% lower EPS for Vinci. The stock fell 2.3% to 108.50 € near the 52-week low (28–29 Sept 2026).
      tradingsat.com
      −4
    • French corporate tax surcharge
      The special tax surcharge for large French companies costs over EUR 300m in H1 and over EUR 400m in 2026; effective tax rate 33.7% TTM.
      investing.com
      −2
    • Traffic and lowered traffic expectations
      Passenger car traffic on French motorways fell 3.7% in H1 (fuel prices, heatwaves); the airport traffic outlook has been lowered to 'stable' and motorway traffic is now expected to fall slightly; Gatwick and Kansai are weak.
      investing.com
      −2
    • Debt
      Net debt (including leases) has risen from €18.6bn (2023) to €25.8bn (June 2026), and net debt/EBITDA from 1.7 to 2.0.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
