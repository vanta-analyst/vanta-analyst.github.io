    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 2 findings    −5 of −35
    • Westinghouse
      The share of Westinghouse's adjusted EBITDA fell from CAD 352m to 163m in Q2, and Q2 2025 was lifted by a one-off contribution of about USD 170m from the Czech reactor project. The large AP1000 projects in the US and Europe only enter construction around 2028–30.
      cameco.com
      −3
    • Operations
      Spring floods hit supply roads in northern Saskatchewan, and there were unplanned shutdowns at Key Lake and McArthur River in Q2. Cigar Lake stood still for about two weeks in July because of problems with the acid plant at Orano's McClean Lake mill. Production guidance is maintained, but with less margin for error.
