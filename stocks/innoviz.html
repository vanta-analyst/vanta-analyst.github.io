    Bull case

    The Mobileye robotaxi task (approx. 150,000 lidars) and 2–3 new programmes in 2026 materialise, VW ID. Buzz goes into series production, and the Perciz defence orders grow. If revenue reaches USD 120–150m in 2027–28 with positive gross margin, EV/revenue of 2–3 would give an EV of USD 250–450m vs approx. 92m today = 3–4x.

    Bear case

    Robotaxi and VW volumes are delayed, NRE dries up, and gross margin stays at zero. With a burn of about USD 60m a year, money must be raised again in 2027 after a reverse split, perhaps at half the price; in the worst case the stock is delisted to OTC. The stock may fall a further 50–80%, but the net cash and the customers make zero less likely on a 12-month horizon.
