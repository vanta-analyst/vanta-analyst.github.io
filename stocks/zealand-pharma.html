    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin and core operations
      Revenue of DKK 4.6bn (TTM) is almost entirely the DKK 4.5bn Roche milestone in Q2. Without it the company has no product revenue, and operating costs of DKK 2.7–3.3bn in 2026 make the core business clearly loss-making; operating cash flow TTM −DKK 1.75bn.
      investing.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      No recurring growth: revenue was DKK 55–340m in 2021–2024, jumped to 9.2bn in 2025 on the Roche deal and is expected −48% in 2026 with EPS of 25 vs 90. Results follow milestones, not sales.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat and future segment
      The lead asset petrelintide delivered 10.7% weight loss after 42 weeks (phase 2), 1–2 points below expectations and called 'undifferentiated'. The amylin field is crowded with Novo, Lilly and others, so an obesity position is not secured despite good tolerability.
      biopharmadive.com
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      Buybacks of up to DKK 1.3bn (6.7% own shares in week 39) are paid from cash, while FCF is −DKK 1.8bn (TTM); at the same time future rusfertide royalties have been sold to Royalty Pharma for USD 100m.
      globenewswire.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −14 of −35
    • Partner risk (survodutide)
      In Boehringer's phase 3 of survodutide, 19% dropped out because of gastrointestinal side effects vs 2.9% on placebo; the stock fell 22.7% in one day in June and was down almost 50% year to date. The royalty value from survodutide is therefore uncertain.
      cnbc.com
      −5
    • Binary pipeline dependence
      The share price hangs on petrelintide, and the next big trigger is the ZUPREME-Combo data (petrelintide + Roche's CT-388) in 2027, which is also called the biggest risk. The stock has fallen 38% over 52 weeks on two data setbacks.
      ugebrev.dk
      −4
    • Regulatory execution
      Dasiglucagon (congenital hyperinsulinism) is still awaiting a new US filing, and glepaglutide is running another phase 3 trial (EASE-5); none of the late-stage assets outside obesity has reached the market.
      investing.com
      −3
    • Macro and regulation
      Trump's order for up to 80% lower drug prices and the price pressure on GLP-1s in the US hit future earnings from obesity drugs before Zealand has a product on the market.
      home.saxo
      −2
