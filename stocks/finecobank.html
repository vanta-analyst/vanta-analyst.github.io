Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Bank/platform: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (customer deposits); the payout is held against net profit, valuation on P/E and P/B. CET1, the share of government bonds in the portfolio and the interest rate sensitivity of margine finanziario (about half of revenue) have not been investigated separately; the half-year PDF could not be read.
