    • Competition and fee pressure
      Fineco faces direct competition in Italy from Trade Republic, flatexDEGIRO, Revolut and Interactive Brokers, and management itself cites the risk of pressure on the AUM margin; growth is partly to come from a new pan-European platform launching in Germany only in 2027, where Fineco is unknown.

    • Bank tax
      The 2026 Finance Act raised IRAP for banks by 2 percentage points (2026–2028); Fineco's net income therefore fell 1.2% in Q1 2026 despite higher operating profit.
