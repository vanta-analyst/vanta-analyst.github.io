    Bull case

    The development agreement with the large customer is signed, the Corning collaboration leads to qualification in wearables/medtech, and the relocation cuts burn by a third. If the market starts to price Ensurge as a future supplier of solid-state microbatteries, the market cap could go from NOK 0.55bn to NOK 2–3bn (4–5x), but only without an expensive rescue share issue first.

    Bear case

    The cash runs out around October 2026; a share issue far below NOK 1.00 triggers the ratchet in the convertible loans and dilutes massively. Without a customer deal the share could fall 50–80%, and if financing fails the going-concern risk is real.
