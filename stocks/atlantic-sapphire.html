    Bull case

    There is no real bull case for the minority: the best outcome is a squeeze-out at NOK 0.80, about 4% above the price of NOK 0.769. A 3–10x is ruled out, because Coral HoldCo owns 99% and the company is being delisted.

    Bear case

    The compulsory redemption happens later or at a lower price than NOK 0.80 (e.g. if the deadline for the offer price is exceeded and the price is set by estimate), or the share remains as an unlisted minority stake without liquidity. Fundamentally the equity is already lost.
