    Bull case

    Profitability in Q4 2026 is reached, monitor sales in the USA turn, and the Philips deal gives OEM revenue from 2027–28. If revenue grows to 200m SEK with 70% gross margin and positive EBITDA, EV/Sales 5–6 could give a price of 6–8 kr (2–3x). An acquisition by Philips or another monitor maker could give more.

    Bear case

    Sales in the US stay flat, the profitability target is postponed, and the credit facility is used up during 2027. Then comes a directed issue at DKK 1.5–2 with 30–50% dilution, and the Philips agreement gives no revenue for now.

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin
      The gross margin has risen to 65.7% (61.8%), but the operating margin is −110% TTM: an operating loss of SEK 113m on revenue of 103m. EBITDA in Q2 was −17.3m on sales of 24.7m. There have been losses of 85–134m every year since 2021.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Sales fell 4% in Q2 (−1% at constant exchange rates), and sales in the US fell 6% because hospital contracts on monitors are delayed. Sensor sales did however grow 48% and now make up about 80% of revenue, so the quality (recurring sales) is better than the growth.
      inderes.fi
      −5
    • Moat
      TetraGraph is one of few EMG-based monitors for measuring muscle relaxation, and there are about 6,000 systems at about 750 hospitals. Large monitor makers (GE, Philips, Dräger, Medtronic) can, however, build the function in themselves; therefore Senzime is betting on OEM agreements.
      biospace.com
      −2

      The share count has risen from 62.5m (2021) to 157.2m through repeated directed issues (including 117m at DKK 6.50 and 110m at DKK 4.60 in June 2025). Each round has been at a lower price, and the price is now DKK 2.7.
