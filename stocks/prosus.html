Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
A holding company: about 81% of net asset value is the 23%-owned Tencent stake, so the P/E (7.95), PEG (0.26) and net profit (USD 11.6bn) are driven by the Tencent share and gains, not its own operations – formula points for PEG are therefore misleading. Accounts in USD (year to 31/3), share price in EUR. Stockanalysis shows −39% in share count in a year, which cannot be explained by the buyback (USD 7.4bn) and is probably due to the cross-holding with Naspers – no points have been given for it. The buyback (USD 7.4bn in FY26) is funded by selling Tencent shares and far exceeds FCF (USD 1.5bn), so it earns no plus, even though it raises NAV per share. The average target of EUR 61 (+70%) has not followed the price fall (−39% over 52 weeks, −44% from the peak of EUR 63.94), and targets are not a finding. Tencent's own affairs have not been assessed separately (avoiding double counting). Its own operating earnings are adjusted EBITDA of ~USD 1.3bn; reported operating margin 3.3%. Insider trading and credit rating have not been examined.
