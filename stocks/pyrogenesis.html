    Bull case

    The order book of CAD 40m is converted into revenue over three years (approx. 13m a year on top of new order intake), revenue reaches CAD 25–30m with a 35% gross margin and break-even, and the fumed silica plant becomes an equipment order in the double-digit-million class. At EV/Sales 4–5 on CAD 30m the market value is CAD 120–150m – approx. 2x; a larger Asian silica deal with several 10,000-ton reactors could give 3–5x.

    Bear case

    Cash is effectively empty: yet another issue at C$0.15–0.25 (as this summer, when the price fell to approx. C$0.20) dilutes 20–40%, the fumed silica agreements drag out again, and projects are delayed. Negative equity and dependence on the CEO's money mean that a closed financing door could send the stock towards zero.
