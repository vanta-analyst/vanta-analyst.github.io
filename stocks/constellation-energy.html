    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Calpine ($21.8bn, closed 7/1/2026) has doubled leverage, and GAAP results are far below adjusted. The Crane restart targets the end of 2027, but the NRC operating licence is only expected in May 2027, and full capacity requires new transmission facilities. PJM's colocation rules are unresolved, and some data centre customers are waiting. Stockanalysis' FCF is distorted by the accounting for sold receivables, so cash flow is only partly captured.
