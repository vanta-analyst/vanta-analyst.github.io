    • Debt

Morgan Stanley downgraded to Sell on 18/9-2026, citing rising leverage and lower earnings growth.

    • Competition in Spain
      MasOrange (now 100% owned and consolidated from 1 June) had EBITDA of −3.1% in H1 2026; management itself points to price pressure from the low-cost operator Digi and expects only low single-digit growth in H2.
      investing.com
      −2
    • SFR acquisition (regulation and execution)
      Orange, Bouygues and Iliad want to buy SFR from Altice France at an enterprise value of €20.4bn; Orange is to take over about 5m customers and spectrum. The competition authority is examining the deal, and closing is only expected in H2 2027 – more debt and integration risk on top of MasOrange.
