    Bull case

    The Satcom orders from NATO countries come in H2 2026, IMU capacity doubles, and new defence and industrial customers come beyond Saab. If sales return to SEK 130–150m with an operating margin of 8–10%, EV/Sales of 2–3 can justify a value of SEK 400–600m (1.5–2.5x); a larger NATO satcom contract can give more.

    Bear case

    The Satcom orders keep failing to materialise, NLAW volume falls after 2027, and the loss continues. Then the cash runs out during 2027–2028, and a new issue at DKK 0.4–0.6 becomes necessary; the price may halve.
