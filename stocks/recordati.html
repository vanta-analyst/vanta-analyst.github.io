    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The price is driven by the CVC/GBL consortium's (Respighi BidCo) voluntary tender offer at €51.29 per share with a view to delisting; the offer period runs 31/8–15/10/2026 with payment on 23/10, and the price (€53.00) reflects the offer and speculation in a raise rather than fundamentals. The stock may be delisted before the score is used. Reported EPS (€2.38 TTM) is markedly below adjusted EPS (about €3.1–3.2) due to amortisation of intangibles from acquisitions; stockanalysis' forward P/E 19.4 and the forecast's 2026 P/E 16.6 are based on different EPS bases. Price targets were last updated 31/7 and partly predate the offer's final terms – not used as a finding. Guidance for 2026 and targets for 2027 exclude the effect of US tariffs and MFN pricing. Isturisa's patent expiry has not been examined. Insider trading not examined beyond the consortium's own purchases.
