Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
The Madrid listing (BME:FER) is used; Ferrovial is a Dutch N.V. also listed on Nasdaq (FER) and is in the process of delisting from Euronext Amsterdam. The consolidated figures understate the value because 407 ETR (43%) is equity-accounted; conversely, stockanalysis' FCF of €1.88bn TTM is lifted by working capital (receivables +€458m) and includes the project companies' cash flows – analysts expect only about €0.94bn in 2026, which is used in the assessment. Consolidated net debt of €6.8bn is mostly non-recourse project debt; excluding infrastructure projects the company has net cash of €1.3bn. EPS expectations are adjusted; reported EPS is €0.85 TTM, and H1 net income (€258m, −52%) fell due to missing gains from the 2025 sale of the UK airports. Impairments of €309m TTM in the cash flow statement have not been examined. Price targets (average €61.40) are only partly updated after the 22% fall from the February peak. The bid for I-285 East (over $10bn) opens in mid-October – not scored. Industry: the value lies mostly in toll roads, but 80% of revenue is construction; 'Construction & materials' chosen as for Vinci. Insider trades not examined.
