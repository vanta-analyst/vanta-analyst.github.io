    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −11 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin 7.7% and net margin 5.4% (TTM); gross margin only 14.7%. Guidance of 7–9% EBIT margin for 2026 is still below the over-10% level of 2016–2018, and the margin was −6.6% as recently as 2022.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Growth comes from Power Solutions (turbines), while the high-margin Service business fell 5% y/y in Q2 to EUR 896m; revenue has only grown from EUR 15.6bn to 20.3bn since 2021 (about 6% a year).
      gurufocus.com
      −2
    • Future segment
      Offshore – the segment meant to carry growth – remains loss-making throughout 2026, with profit in 2027 at the earliest, and there were no offshore orders in Q2.
      gurufocus.com
      −3
    • Moat
      Chinese manufacturers sell turbines at about USD 400,000/MW vs about EUR 1m/MW at Vestas; Goldwind, Envision, Windey and Mingyang each install more than Vestas globally. Vestas' average price fell to EUR 1.0m/MW from 1.11 a year earlier.
      greentechlead.com
      −2

      The US tax credits require construction to start before July 2026, which could slow US orders from 2027; wind turbines are covered by the Section 232 tariff of 50% on steel and aluminium content, and a separate Section 232 investigation of wind turbines is under way.
