    Bull case

    Controlled manufacturing is reached in the autumn, and the CE study application is approved in 2027. Sanofi then exercises its right of first refusal for a global licence (agreed in the development agreement), or a veterinary partner signs. A licence with USD 50–100m of milestones could lift the value to NOK 0.6–1.5bn, i.e. 3–7x from about NOK 0.22bn.

    Bear case

    The cash of NOK 36.8m only lasts to about Q1–Q2 2027. A new share issue at a low price (the latest rounds were at NOK 0.25–0.50), another rejection from NOMA or production problems could halve the price again. The share has fallen 88–94% in a year, and the 75% rally could vanish just as quickly.
