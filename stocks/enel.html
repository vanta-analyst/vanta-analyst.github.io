    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' EPS (€0.41 TTM, trailing P/E 20.9) is reported and weighed down by impairments of €2.2bn; the company's ordinary EPS guidance is about €0.74 for 2026, and forward P/E is based on adjusted figures. Stockanalysis shows −7.4% in share count over a quarter, but the company has only bought back 2.25% of the capital (229m shares) – the change in share count is not used for points. 'Issuance of Common Stock' of about €2bn is probably hybrid bonds. The price fall in September (about −10% in a month) appears to be due to rate expectations and technicals, not new company news. Credit rating, insider trading and details on the Latin American currencies have not been investigated.
