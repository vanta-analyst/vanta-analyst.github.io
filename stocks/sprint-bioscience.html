    Bull case

    Another programme deal at TREX1 level (USD 10–20m upfront = SEK 100–200m) for NNMT, DCPS or VPS34 plus a Gilead milestone at clinical start. With self-funding operations the value could reach SEK 0.8–1.1bn = SEK 8–10 per share (2.5–3x).

    Bear case

    No new deal in 2027, the cash shrinks and momentum turns: the price back to SEK 1.5–2 (−45–55%) and a share issue at a low price. Gilead could drop TREX1, so the milestones disappear.
