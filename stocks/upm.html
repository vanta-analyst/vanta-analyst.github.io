    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The forestry and paper industry has no industry of its own on the list; the closest is 'Commodities & uranium' (pulp is a commodity). The company is cyclical (pulp). The portfolio is changing: plywood is being spun off as WISA Group (trading from the start of November 2026), and graphic paper is being placed in a JV with Sappi (EU phase II, decision expected at year-end). The historical figures therefore include businesses that are disappearing. The energy business (hydropower and the stake in OL3) has not been assessed separately.
