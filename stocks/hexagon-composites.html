    Norwegian producer of composite tanks (Hexagon Agility: Fuel Systems, Mobile Pipeline and Aftermarket) with a residual stake in Hexagon Purus. NOT the same company as Hexagon AB or Hexagon Purus (own JSON files). Market cap calculated by us: 332.98m shares × NOK 12.78 = NOK 4,255m ≈ DKK 2,926m. Nordnet's DKK 1,339m is outdated/wrong (based on a far lower share count); the share count is confirmed by the Financial Supervisory Authority's short register (3.32m shares = 0.99%). The directed issue in May 2026 gave 320.8m shares; the last approx. 12m shares are assumed to stem from a subsequent repair issue (not checked). Net result (-NOK 646m TTM) is dominated by losses and write-downs on the Purus stake (545m). Industry set to 'Cars & car parts' because Fuel Systems (tank systems for trucks and buses) is the largest segment.
