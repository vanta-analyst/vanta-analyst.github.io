Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
The share price includes an upcoming special dividend of up to EUR 2.5bn (about EUR 14.6 per share minus ordinary 2026 dividends), paid on completion of the Axalta merger; after payment net debt rises mechanically, while EV multiples are unaffected. The merger awaits EU approval (provisional deadline 22/10) and is expected to close in late 2026/early 2027; the share then moves to a pure NYSE listing after a period of dual listing. Forward EPS and forward P/E are adjusted (non-GAAP); reported EPS of 3.71 TTM is lifted by the gain on the sale of Akzo Nobel India (divestment of EUR 816m in 2025). The information on Nippon Paint/Sherwin-Williams' EUR 73 bid comes from one article and is not confirmed in the company's own announcements. Insider trading and litigation are not examined. Paints/coatings do not fit the industry list precisely (placed under Construction & materials, as decorative paint is about 40% of sales). Q3 results 22/10.
