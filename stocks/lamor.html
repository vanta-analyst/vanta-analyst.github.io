    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Lamor makes equipment and services for oil spills and environmental cleanup (including soil remediation in Kuwait) and is building a plant that makes oil from plastic waste (Kilpilahti). 'Machinery & automation' was chosen as the closest industry (like Vow). The share count is 27.50m according to the company's flagging notice; stockanalysis shows 26.96m and a misleading rise of +27% over a year, and Nordnet's market cap (DKK 172m) is outdated. Market cap calculated myself: 27.50 × 0.806 = EUR 22.2m ≈ DKK 166m. The profit warning of 29/9-2026 is factored in; the 1/10 price probably reflects it. Runway is calculated on cash alone; the new EUR 15m bank facility is conditional on an issue of at least EUR 5m by 15/12-2026 at the latest. No analysts on stockanalysis (Inderes: Reduce). Short register and insider trades not systematically checked.
