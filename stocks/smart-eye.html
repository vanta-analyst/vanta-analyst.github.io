      The gross margin is 88–91%, and EBITDA came to 54m in H1 2026. But EBIT is −98m TTM (−20%) and net income −130m, because amortisation of capitalised development (115m) and interest weigh. There have been losses every year (−173m in 2025).

    • Quality of growth
      Automotive grew 128% organically in Q2 (93m vs 42m), driven by the EU's DMS requirements (applying to all new cars from 7/7-2026), which is a one-off lift in penetration. Behavioral Research fell (48m vs 50m). Redeye assesses that the realised market share in Europe is lower than expected.

    • Capital allocation
      The company capitalises about SEK 127m a year in development (26% of sales), so FCF after development is about −126m TTM. The losses are financed with issues (478m in 2021, 295m in 2023 and 142m in 2024), a bond loan of 300m and acquisitions against shares (Sightic). The share count is +4.6% in a year, and ROIC is −3.7%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6

      The company has 379 design wins (175 in production) at 24 OEMs and a remaining lifetime order value of over SEK 7.6bn. That is a strong position, but competition from Seeing Machines, Cipia, Tobii and Mobileye/Valeo and Chinese DMS suppliers presses the price per car.
