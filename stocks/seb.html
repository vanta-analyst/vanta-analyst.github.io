    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (stockanalysis shows negative FCF due to lending movements); valued on P/B and P/E, and payouts are measured against net income. Dividends + buybacks (SEK 33.5bn) are 108% of net income (31.1bn), so buybacks give no plus. The share is +28% over one year and above the average analyst price target. The Riksbank (1.75%) signals rate hikes that may lift NII; Q3 results in October. Baltic AML risk, property exposure and rating have not been examined separately.
