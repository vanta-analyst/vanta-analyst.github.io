    Bull case

    The FRH proposal is adopted with a capital increase in which the minority can participate, EBITDA after rent doubles as in H1 (109 m vs 55 m), and net debt falls to under 1 bn. Even then existing shares will be heavily diluted; a 3–10x on the current share requires the restructuring to avoid debt conversion – there is nothing to suggest that.

    Bear case

    The TLB creditors' proposal is carried out, or the court revokes the 2024 plan: the creditors get 99.9%, and the current shareholders are left with about 0.1% – in practice a price close to zero. Even the FRH proposal with 610m converted against a market cap of 69m can give −80–95% for the current shares.
