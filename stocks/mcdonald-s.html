    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −8 of −30
    • ROIC
      ROIC 17.8% vs 19.7% in 2023 (18.7% in 2024 and 18.3% in 2025): still high, but falling three years in a row. The operating margin is flat at 45.7% vs 46.1% in 2025.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1
    • Quality of growth
      Global same-store sales only +1.3% in Q2 and US +0.8%, driven by a higher average check while traffic fell. Analysts expect only 3.2% annual revenue growth and 6.5% EPS growth over three years, and new restaurants contribute less (about 2.5% in 2027, about 2% in 2030).
      mcdonalds.mediaroom.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The investor day promised franchisees about $8.5bn of support to 2036 (about $5bn before 2030) as rent relief and capital contributions, plus $1.5–2bn of extra investment in 2027–30 on top of about $3bn a year. The money is meant to keep the system competitive rather than create new growth, and rent relief reduces McDonald's own rental income.
      sec.gov
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −20 of −35
    • Customers are shifting
      Visits from low-income customers keep falling; in 2025 industry visits from this group fell by double-digit percentages, and in Q2 2026 US traffic fell again. The goal is to gain 1.5 percentage points of share in both chicken and beverages by 2030. GLP-1 is a long-term threat to traffic.
      trefis.com
      −6
    • Execution
      Too many simultaneous value offers muddled the message in the US in Q2, and a new head of McDonald's USA has been appointed to turn around the home market.
      stockstory.org
      −2
    • Lawsuits
      Discrimination lawsuits from Black franchisees have been partly dismissed, but parts of the case continue, and there are calls for boycotts.
      thegrio.com
      −2
    • Debt
      Net debt about $53.8bn (3.6x EBITDA) and negative equity; rated BBB+/Baa1 stable. The extra franchisee support and higher investment must be funded on top of dividends and buybacks that already use almost all FCF.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Insider trading
      About $27m sold in 2026 with no purchases; CEO Chris Kempczinski sold $17.5m at $332 in February, and the head of McDonald's USA has sold continuously through the year.
      secform4.com
      −2
    • Investor day and price targets
      The stock fell 4.3% to a four-year low on the investor day, 23/9. Price targets have since been cut: Baird $285→250, Oppenheimer 325→295, Morgan Stanley 308→297 and Melius (Sell) 250→230. No EPS target was given, only a 2030 margin target. (The old list mentioned that the 50,000-restaurant goal had been pushed to 2028; that is not in the investor materials and has been removed.)
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Macro
      Falling consumer confidence and high petrol prices squeeze the low-income core customer.
      foodbusinessnews.net
      −3

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Falling US visits, especially from low-income customers. Price targets are still being cut after the investor day on 23/9 (Baird $250, Melius Sell $230, Morgan Stanley $297), so the upside to the average price target (about $300) is too high. Support for franchisees (about $8.5bn to 2036, partly as rent reductions) squeezes future rental income; the effect on EPS is not disclosed. Q3 results 4/11.
