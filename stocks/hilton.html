    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Q3 2026 has NOT been reported yet (27 Oct); figures run to 30 Jun 2026. Stockanalysis' revenue (USD 5.1bn) excludes reimbursed costs from the owners, while analysts' 2026 estimate (USD 13bn) includes them, so revenue growth in the forecast is not comparable, and the operating margin of 57% is overstated measured against gross revenue. Negative equity (−USD 6.3bn) stems from debt-funded buybacks, so ROE/P/B are meaningless. Industry: hotel = 'Transport & logistics' (closest; could also be consumer). Insider trading has not been investigated. 13F purchases give no points.
