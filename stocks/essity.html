    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The B share (ESSITY.B). Hygiene and health products do not fit the industry list: placed under 'Food, drinks & restaurants' as the closest consumer goods category (alternatively Pharma & medtech for Health & Medical, but that is only about 20% of sales). Reported revenue is depressed by the sale of Vinda (2024), and 2024 net income was lifted by the gain on the sale, so EPS history is distorted. The sale or IPO of Consumer Tissue (31% of sales) may change the profile markedly in H1 2027. Q3 report 22 Oct. 2026. Peers (Kimberly-Clark, P&G) have not been compared.
