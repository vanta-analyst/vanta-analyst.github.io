    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −14 of −35
    • Macro and cycle
      Record orders (EUR 17.9bn in Q3, book-to-bill 1.57, backlog EUR 162bn) are driven mainly by power demand from AI data centres in the US. A slowdown in data-centre capex or the gas-turbine cycle would hit both orders and the multiple; beta 1.87.
      quartr.com
      −5
    • Quality of cash flow
      Operating cash flow of EUR 9.6bn (TTM) includes a EUR 6.1bn increase in customer prepayments; the current ratio is 0.90 and working capital −EUR 4.3bn. Management itself says the timing effects are unlikely to repeat.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Execution
      The quality problems on the 4.X and 5.X onshore platforms (rotor blades and main bearings, up to 30% of about 2,900 turbines) cost EUR 2.2–2.4bn and have led to disputes with customers and US investor-lawsuit investigations. The warranty risk on the installed base remains.
      renewableenergyworld.com
      −4
    • Share offering
      The stock fell 8% on 14/9 on reports that Siemens AG would sell about 6% for EUR 2.5bn; Siemens AG is now below 5% and has lost its board seat.
      ad-hoc-news.de
      −1
    • Management and regulation
      The US subsidiary Siemens Energy Inc. pleaded guilty to stealing confidential competitor information in a tender; the settlement was USD 104m.
