    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −14 of −30
    • Margin
      Bigger, not stronger: revenue has risen from $291bn to 413bn since 2021, but operating profit has fallen from $14.2bn to 12.2bn. The operating margin has gone from 4.9% (2021–22) to 2.2% (2024) and 2.95% TTM, and the gross margin from 17.5% to 13.6%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • ROIC
      ROIC 7.6–8.8% in 2021–23, 4.1% in 2024, 5.5% in 2025 and 7.2% TTM, only slightly above a WACC of 5.8%. Improving, but not yet back at the level before the Aetna crisis.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Acquisitions of $16.6bn in cash in 2023 (Oak Street Health and Signify) were followed by write-downs and restructuring of $5.7bn in 2025, which pushed net profit down to $1.8bn. The dividend has been stuck at $2.66 since 2023, and the share count has risen 1.2% in a year.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Future segment
      The integrated model rests on Caremark, which is losing members in 2027, and the retail pharmacies, where revenue is flat ($33.8bn in Q2) and stores are closing. The improvement in 2026 comes from Aetna cutting benefits and exiting markets, not from growth.
      healthcaredive.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 8 findings    −25 of −35
    • Customers are shifting
      Management expects Caremark's membership to fall in 2027 because clients are leaving, and retention is below the historical level; the stock fell over 6% on results day, 5/8, despite raised guidance. 340B restrictions and cheaper generic specialty drugs are also squeezing Health Services.
      briefs.co
      −5
    • PBM reform
      The Consolidated Appropriations Act 2026 (February) decouples PBM pay from list prices in Medicare Part D and requires more reporting to clients; states such as Arkansas, California, Florida and Tennessee are also restricting spread pricing, patient steering and PBM-owned pharmacies.
      medcitynews.com
      −4
    • FTC settlement
      The FTC settlement with Caremark (summer 2026) requires a standard product in which rebates are passed on to members at the counter, manufacturer-paid fees are decoupled from list price, and retail pharmacies are offered cost-plus reimbursement: a change to the rebate model.
      global.lockton.com
      −2
    • Customer concentration
      The federal government (Medicare, Medicaid and others) accounts for about 20% of revenue according to the annual report, so CMS rates and rules feed straight through.
      sec.gov
      −3
    • Omnicare (governance)
      A jury found that Omnicare had submitted over 3m false claims to Medicare, Medicaid and TRICARE, and the $948m judgment made CVS jointly liable. After Omnicare's bankruptcy the case has been settled for $440m: $130m immediately and $310m by March 2028 at the latest, guaranteed by CVS.
      arnoldporter.com
      −2
    • Debt
      Net debt $62.3bn (net debt/EBITDA 3.7), interest cover 3.9x, current ratio 0.87 and Altman Z 2.43. Leverage has fallen from 5.7x in 2024, though.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Insider trading
      Board member Larry Robbins (Glenview) sold 3.37m shares for $317m on 19/5/2026 and more shares in the following days, and he left the board in August. An activist major shareholder's exit, not management selling.
      marketscreener.com
      −4
    • Medicare Advantage cycle
      Aetna's medical loss ratio swings wildly: over 95% in Q3 2024 and 87.4% in Q2 2026. The improvement was bought with benefit cuts and 600,000 fewer members since the start of the year, and a new rise in utilisation could quickly reverse the picture.
      healthcaredive.com
      −2
