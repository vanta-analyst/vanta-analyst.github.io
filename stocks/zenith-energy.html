    Bull case

    The ICSID tribunal awards Zenith significant compensation, and Tunisia pays or settles. Already 10% of the claim (about US$57m ≈ DKK 375m) equals more than the entire market cap (about DKK 257m). 25–50% of the claim (US$143–286m) could in theory give 3–7 times the price, even after the debt of about C$69m. At the same time the solar projects must reach shovel-ready status and be sold or financed without issues.

    Bear case

    The ICSID claim is dismissed or gets a small award that is not recovered for years, while bond maturities of C$16.8m and operating burn force the company into repeated issues close to C$0.04–0.05. With debt of about C$69m incl. deferred purchase price above market cap, shareholders could be diluted toward zero (−70–100%).
