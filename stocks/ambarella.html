    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Fiscal year Feb-Jan (FY2027 ends 31/1-2027); TTM = through Q2 FY27 (July 2026). Price $67.43 (9/10-2026), market cap $2.98bn, EV $2.72bn. Forward P/E and analyst EPS are non-GAAP and exclude share-based compensation (about 21% of revenue) - on a GAAP basis there is a loss. Revenue fell 33% in FY2024 in an inventory correction at customers, but the company is fabless and not on the list of cyclical industries, so cyclical: false. The share via WT Microelectronics (60%) comes from an AI-generated 10-Q summary and has not been checked directly in the 10-Q, but is in line with the 58-60% in older 10-Ks. The outcome of the 2025 sale process has not been found in any source; no deal has been announced. Competition (Qualcomm, Nvidia, Horizon Robotics, Mobileye) and the design-win pipeline in the automotive segment have not been examined in depth, so T1 moat has 0 points and the score is an upper limit.
