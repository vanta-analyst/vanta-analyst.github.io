    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • ROIC and margin
      ROIC has fallen from 8.3-10.0% (2021-22) to 1.2% TTM, and gross margin from 42.8% to 24.7%. The adjusted EBIT margin was 6.6% in Q2 2026, and the reported operating margin only 2.0% TTM. The return is far below the cost of capital (WACC about 6%).
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has gone from EUR 11.7bn (2022) to EUR 9.3bn TTM and stood still in Q2 2026 (EUR 2,423m). The improvement in adjusted EBIT (+27% in Q2) comes from the Oulu ramp-up and own savings, not from demand, which the company itself calls subdued.
      placera.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Investments of EUR 1.0-1.1bn a year in 2023-24, especially the rebuild of Oulu for consumer board, have produced negative FCF for four years. Oulu reaches full capacity only in 2027, and extended shutdown weighs again in Q3. At the same time parts of the portfolio are being sold (German corrugated plants, Swedish forest, strategic review of sawmills).
      placera.se
      −5
    • Future segment
      The most stable and valuable asset, the Swedish forests (EUR 8.5bn), is being spun off in 2027. What remains is a cyclical business of cartonboard, paperboard, pulp and wood products with a low margin history.
      placera.se
      −2

    • Cash flow and dividend
      FCF has been negative four years in a row (EUR −237m, −161m, −130m and −50m TTM), while the dividend (EUR 198m TTM) was paid with divestments (EUR 619m from the forest sale) and debt. The dividend is not covered by FCF.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Debt
      Net debt EUR 2.7bn, and interest cover on reported EBIT is 0.93. The company's own leverage of 2.2x adjusted EBITDA looks better because the proceeds from a hybrid bond are classified as equity. Altman Z-score 1.68.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Execution and one-offs
      Recurring impairments and restructurings: −EUR 83m in items outside comparison in Q2 2026 (on top of −EUR 61m in fair value adjustment of biological assets), after impairments of EUR 561m and EUR 325m in 2023-24. IFRS operating profit was only EUR 16m in Q2.

      −3

      Subdued demand in many end markets, rising energy and logistics costs after the Iran conflict, and income from CO2 allowances falling from about EUR 72m (2025) to EUR 10-20m in 2026 because more plants have lost free allowances.
