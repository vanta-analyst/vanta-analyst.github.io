    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 2 findings    −3 of −35
    • Tariff refund litigation
      After the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs, four class actions were filed in March 2026. They demand that Costco pass the refund (estimated at over $1bn) on to members, and Costco wants them dismissed. Management says the money goes to lower prices; that removes the gain, but not the litigation risk.
      tikr.com
      −2
    • Customer behaviour
      The renewal rate in the US/Canada has slipped from 93.0% (Q2 FY2025) to 92.1–92.3%, and the global rate is 89.8%. Management explains this by a larger share of online sign-ups, who renew less often. The decline is small, but it is the most important KPI series in the model.
      finviz.com
      −1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The price is the whole story: P/E 47 for 10–11% EPS growth. Even flawless operations can give a poor return if the multiple approaches the 10-year average (about 39). FY2026 EPS includes a one-off gain of USD 0.15 from refunded IEEPA tariffs; it has been adjusted for, but matters little. Net cash is not precisely adjusted for prepaid membership fees (estimated at about USD 3bn). Insider sales have not been verified against Form 4 and are therefore not scored. The price is 1% above the 200-day average.
