    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' TTM EPS (€1.00, trailing P/E 24.9) is reported and lifted by a gain of €953m on the sale of the Mexico business as well as UK tax credits (€182m); adjusted net profit rose 8% in H1 vs. 22% reported. T2 is therefore based on forward P/E (adjusted) and EV/EBITDA. Stockanalysis' +2.4% in share count was checked against the cash flow: the €5bn capital increase in July 2025 (issuance €5.16bn TTM) is partly offset by buybacks of €3.4bn, which mainly neutralise the scrip dividend (Retribución Flexible); the cash dividend is therefore only €1.1bn in the cash flow, while the company itself reports the distribution at €4.5bn. Analysts' FCF estimate of −€8.6bn for 2026 probably includes acquisitions (Caruna) and is used only as support. Price targets (median €20.00) are below the price after +28% over 52 weeks. Q3 results due 21/10-2026. Credit rating (BBB+/Baa1), insider trading and Brazil currency have not been investigated in depth; the Caruna purchase (80% for €2.0bn, EV about €5bn) is counted only under debt.
