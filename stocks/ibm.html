    • Quality of growth
      Revenue grew only 1.4–5.5% a year in 2021–24. The jump to +7.6% in 2025 and 4–5% in 2026 is carried by acquisitions: USD 8.3bn in cash acquisitions in 2025 and 10.9bn TTM (Confluent). Organic growth is low.

      Consulting is standing still: $5.3bn in Q2 (0%, +1% in constant currencies), and Strategy & Technology has been at $11.4–11.7bn since 2023. The consulting part (about 30% of revenue with Intelligent Operations) is the part that AI coding tools put most pressure on.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −10 of −35
    • Guidance cut
      Q2 revenue missed: large, capex-sensitive software deals were postponed, Transaction Processing fell 8% and IBM Z 42%. Full-year growth was lowered to 4–5% at constant currency from over 5%. The FCF target (+1bn $) was maintained.
      business-news-today.com
      −3
    • Competition (AI disruption)
      The stock is −21.5% over 52 weeks. It was downgraded on fears that AI coding tools (e.g. Claude Code) can modernize COBOL systems and bypass IBM's mainframe and consulting base. Wedbush believes the fear has eased, but the risk is structural.
      finance.yahoo.com
      −3
    • Debt and liquidity
      Cash fell $6.3bn to $8.2bn in H1 after acquisitions. Debt $65.3bn, debt/EBITDA 3.7 and current ratio 0.79. The rating is stable (A-/A3/A-), and there are no rating triggers.
      sec.gov
      −3
    • Execution (quantum roadmap)
      The 2026 targets (verified quantum advantage and a fault-tolerant module, Kookaburra) have not yet been confirmed as met. Starling (200 logical qubits, 2029) is still a plan, and Quantinuum and Google lead on gate fidelity.

    • Cash flow
      FCF $13.8bn TTM (+12.7%) covers the dividend ($6.3bn) and buybacks ($1.0bn) with a good margin. The target of about $1bn more FCF in 2026 was maintained despite the revenue miss.
      stockanalysis.com
      +1
    • Quantum position
      IBM has the most concrete fault-tolerant roadmap among the large players: qLDPC codes (up to 90% fewer physical qubits), Nighthawk/Loon (2025), Kookaburra (2026), Cockatoo (2027) and Starling in Poughkeepsie (2029, 200 logical qubits and 100m operations). Cumulative quantum 'book of business' is just under $1bn.
      thequantuminsider.com
      +1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The whole company has been scored. The quantum part is an option: IBM does not disclose quantum revenue separately (only a cumulative 'book of business' of just under $1bn in 2017–24, under 1% of revenue per year). Growth in 2025–26 is partly bought (HashiCorp, Confluent about $11bn). Q3 figures come 21/10. Insider trading and customer concentration in the consulting segment have not been investigated.
