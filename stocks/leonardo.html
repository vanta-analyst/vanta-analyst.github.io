      ROIC 10.0% TTM vs 12.7% in 2025; down after the IDV acquisition, and the 8–10% level in 2021–24 is only mediocre for a defence group in the middle of a rearmament cycle.

    • Portfolio: Aerostructures and space
      Aerostructures (mainly Boeing 787 parts) continue to generate significant losses – only about EUR 50m less than a year earlier – and breakeven is not expected until 2028; the business depends on Boeing raising the 787 rate from 8 to 10 per month. The space joint venture Thales Alenia Space had a net loss of EUR 14m in H1.

      Revenue rose 12% to €10bn in H1, but about €0.4bn came from the acquired Iveco Defence Vehicles; helicopters (about 29% of revenue) grew only 4%. Organic growth is therefore closer to 7–8%, and full-year revenue of €22.1bn was not raised.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Leonardo steers by EBITA and FOCF; forward P/E 18.8 is based on adjusted EPS (2026E €2.39), while reported EPS TTM is €1.95 (trailing P/E 25). FCF is highly seasonal (H1 FOCF −€249m), so net debt at 30/6 (€3.5bn at stockanalysis) is higher than the year-end guidance of €2.2bn; the IDV acquisition (€1.6bn) is included from April. The Italian state (MEF) owns about 30% and appoints management – mentioned, not scored. The NH90 settlement (€113m paid in H1) is concluded and not deducted. The buyback (about €47m) covers only incentive programmes and gives no credit. The price fall of about 13% from the August peak is attributed by the Italian press to peace negotiations over Ukraine, not cancelled orders. Only 5 years of multiple history (2021–25) available. Q3 comes 6/11.
