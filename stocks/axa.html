    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Insurance: FCF (stockanalysis 14.5bn) is not meaningful because it includes customer funds; the payout is measured against net income. Stockanalysis' TTM net income (9.9bn, EPS 4.79) includes the gain on the sale of AXA IM to BNP Paribas; underlying EPS for 2025 was 3.86, and stockanalysis' revenue forecast (+20% in 2026) is an accounting artefact. Trailing P/E on the statistics page (11.9) does not match market cap/net income (about 9); forward P/E is used in T2. Peers: forward P/E 10.2 and P/B 1.74 at ROE 14.9% are cheaper than Allianz (13.3/2.39), Generali (13.0/1.87) and Zurich (14.4/3.27), so there is no peer deduction. AXA XL's reserve history, the interest-rate risk of life insurance and the holding of French government bonds have not been investigated separately. Consensus EPS is adjusted (underlying).
