    Bull case

    FDA approves the CMAP study and the integrated package without a new efficacy study, and ABP-450 becomes the first BOTOX biosimilar in a therapeutic market of over 3 bn USD. Even 2–3% market share (60–90 m USD in sales) can justify a market cap of 100–200 m USD; fully diluted (about 150 m shares) that equals 0.7–1.3 USD = 2–4x.

    Bear case

    Cash runs out in Q1 2027, and the next issue comes at an even lower price with new warrants; the FDA still requires a large comparative efficacy study (USD 50-100m), which AEON cannot finance. The stock could then go toward zero or end in a reverse split and dilution of 50-80%.
