    Penny deep dive (the price is above DKK 3). Gold developer with Goldboro (Nova Scotia, main project) and Goliath/Goldlund (Ontario). Price CAD 1.43 and market cap CAD 366.8m (256.5m shares) from stockanalysis 2/10-2026; Nordnet showed DKK 1,711m. The updated Goldboro FS was targeted for the end of Q3 2026, but was not published as of 23/9-2026; I therefore use the 2022 FS figures (about 100,000 oz/year, IRR 26%, NAV5 about 328m) as a reference, and they are outdated at today's gold price.
