    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −25 of −30
    • Margin
      No revenue. The operating result was −EUR 19.5m (TTM) and −11.1m in H1 2026. There have been losses every year (−18.7 to −28.6m EUR in operating result 2021–2025), and the accumulated deficit is EUR 231m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has gone from 53m (2021) to 206.5m shares. The issue in April 2026 (EUR 40.1m at EUR 0.50) was only 52% subscribed with subscription rights, the rest came from underwriters, and costs were EUR 7.3m (18% of gross proceeds).
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Moat / segment of the future
      The whole company rests on one molecule, bexmarilimab (anti-Clever-1, macrophage immunotherapy). There is no other clinical engine, and after several years of trials there is still no partner to share the costs.
      inderes.dk
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Phase 2b in high-risk MDS (90 patients, placebo-controlled with azacitidine) has not yet started (planned H2 2026), and data only come in 2027. The actual value creation is thus at least a year away, and the company has no income source of its own in the meantime.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −24 of −35
    • Dilution and financing
      The Heights Capital convertibles (up to EUR 35m, of which 25m issued) are amortised over three years, and Faron can pay in shares, which gives a continuous supply of new shares. The company gives no short-term guidance, and money will almost certainly have to be raised again before or around the phase 2b data.
      inderes.se
      −9
    • Binary clinical risk
      High-risk MDS in first line is a graveyard for combinations with azacitidine: venetoclax (VERONA, HR 0.908), magrolimab (ENHANCE, stopped for futility) and sabatolimab (STIMULUS) have all failed in phase 3. Bexmarilimab has to beat the same standard.
      targetedonc.com
      −8
    • Price risk and overhang
      The price has fallen about 79-80% over a year and now stands at EUR 0.419, below the April issue price of EUR 0.50. The underwriters received about 24.5m shares and may sell.
      biostock.se
      −4
    • Debt
      Total debt is EUR 17.8m (8.5m short-term) against cash of EUR 32.0m. Cash repayments on the bonds eat directly into runway if they are not paid in shares.
      inderes.dk
      −3
