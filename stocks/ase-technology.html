      Investments of 211bn TWD (TTM) exceed operating cash flow of 169bn TWD, so FCF is -42bn TWD; 2026 capex has been raised by $2bn to about $10.5bn, and management says FCF may stay negative for some time. The dividend of 23bn TWD is thus paid with borrowed money.

      Revenue only grew from TWD 570bn to 645bn from 2021 to 2025 (about 3% a year), and about 37% of the group is low-margin EMS. Growth in 2026 is concentrated in advanced AI packaging (LEAP).

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    ADR on NYSE (ASX); the accounts are in TWD, so the TWD/USD exchange rate affects returns. stockanalysis has no analyst coverage (forecast returns 404), so price targets and revisions are missing. The Q3 2026 report is due 29 Oct 2026; only Q3 revenue (monthly figures) is included. About a third of revenue is EMS (USI) with low margin, so group margins understate the packaging and testing business (ATM). The 'Semiconductors' industry is chosen; chip packaging/testing has no category of its own. cyclical = true, because earnings follow the chip industry's inventory and utilization cycle (EPS -48% in 2023). 13F purchases give no points.
