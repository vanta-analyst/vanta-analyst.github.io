Acquisition multiples are not disclosed per deal and have not been examined; the marginal return is calculated as EBIT growth over growth in intangible assets. The insider trades in September 2026 are purchases of warrants in the incentive programme, not open-market share purchases, and give no plus. Only 7 analysts; next report 22 October 2026.
