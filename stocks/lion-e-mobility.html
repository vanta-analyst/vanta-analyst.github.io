    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    LION E-Mobility is NOT in restructuring; the standard calibration is used. The company is Swiss (CH ISIN) with production in Germany (LION Smart). stockanalysis is lagging on the share count: it shows 12.38m shares, but after the conversions in January and November 2025 there are 18,375,633 shares (ad hoc 28 Nov 2025). Market cap is therefore 18.38m x EUR 1.82 = EUR 33.4m (about DKK 250m, as Nordnet), and stockanalysis' multiples (P/S 0.90, EV/EBITDA 6.9) are too low; own calculations are used. The balance sheet at 30 Jun 2026 was not found, so cash (1.16m) and debt (15.6m) are as of 31 Dec 2025. The rest of Mukherjee's convertible loan (amount and price) is not disclosed. Mukherjee's exact ownership share is not verified (the company calls him majority shareholder). Customers are not named in 2026 (bus manufacturers; KARSAN was a major customer in 2024). The price target from NuWays (EUR 3.20, paid research) is not used.
