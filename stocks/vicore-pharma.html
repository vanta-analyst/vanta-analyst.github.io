    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −20 of −35

      The investment case is built on AIR, an open-label single-arm study in which FVC rose 216 mL after 36 weeks vs a historically expected decline. The use of synthetic control arms has been criticized, and placebo-controlled data are lacking.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company writes that the cash lasts into H2 2028, but Q2 burn (SEK 156m) gives only about 17 months using the formula. The company expects lower burn when ASPIRE is completed. Vicore was listed on Nasdaq in the US (VCRE) in July 2026 without raising capital. No one has found a concrete news item for the 25% price fall over three months. The owner list is verified only for HealthCap (9.8% as of 31/12/2025). Insider trades and short share have not been reviewed. AIR data is from an open-label single-arm study without placebo.
