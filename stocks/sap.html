Business−8 of −30

Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger?

  • Margin and quality of earnings
    The operating margin has risen from 19.3% (2023) to 28.8% (TTM), but in Q2 non-IFRS operating profit grew only 9% (constant currency) vs 11% in revenue, the cloud gross margin fell to 74.6% from 75.2% because of token costs and higher R&D (18.7% of revenue), and non-IFRS EPS rose only 6%.

    −2
  • Moat and future segment (AI)
    SAP's moat is switching costs in ERP, but AI agents threaten the seat-based pricing model in application software. A BofA survey of 325 IT leaders shows that AI monetisation is still at an early stage and that most customers are only testing; the roughly 400 AI agents only launch in autumn 2026.
    wallstreet-online.de
    −3
  • Quality of growth
    Total revenue has grown only 6.4% (TTM, reported). Cloud (EUR 23.1bn TTM, +22% in Q2) is growing strongly, but support is falling (11.3 → 10.0bn since 2024) and licences have halved, because growth is largely migration of existing customers; 60% of customers are still on-premise, and the migration peak is expected in 2027.
    stockanalysis.com
    −2

    Goodwill of EUR 30.4bn equals 67% of equity (45.2bn), and acquisitions (EUR 1.7bn TTM: Reltio, Dremio, Prior Labs) dilute operating profit, so 2026 guidance was cut by EUR 100m.

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Q3 results 21/10. Revenue growth in EUR (+9% in Q2) is about 2 percentage points lower than at constant currency. A large part of cloud growth is conversions of existing on-premise customers who must switch before ECC support ends in 2027; what happens to growth after the migration peak has not been explored. AI monetisation is still at the pilot stage. SBC of EUR 0.8bn (about 2% of revenue) is not deducted from FCF. Hasso Plattner's possible sales in 2026 have not been examined.
