    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Milan listing (RACE). Cyclical = no, unlike BMW/Mercedes/Stellantis: Ferrari's earnings have risen every year 2021–26, whereas BMW's EPS has more than halved, the order book covers all of 2027, and several models are sold out for the entire production period – demand does not follow the car cycle (although revenue fell about 10% in the covid year 2020). Stockanalysis' net debt of €1.7bn includes customer financing; industrial net debt was only €131m at 30/6 – not given a plus, as it is not net cash. Forward P/E is based on adjusted EPS; guidance for diluted EPS is ≥€9.68. Exor (about 24% of shares) and Piero Ferrari have enhanced voting rights via loyalty shares – mentioned, not scored. F1 results and regional distribution of deliveries have not been examined in detail. Q3 is due 3/11.
