    Bull case

    FLEX-HCC shows a clear benefit vs lenvatinib alone. That could bring a licence for fostrox and make it the first approved second-line treatment after immunotherapy. At the same time MIV-711 delivers proof of concept in OI or Perthes (orphan and Rare Pediatric status could trigger a priority review voucher). That could lift the value from SEK 1bn to 3–4bn (3–4x).

    Bear case

    FLEX-HCC disappoints (a small Korean study in a hard indication), and the MIV-711 studies are delayed. The share price has already tripled in 2026 and could fall back towards the DKK 0.50–1.00 issue prices (−40 to −70%). Medivir has a long history of disappointments.
