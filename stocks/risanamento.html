    Bull case

    The step-in is completed, the obligations disappear, and the annual accounts show positive equity; Bizzi & Partners or the banks use the listed company as a platform for new projects (e.g. by contributing assets). It is the only path to a 3–10x, and no plan for it has been published.

    Bear case

    The fund's restructuring drags on again, the step-in falls through, and the negative equity forces a recapitalization at a fraction of the share price or liquidation. Even in a successful step-in the company is an almost empty shell with 10–15 m EUR in cash against a market cap of 80 m – a return to the March level of 0.013 EUR (−70%) is plausible.
