    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Q3 2026 is reported on 9 Nov; TTM figures run to 30 Jun 2026. TTM net income (USD 6.5bn) includes an after-tax gain of USD 3.1bn from the sale of OxyChem to Berkshire Hathaway (closed 2 Jan 2026), and the oil price is lifted by the Iran conflict, so TTM margins and trailing P/E are not normalised. Berkshire's ownership and the preferred shares give no points; the remaining amount and redemption of the preferred shares have not been investigated. Insider trading and the economics of the low-carbon projects (DAC) have only been investigated superficially. 13F purchases give no points.
