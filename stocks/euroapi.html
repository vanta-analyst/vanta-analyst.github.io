    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Euroapi is NOT in a restructuring (no sauvegarde or redressement); it is a voluntary restructuring (FOCUS-27) financed by a secured revolving credit facility of EUR 451m to February 2029 and a perpetual hybrid bond of EUR 200m from Sanofi. The normal penny-stock calibration was therefore used. Market cap is calculated at EUR 70.6m (95.53m shares × EUR 0.739, about DKK 527m), in line with Nordnet's DKK 535m. H1 burn (FCF EUR -103m) contains seasonal working capital and restructuring costs; 2025 as a whole had positive FCF. Runway is calculated incl. the undrawn part of the committed credit facility (about EUR 361m); on cash alone it is only about 3 months. The shareholder stakes (Sanofi about 30%, EPIC Bpifrance about 12%) are from the spin-off in 2022 and not verified for 2026. The half-year report (PDF) could not be read directly; the figures are from the company's press release and abcbourse. Insider trades and short interest (AMF register) have not been examined. Peer multiples are approximate and not verified.
