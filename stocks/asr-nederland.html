    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Insurance: payouts are measured against net profit; stockanalysis' FCF swings wildly (−0.6bn in 2025, +1.3bn TTM) and is not meaningful. Stockanalysis' revenue forecast (−29% in 2026) is an IFRS 17 artefact. The trailing P/E of 14.7 is on reported EPS of 4.98; the forward P/E of 11.3 is on adjusted EPS (6.43). 2025 net profit (548m) was depressed by losses on investment sales, so IFRS ROE (12.2%) is lower than ASR's operating ROE (above 15%). Share count −0.23% in a year, but −8.9% in the quarter according to stockanalysis, which does not match buybacks of 175m; not used for points. Interest-rate risk in the life book and the pension transition (Wtp) have not been examined separately. Next report 17 Feb 2027.
