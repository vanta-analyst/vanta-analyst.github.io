    Bull case

    The 2026–27 season shows that Albor/Cruz del Sur is part of the same large copper-gold system as Filo del Sol (which BHP and Lundin bought via Filo Corp. for about CAD 4.5bn in 2024), and Rio Tinto bids for the project or the company. Then market cap could go from about CAD 285m to 1–2bn (3–7x).

    Bear case

    The findings turn out to be small or discontinuous, Rio Tinto lets the exclusivity lapse, and the company must raise 40–60 m C$ in 2027 at 0.20–0.30 C$ (+25–40% shares). With a P/B of 6.5 and no resource, the share price could fall 50–70%.
