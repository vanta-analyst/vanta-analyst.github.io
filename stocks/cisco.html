    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 2 findings    −7 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has only grown from $51.6bn (FY22) to $63.3bn (FY26), about 5% a year including the Splunk acquisition; operating margin is 25.4% vs 27.2–27.4% in FY22–FY23. The new growth comes from AI hardware for hyperscalers, which pulls gross margin down (66.3% in Q4, −2.1 points year over year).
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The Splunk acquisition turned a net cash position of $16.9bn (FY23) into net debt of $15.3bn, and ROIC has fallen from 38-44% to 20%. Buybacks ($8.0bn) and dividends ($6.6bn) exceeded FCF of $12.8bn in FY26, and stock-based compensation of $3.8bn (30% of FCF) means the buyback does little more than hold the share count steady (-0.3%).
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Q1 FY27 is reported on 11 Nov 2026 and is not included. Multiples and ROIC before FY24 are without Splunk (bought for about $28bn in March 2024) and only partly comparable. Insider trading, credit rating and segment revenue for Collaboration/Observability have not been investigated; the score is an upper bound on those points. The industry 'Tech hardware & optics' was chosen; network equipment has no category of its own. 13F purchases give no points.
