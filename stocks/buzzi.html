    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The share is −21% over 52 weeks and close to the bottom after UBS's downgrade to sell (22 Sep); several price targets (Barclays EUR 56, median 52) have not been cut correspondingly, so the upside in consensus is probably too high. Net profit includes about EUR 134m from associates (including Mexico), so EBIT (EUR 845m) is lower than net profit; forward EPS is adjusted. The company reports net cash of EUR 896m (30/6), stockanalysis EUR 707m. The Buzzi family controls the share (free float about 53m of 177m shares); stockanalysis' insider share of 0.14% is therefore misleading, and no points have been given for governance. Consolidation in the United Arab Emirates lifts volume and revenue inorganically. Credit rating and litigation have not been investigated.
