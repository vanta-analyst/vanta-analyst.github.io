    Bull case

    A new crypto bull market lifts AUM from USD 120m to 400-500m, and Enigma and Syntetika provide performance fees. With the savings, the company can reach break-even at about SEK 80-100m in fees, and then the market cap can rise 3-5x from about SEK 220m.

    Bear case

    The crypto market falls, AUM and fees stagnate, and cash must be topped up via the LDA equity line and Helena convertibles at ever lower prices (a classic dilution spiral). With 67m of debt and spending of about 100m a year, the stock could go toward zero.
