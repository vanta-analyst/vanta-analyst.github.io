    Bull case

    Gold holds above US$3,600, the feasibility study confirms the PEA, the environmental assessment progresses, and a mid-sized or large producer buys the company. Even 10–15% of NPV5 (US$2.16bn) equals an EV of US$215–325m, i.e. 2.5–3.5x the current value; Wheaton's involvement makes a takeover more likely.

    Bear case

    The gold price falls toward US$2,500–3,000, where a 0.41 g/t project with AISC of US$1,450 loses most of its value; the environmental assessment drags on, and capex of over US$1bn can only be financed with an issue far below the market price. The price could fall back to C$0.10–0.15 (−50–65%), as in 2024.
