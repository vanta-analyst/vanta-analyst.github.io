    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Telecom does not fit the industry list; 'Software, cloud & internet' was chosen as the nearest (same as Orange, Deutsche Telekom and Telenor). The reported loss (−EUR 3.6bn TTM, −338m in H1 2026) is mainly due to the exit from Latin America (Chile about −1.0bn) and a restructuring provision in Germany; the result from continuing operations was +954m in H1. Forward P/E and EPS forecasts are adjusted figures. stockanalysis' FCF (3.7bn) is before leasing; after leasing it is 1.7bn TTM, and the company's guidance is about 3.0bn for 2026. The revenue forecast (−7% in 2026) reflects divestments. Morgan Stanley's fall in ownership from 13.1% to 8.4% is due to expired derivatives linked to STC and is not counted. Brazil's growth in H1 is in euro and may include currency effects. Q3 figures 12 Nov 2026.
