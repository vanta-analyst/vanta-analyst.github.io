    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −4 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Retail growth is only 2.5% year to date vs a target of 3–4%, and management itself says customer retention 'needs to improve' because price sensitivity is rising. Life/Health grew only 2.4% in operating profit in H1; growth is carried by Asset Management and P&C prices.
      investing.com
      −2
    • Recurring restructuring
      Restructuring costs of EUR 1.3–1.5bn are expected in 2026 (200m in Q2 alone) plus 400m of accelerated IT decommissioning, and management signals more in H2 'in line with past experience'. Restructuring is a recurring cost, not a one-off.
      investing.com
      −1
    • Business mix
      A conglomerate with a large, capital-heavy life and health business (Life/Health revenue EUR 89.5bn TTM, down from 92.3bn in 2025) alongside P&C and asset management; less focused and with a lower return per unit of capital than pure P&C insurers such as Sampo and Gjensidige.

      −1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Stockanalysis' revenue forecast (+63% in 2026) is an accounting artefact (IFRS 17 vs total business volume) and should be ignored.
