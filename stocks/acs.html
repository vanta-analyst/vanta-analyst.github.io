    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis' FCF of €2.49bn TTM is lifted by working capital (+€765m, incl. customer prepayments in construction); analysts expect only €1.32bn in 2026, which is used in the assessment. Net debt: the company reports net cash of €1.0bn as of June 2026 (after the capital increase and the unwinding of equity swaps in May), while stockanalysis shows net debt of €0.47bn incl. project debt and leasing; gross debt is €16.3bn. EPS expectations (€4.20 for 2026) are adjusted ('ordinary' profit); reported EPS is €3.88 TTM. Price targets (average €134.71) have largely not been updated after the fall from €141 in May to €92.50, so the upside is probably overstated. ACS owns about 78% of Hochtief (which owns Turner and Cimic) and 50% of Abertis – minority interests and Abertis' debt have not been analysed separately. Insider selling not investigated.
