    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The merger with Neles in 2022 makes historical ROIC and share figures less comparable. Only 6 analysts. Not examined: insider trades, competition from Andritz and Chinese suppliers, and customer concentration on large projects.
