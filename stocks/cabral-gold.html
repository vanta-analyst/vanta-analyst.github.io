    • Debt in gold
      The 353 kg gold loan runs 39 months at 10% interest; first interest in December 2026 and repayments from March 2027. The carrying value of the debt was CAD 67.4m at 30/6 and rises when the gold price rises.

      The heap leach plant must be ramped up to 3,000 tonnes a day; commercial production (60% of capacity) is only expected at the end of 2026, and no guidance is given for 2026. Repayments on the gold loan start shortly after.

    • Ownership concentration
      Aegis Value Fund owns about 173m shares (about half) and is also a lender; Alpayana owns 9.99%. Free float and liquidity are therefore limited, and a large owner can steer financing and terms.
