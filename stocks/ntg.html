      ROIC has fallen from 39% (2021) and 24% (2023) to 15.9% (2024) and 10.8% TTM after the acquisitions; still above a WACC of 7.9%, but ROE has halved to 19.6%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      EPS is DKK 12.3 TTM vs 21.4 in 2022 and 17.2 in 2023 despite higher revenue; growth comes from acquisitions (DTK) and higher freight rates. In Air & Ocean revenue rose 17.4% in Q2, while gross profit was unchanged (gross margin 20.7% vs 24.3%).
      investing.com
      −3
    • Moat
      An asset-light freight forwarder in a fragmented market with low switching costs and big competitors (DSV/Schenker, DHL, DB, Kuehne+Nagel); a gross margin of about 22% and EBIT margin of about 5% show limited pricing power.

      −2
