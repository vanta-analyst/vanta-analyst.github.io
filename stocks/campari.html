    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −13 of −30
    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen from 10.2% (2022) through 9.5%, 8.1% and 7.7% to 6.1% TTM – only just above the WACC of 5.5%; ROE 6.7% vs 13.1% in 2022.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Courvoisier was bought for about EUR 1.1bn in 2024 and partly paid for with a share issue of EUR 649m (share count +9.8%) – in a cognac category that has since weakened. Non-priority brands are now being divested with write-downs of EUR 82m and brand impairments of EUR 10m in H1 2026.
      investing.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Reported revenue is falling (3,070 m € in 2024, 3,051 in 2025, 3,035 TTM), and reported EPS is 0.22 € TTM vs 0.29 € in 2022–23; organic growth is only about 3%, and the operating margin has fallen from 20.7% (2023) to 18.1% TTM.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Future segment
      Spirits are under structural pressure: whiskey and vodka are declining in the US (Wild Turkey in decline), cognac is weak, and Germany suffers from low consumer confidence; growth is carried by a few brands (Aperol +3.3%, Espolón +8.2%).
      investing.com
      −2

      The Guardia di Finanza seized Campari shares worth about €1.29bn at the controlling holding Lagfin (Garavoglia family) in an exit-tax case; the €405m settlement is paid over four years to 2029, which could pressure the family to sell or pledge shares.
