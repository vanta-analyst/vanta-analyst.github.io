    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Terna is a regulated transmission monopoly, so negative FCF is structural in an investment phase and should be read together with RAB growth; stockanalysis' RAB figure does not exist, and RAB has not been investigated. 'Issuance of Common Stock' of €845m TTM is the green hybrid bond from January 2026, not an equity issue – no deduction was made for dilution. Stockanalysis shows a dividend of €0.37 for 2025 vs. €0.40 for 2024; according to the company the dividend has been raised 10 years in a row, so the figure is not used. The regulated WACC of 5.5% is confirmed for 2026; the level for 2027 and the new period from 2028 is unknown. Industry set to Utilities & power like E.ON (regulated networks); Grids & electrical equipment is used in the model for equipment manufacturers. Insider trading and state ownership (CDP) have not been investigated further.
