    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −13 of −35
    • Customer base and demand
      About half of beer sales go to Latin American consumers, who according to management are buying less due to immigration policy and inflation; Q2 depletions were below the expectation of flat to slightly positive development.
      nbcsandiego.com
      −5
    • Inventory build-up at distributors
      Shipments rose 5.5% vs depletions -0.6% in Q2, because distributors rebuilt days of inventory; the company expects shipments and depletions to move together for the full year, so the second half gets less tailwind.
      stockhub.kr
      −2
    • Tariffs and trade policy
      Almost all beer is brewed in Mexico and imported to the US; aluminium tariffs (Section 232) hit the margin in 2025, and although lower tariffs gave a USD 24.1m tailwind in Q2, the entire beer business is exposed to US trade policy towards Mexico.
      metalpackager.com
      −3
    • Debt
      Net debt/EBITDA 3.0, current ratio 1.0 and Altman Z-score 2.92; debt has only been reduced from USD 11.2bn to 10.3bn since February.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Wine and spirits
      The remaining wine and spirits business (USD 716m TTM) causes recurring write-downs and is expected to ship less in the second half; the operating margin is only 3.8%.
      beancount.io
      −1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Fiscal year ends end of February; Q2 FY2027 (to 31 Aug 2026) was reported on 6 Oct 2026. The beer segment is about 92% of revenue, and reported FY2026 revenue is depressed by the wine divestiture (June 2025), so year-on-year revenue growth is not comparable. The Latino customer share (about half of beer sales) and the immigration effect come from management statements in 2025. The USMCA review and other tariffs on Mexican production have not been examined in detail. Insider trading has not been examined. 13F purchases earn no points.
