    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    E.ON is primarily a regulated network company (Energy Networks) plus a large, low-margin retail business (about 2/3 of revenue), so revenue and net margin say less than adjusted EBITDA and net income. Stockanalysis' EPS (€1.29 TTM) differs from the company's adjusted EPS (guidance €1.03–1.11 for 2026). The final decision on the return on gas networks comes at year-end, and the return on electricity networks will not be decided until 2027. Credit rating, insider trading and British retail regulation have not been investigated.
