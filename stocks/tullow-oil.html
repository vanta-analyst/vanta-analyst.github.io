    Bull case

    Ghana accepts payment over several years, the oil price stays above USD 80, and FCF of USD 200m+ a year pays down debt. A sale of the Ghana assets or the whole company at e.g. 4x EBITDA (about USD 2.8bn EV) minus net debt of 1.4bn and tax of 0.4bn gives about USD 1bn for shareholders – about 5x the current market cap.

    Bear case

    The tax bill must be paid quickly, the oil price falls to USD 60 (hedged only about 60% in H2 2026 and 25–43% in 2027), and the second tax case is also lost. Then the 2027 sale could become a distressed sale in which the creditors get everything and the stock ends close to zero.
