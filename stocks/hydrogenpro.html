    Bull case

    A strategic investor (e.g. ANDRITZ or LONGi) or an acquirer comes in at a premium, the two projects of NOK 300m are contracted, and ReFuelEU requirements give orders in 2027–28. With an EV of only about NOK 19m and an order backlog of 262m, a fully funded plan could give a market cap of NOK 200–400m, i.e. 3–6x from 65m, if dilution is kept down.

    Bear case

    No strategic solution, new orders fail to materialize, and the cash runs out during 2027. Then comes an emergency issue at DKK 0.1–0.3 with over 100% dilution, or a restructuring in which shareholders lose almost everything. Green Hydrogen Systems' bankruptcy in 2025 shows that this happens in the sector.
