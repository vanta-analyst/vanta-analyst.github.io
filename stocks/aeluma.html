    Penny deep dive (the price is above DKK 3). Aeluma develops photodetectors and quantum dot lasers on large semiconductor wafers (InGaAs etc. on silicon/germanium) for AI data centres, defence and aerospace. Revenue is still almost entirely government development contracts (NASA, Navy, DARPA and others). Fiscal year July–June; latest figures are FY2026 (as of 30/6-2026, published 16/9-2026). Price USD 13.12 and market cap USD 252.8m from stockanalysis 2/10-2026 (Nordnet showed DKK 1,618m). The total ATM programme (size and remaining amount), warrants and options have not been reviewed in the S-3/424B. The CFO and the largest individual shareholders are not verified. The CHIPS support of up to USD 30m is only a letter of intent (LOI). Quarterly burn is an estimate from the Q4 loss, because operating cash flow for the year (−USD 3.3m) was held down by contract payments.
