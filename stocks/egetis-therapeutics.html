    • Margin
      Revenue SEK 66m vs an operating loss of -389m TTM, i.e. an operating margin of about -590%. There have been losses every year in 2021-2025, and the loss has grown from -105 to -330m.

    • Future segment
      The company has one product in one ultra-rare disease (MCT8 deficiency, Allan-Herndon-Dudley syndrome). The US label covers only peripheral thyrotoxicosis, not the neurological symptoms. Aladote is parked, and RTH-beta is early.

      The EU launch is going slowly. Sales of Emcitate were SEK 17.4m in Q2 2026 (+23% at constant exchange rates), and Germany remains the main market. Italy and France are awaiting survival data, and Spain has just submitted a pricing application.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    FDA approved Emcitate on 28/9-2026 and granted a Priority Review Voucher (PRV). Egetis gets only 50% of the net proceeds from a PRV sale (the rest goes to the sellers of Rare Thyroid Therapeutics). The sale is planned for Q4 2026 but is not secured, so the runway is calculated without the PRV. The US price has not been published (the press mentions up to about 1 m USD a year, unconfirmed). Quarterly burn is calculated by myself from the H1 cash flow (about 90 m SEK per quarter). Analysts' 2026 revenue (avg. 538 m) probably includes the PRV. Largest owners, insider trades, short interest and peers are not verified.
