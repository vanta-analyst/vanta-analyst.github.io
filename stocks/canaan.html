    Bull case

    The bitcoin price rises sharply, and the miners resume purchases: machine sales return to the 2025 level (about USD 100m per quarter with a positive gross margin), and the holding of 1,915 BTC rises in value. Then P/S of 1 on USD 400–500m of revenue could give 2–3 times the market cap; a 5x requires a new crypto bubble like 2021.

    Bear case

    The bitcoin price ends up low, machine sales stay near zero, and the company burns USD 30–40m per quarter. Cash is empty during 2027, the BTC loans must be repaid, ATM sales and a reverse split dilute shareholders by 50%+, and on non-compliance the stock moves to OTC – effectively zero for a Nordnet investor.

Largest owners and the precise use of the ATM programme have not been investigated in detail. Industry: Canaan designs ASIC chips and builds bitcoin mining machines, and more than half of revenue is now own mining; placed in 'Tech hardware & optics' as the closest category. Cyclical, because earnings follow the bitcoin price and the miners' investment cycle.
