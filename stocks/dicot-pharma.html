    Bull case

    Phase 2b shows a clear effect across severities and confirms an effect for weeks after three days of treatment. That could bring a licence with big pharma in an USD 8bn market: 100+ USD m upfront could lift the value to SEK 2–4bn (3–5x). The main result is unlikely before 2028.

    Bear case

    Phase 2b fails (phase 2a was only significant in one subgroup), or toxicology data delays part II. Cheap generic PDE5 inhibitors make it hard to find a partner. When the cash runs out around 2028, a new share issue at a low price follows, and the price could fall 70–90%.
