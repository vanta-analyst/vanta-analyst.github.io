    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −8 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue in CHF has gone from CHF 65.9bn (2021) to CHF 63.0bn TTM, and consensus expects −0.8% in 2026. At constant exchange rates sales grew 6% in H1 2026, but a Swiss reporter bears the strength of the franc year after year: core EPS was −2% in CHF vs +9% at constant rates.
      roche.com
      −3
    • Diagnostics and ROIC
      Diagnostics fell 3% in CHF in H1 2026 (+3% at constant exchange rates) because of price reform in China and a weak respiratory season. Group ROIC has fallen from 38% (2021) to 28% TTM, partly after the covid tests, partly after acquisitions.
      roche.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Roche bought its way into obesity via Carmot (about USD 2.7bn) and the partnership with Zealand on petrelintide (USD 1.65bn upfront). Petrelintide's weight loss was called disappointing, and JPMorgan cut its price target on that basis.
      cash.ch
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −12 of −35
    • Pipeline execution
      The phase III study persevERA with giredestrant in first-line breast cancer missed the primary endpoint (March 2026), and the obesity candidate petrelintide disappointed. BNP Paribas downgraded to Neutral because the big pipeline data come only in 2027. Fenebrutinib's positive MS data pull the other way.
      finviz.com
      −5
    • Patent expiry and biosimilars
      Avastin and Herceptin continue to lose ground to biosimilars. Analysts point to medium-term risk from large patent expiries and price pressure in key markets, including for Ocrevus, Roche's biggest product (about CHF 7bn in 2025). Fenebrutinib does not fill that gap in the short term.
      simplywall.st
      −3
    • Price pressure and tariffs (USA)
      Trump is threatening new pharmaceutical tariffs. Roche's pricing and investment agreement with the US government is expected to dampen the effect, but the agreement locks in prices in the USA, Roche's most important market.
      cash.ch
      −2
    • Governance
      Genusscheine (ROG), which is the liquid line, carry no voting rights. The shareholder group with pooled voting rights (the founding families) owned 64.97% of the issued voting shares at 31/12/2025. Holders of Genusscheine have effectively no influence.
      roche.com
      −2
