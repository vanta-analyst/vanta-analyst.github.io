    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −15 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen three years in a row: from SEK 236.0bn in 2023 to 228.3bn in 2025 and 220.8bn TTM; nine months of 2026 −4.4% in SEK, Q3 only +1% in local currency, and September is expected at +1%. Consensus expects −2.3% for 2026 as a whole.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Margin
      Operating margin 9.0% TTM vs 20.1% at Inditex; Q3's 10.6% was lifted by a one-off effect of about 1.6 pp from tariffs, so the underlying operating margin was about 9%. H&M was above 15% in the 2010s.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat
      Squeezed between Inditex (expected growth of about 7% a year, ROIC 46%) and ultra-cheap online players; H&M's revenue is shrinking while its largest competitor grows, and margin improvements come from cost cutting, not pricing power.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The dividend (SEK 10.5bn and 10.9bn) exceeded FCF after leases (9.1bn and 9.6bn) in both 2024 and 2025, and the dividend payment is 84% of net profit; buybacks are small (1.2bn TTM).
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −9 of −35
    • One-off items and tariffs
      The 54.0% gross margin in Q3 included a one-off effect of about 1.6 pp from refunded tariffs; underlying about 52.4% vs 52.9% a year earlier. The +23% in operating profit therefore overstates the improvement, and tariff costs on goods from Asia are a lasting risk.
      finance.yahoo.com
      −4
    • Logistics and inventory
      Disruptions in the European logistics network during consolidation hit sales in Q3, and inventory rose to SEK 39.4bn from 37.9bn (+3.7%) while sales were flat.
      finance.yahoo.com
      −2
    • Governance and control
      The Persson family owns 68.7% of the capital and 85.1% of the votes and keeps buying; minority shareholders have no influence, and the free float is shrinking, with a risk of a later squeeze-out bid at a low premium.
      placera.se
      −3
