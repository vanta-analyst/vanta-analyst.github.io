      Revenue stood still for three years (EUR 5,435m → 5,401m → 5,425m in 2023–2025, incl. Octave). Q2 2026 with 12% organic growth was the highest in five years, but after years of stagnation and a structural 7% drop from the D&E divestment.

    • ROIC
      ROIC has been 5.8–9.3% since 2022 (5.8% in 2025) vs a WACC of about 8.6%; a P/TBV of 187 shows that almost all equity is goodwill from decades of acquisitions.
      stockanalysis.com

    • Quality of earnings
      Reported EPS was EUR 0.23 in 2025 vs adjusted EUR 0.41, and reported net income has fallen from EUR 1,023m (2024) to 617m. Hexagon steers by EBITAC, which ignores amortisation of capitalised development costs and acquired intangibles.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

      A cash purchase of Waygate Technologies from Baker Hughes for about USD 1.45bn – about 2.3 times revenue and about 23 times EBIT at an EBIT margin of only 10%, half of Hexagon's own margin. Bigger, but not stronger.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The B share (HEXA.B). Accounts in EUR, price in SEK. Octave (software) was spun off with effect from 22/5/2026 (1 Octave share per 10 Hexagon shares). stockanalysis' TTM figures mix Octave and continuing Hexagon: the trailing P/E of 11.2 and a pre-tax margin of 46% are due to an accounting gain on the spin-off, and the 52-week change (−11%) is not adjusted for the Octave distribution. The score therefore rests on the company's own Q2 figures for continuing operations and forward multiples. Industry 'Tech hardware & optics' (precision measurement, sensors, GNSS/lidar) – the closest match after the software spin-off. The Waygate acquisition (USD 1.45bn) is expected to close in H2 2026.
