    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock is −40% over 52 weeks and −55% from the end of 2024 (EUR 151 → 68), because the market is pricing in AI substitution of the content business; the low multiple is therefore not free. The company reports an adjusted operating margin of 29.4% (H1) and adjusted EPS (+14% at constant currency), while the reported operating margin TTM is 25.6% and reported EPS 5.87; stockanalysis' 2026 estimate (5.60) is on a different basis than the TTM figure. P/B of 16 is meaningless because of buybacks and negative tangible equity. The company's net debt/EBITDA is 2.0x vs stockanalysis' gross debt/EBITDA of 2.77. The fall in share count (−3.4%) matches buybacks of EUR 0.8bn TTM. Insider trading has not been examined. Next report 24/2/2027 (possibly a Q3 trading update in November).
