Cracks−8 of −35

  • The Middle East and costs
    The Q2 profit disappointed because the conflict in the Middle East increased freight costs; operating costs rose 8.3% vs sales of 7.6%, the gross margin in Q2 was 56.7% vs a higher expectation, and the stock fell about 3%. Around 480 franchise stores are located in the Middle East.

    Shein and Temu undercut Zara, H&M and Mango with young, price-sensitive customers; Shein had revenue of about 41.8 bn USD in 2025 and has thus nearly caught up with Inditex, which responds by moving Zara upmarket.

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Fiscal year Feb–Jan (FY2025 = year to 31 Jan 2026). Stockanalysis' FCF of 8.27 bn EUR is before lease payments of 1.87 bn EUR (IFRS 16); FCF after leases is about 6.4 bn EUR, and the dividend (5.34 bn) plus buybacks (0.16 bn) are below that. EV/EBITDA is also inflated by lease depreciation. The share count is effectively unchanged (the buyback of 161 m EUR covers employee shares), so no plus has been given for buybacks. The insider share of 5% at stockanalysis is wrong: Pontegadea (Ortega) owns about 59%. Price targets were updated after the H1 results on 9 Sept.
