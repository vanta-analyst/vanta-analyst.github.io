    Bull case

    Metallurgy raises recovery at Cebolleta to 90% (NPV approx. $160m), the Kaycee drilling points the way to a first resource in 2027, and the DISA money is released. With a market cap of approx. C$31m, a re-rating to 0.3–0.5x of an NPV of $160m could give C$65–110m, i.e. 2–3.5x – more if Sumitomo or enCore bid.

    Bear case

    The price collapse covers up something negative, the Colorado sale is not closing, and the cash is empty at the start of 2027. Then C$8-10m must be raised at C$0.20-0.25 (+35-50% shares). The metallurgy confirms only 80%, and an NPV of $84m with a 5-10 year permitting process carries not much more than the current market cap.
