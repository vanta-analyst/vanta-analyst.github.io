Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Accounts in USD, price in NOK. The net margin (8%) is low due to Norwegian petroleum tax (effective tax 73%), not weak operations – the operating margin is 29%. 2026 earnings rest on an oil price of about USD 98/barrel and European gas at USD 15.8/mmbtu (Q2). A peer check against Aker BP (EV/EBITDA 2.8, P/B 1.9, ROIC 8.8%) does not show Equinor to be relatively expensive – hence no peer deduction. The Ørsted stake (about 10%) has not been valued.
