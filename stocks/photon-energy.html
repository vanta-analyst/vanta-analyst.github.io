    Bull case

    The sale of the Romanian parks and the RayGen stake provides cash, the bond is reprofiled with a maturity extension without conversion, and EBITDA rises to EUR 20–25m on higher power prices and lower costs. At EV/EBITDA of 9–10, EV becomes about EUR 200–230m; minus about 170m in debt = EUR 30–60m equity, i.e. 2–3.5x the current market cap of 17m.

    Bear case

    The bondholders demand a debt-to-equity conversion of EUR 78.8m, or the bank pulls the overdraft facility; current shareholders are diluted to a small fraction, or the company goes into restructuring (Dutch WHOA/German SchVG), where the stock becomes almost worthless.
