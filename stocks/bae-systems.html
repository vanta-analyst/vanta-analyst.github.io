      Underlying return on sales was 10.8% in H1 2026 vs 10.6% the year before, and operating margin is 9.9% TTM. Despite a defence boom and a record order book, the margin is almost standing still: the company is getting bigger, not markedly stronger.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −9 of −35
    • Cash flow (customer prepayments)
      FCF of £1,791m in H1 2026 (vs −£368m) came from a high level of customer prepayments. The cumulative FCF guidance for 2025–27 and 2026–28 is unchanged, so the cash has merely been brought forward and will flow back in the coming years. Contract liabilities have risen to £7.9bn.
      investegate.co.uk
      −3
    • Execution (Maritime)
      Maritime (submarines and ships) had an operating margin of only 6.7% in 2025 vs guidance of about 8%; Deutsche Bank downgraded to hold for that reason and explains it with milestone timing and more first-in-class programmes. Large fixed-price contracts such as Dreadnought and AUKUS carry execution risk.
      proactiveinvestors.co.uk
      −2
    • Customer concentration and budget
      The customers are a few governments (UK, US, Saudi Arabia). According to The Times, the British government is considering delaying the timetable for 3% of GDP on defence, and the stock fell on the news. The stock is about 21% below the March peak and −1% over 52 weeks.
      ajbell.co.uk
      −2
    • Debt
      Net debt is GBP 4.95bn (gross debt GBP 9.15bn) and 2.3 times EBITDA in gross debt, after the Ball acquisition. The cash of GBP 4.2bn is lifted by advance payments, so the adjusted net debt is higher.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The share price is in pence (GBX), the financial figures in pounds. TTM FCF of £4.7bn and P/FCF 11.6 at stockanalysis are lifted by customer prepayments in H1 2026. Cumulative FCF guidance for 2025–27 and 2026–28 is unchanged, so the prepayments are cash brought forward. T2 therefore uses the guided FCF (>£2.0bn). The amount of the prepayments is not disclosed. Pension, US segment margins, F-35 production and export risk to Saudi Arabia are not examined in detail. No insider trades are examined. The report on a delayed British defence budget is based on press coverage (The Times).
