    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −9 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin before other items 4.6% in H1 2026 and about 5.25% guided for 2026; net margin 3.2% (TTM). Even the 2028 target of 5.5–6.0% is a thin margin in a labour-intensive industry with low barriers to entry.
      sg.finance.yahoo.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has only grown from DKK 71.4bn to 84.7bn in 2021–2025 (about 4% a year), and much of 2026 growth is price increases; management itself expects the price contribution to fall towards 2028. H1 growth of 8.2% is also lifted by a one-off adjustment from the DTAG settlement.
      globenewswire.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      The 2026 buyback programme has been raised to DKK 3.1bn; with dividends (about 0.5bn) payouts exceed FCF guidance of over DKK 3.1bn, while acquisitions are being made at the same time (Tomagruppen, ISS Türkiye). Net debt rose from DKK 13.2bn to 15.8bn in H1.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
