    • Banco BPM and M&A
      Crédit Agricole owns 29.3% of Banco BPM (partly via derivatives) and, according to Il Sole 24 Ore, is studying a joint takeover with UniCredit in which CA would get about a third of the assets (25 Sep); alternatively a merger with Crédit Agricole Italia. Italian Golden Power policy and a large acquisition with thin CET1 create execution and dilution risk.

      −3
    • Capital buffer
      CET1 for Crédit Agricole S.A. is 11.3%, markedly below BNP Paribas (13.0%) and the Nordic banks (about 17%); the stronger group capital sits with the regional banks, not with the minority shareholders.
      investing.com
      −1
    • Controlled company
      Only 874m of 2.99bn shares (29%) are free float; the regional banks control the company via SAS Rue La Boétie and decide strategy and distributions, and insiders' ownership is not disclosed.
      stockanalysis.com

    • French government debt and politics
      French banks fell 3–4% on 27 Aug on concern over public finances: 10-year OAT yield about 4.1%, spread to Germany about 88 bp, and proposals for a tax on dividends and buybacks ahead of the presidential election. Crédit Agricole is the most domestically exposed of the large French banks.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The analysis covers the listed Crédit Agricole S.A. (ACA), about 62% of which is owned by the regional banks via SAS Rue La Boétie; it is not the whole Crédit Agricole group. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B and P/E, and the payout is held against net profit. Stockanalysis' ROE (8.6%) is calculated on equity including goodwill and hybrid capital; the company's RoTE was 14.3% in H1 2026. TTM EPS (−13%) is affected by a gain of EUR 304m on the deconsolidation of Amundi US in Q2 2025. Peers: P/B 0.62 and forward P/E 7.5 at ROE 8.6% are in line with BNP Paribas (0.78/7.9/10.5%) and Société Générale (0.64/8.3/9.5%), so no peer deduction. Consensus EPS is adjusted. The French special tax on large companies and LCL's net interest margin have not been investigated separately. Q3 results 30 Oct.
