    Bull case

    Phase 2a confirms complete response in about 50% after 6-12 months with good safety, and a CAR-T company licenses iTANK with payment on signing. ELC-401 gets a partner or a grant. Then the market cap can go from about SEK 186m to 0.6-1.5bn (3-8x) without large dilution, because cash lasts about 2½ years.

    Bear case

    The responses do not hold (relapse after 6–12 months), and autologous CAR-T in lymphoma loses commercial interest because of bispecific antibodies and in vivo CAR-T. No licence comes, and ELC-401 must be financed with a new issue in 2027–28 at DKK 1–1.5. The price may fall towards DKK 1 (−60%).
