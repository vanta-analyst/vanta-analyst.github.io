    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company: revenue, earnings, ROE/ROIC and P/E are fair-value changes on the portfolio and not meaningful; the PEG formula therefore gives 'n/a', and there is no analyst coverage at stockanalysis (forecast 404). Valued at a discount to net asset value (NAV 78.86 USD / 59.51 GBP as of 13 Oct 2026; price 3,802 GBX on 16 Oct gives about a 36% discount). Scored on the GBX line PSH in London (quote/lon/PSH); there is also a USD line (PSHD), and it has not been checked which line Nordnet shows. The portfolio companies' own findings (Uber, Brookfield, Alphabet, Amazon, Hilton, Fannie/Freddie etc.) are not counted, only PSH's exposure. Buybacks (share count -3.2%) and dividend are financed by the portfolio, not by FCF, and therefore give no plus. The full discount history 2016-2024 and ongoing charges have not been found; position weights have not been found. Industry set to 'Finance, data & insurance brokerage' like Investor AB and Exor.
