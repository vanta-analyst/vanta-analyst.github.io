    Bull case

    The PFS shows robust economics (e.g. NPV of several hundred m USD at high gold and silver prices), the Nebari tranche is opened, and/or TSJA rules in Emerita's favour in the Aznalcóllar case. With an EV of approx. 105m C$, that could send the price towards the 2025 level of 1.0–1.6 C$ (3–4x).

    Bear case

    The PFS disappoints or is delayed again, cash runs out around year-end, and the company must raise 15–25m C$ at 0.15–0.30 C$ (+20–50% shares) or take expensive debt. With board unrest and the Nebari loan ahead of shareholders, the share price could halve again.
