    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    cyclical set to false: Mining (over half of orders) follows mine capex and production, and a large part is aftermarket; Machining follows industrial production. The tungsten price affects the margin via a temporary timing effect (see T3), but earnings do not primarily follow a commodity price – the same assessment as FLSmidth. Trailing P/E (27.9) is on reported EPS; forward P/E 19.1 rests on adjusted EPS, which analysts expect to rise 45% in 2026 – part of that is tungsten prices. Insider trades and litigation have not been examined in depth.
