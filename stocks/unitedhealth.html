Cracks

Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings−24 of −35
  • DOJ investigation (governance)
    The DOJ is investigating UnitedHealth's Medicare billing both criminally and civilly, and the criminal part has been widened to Optum Rx and the pay of its own doctors. The Senate report (Grassley) concluded that the group 'aggressively' inflates risk scores through home visits and coding. A court-appointed special master has, however, rejected an older civil claim of about $2bn in overpayments.
    fiercehealthcare.com
    −9
  • Customer concentration
    Premiums from CMS (Medicare and Medicaid) make up about 44% of revenue, so rates, the risk-adjustment model and star bonuses are set by one payer.
    sec.gov
    −6
  • Macro and regulation
    Medicare Advantage margins are only 3–5%, and managed-care stocks sold off in September on rising health costs; the stock closed at $388 on 10/9, 16% below its peak. The 2027 star ratings (5% quality bonus) are published in mid-October.
    ad-hoc-news.de
    −4
  • Customers are shifting
    UnitedHealthcare has lost 965,000 Medicare Advantage members since the start of the year, and in Q2 Commercial fell by 145,000 and Medicaid by 380,000 (incl. exiting Louisiana); 48.5m members in total. Much of it is deliberate clean-up, but it weakens scale.
    sec.gov
    −3
  • Debt and credit
    Moody's and S&P have negative outlooks according to the Q2 10-Q. The balance sheet is not under pressure, though: net debt/EBITDA 1.7 and debt/capital 41.2% (44.1% a year earlier).
    sec.gov
    −2

Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
The DOJ's criminal and civil investigation is unresolved and may lead to fines and demands for changed practices. The Q2 margin was helped by $860m of favourable development of prior-year claims reserves. CMS publishes the 2027 star ratings in mid-October, and Q3 results come 13/10. The credit ratings' negative outlook is from the Q2 10-Q and has not been reconfirmed. Insider trading is only two small sales (the Optum head, $0.66m) and is not included.
