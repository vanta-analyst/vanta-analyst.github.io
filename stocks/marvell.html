    • Earnings quality (GAAP vs. non-GAAP)
      Q2 FY27: GAAP EPS $0.33 versus non-GAAP $0.94 (GAAP is only 35% of the adjusted figure). The difference is amortization ($215m in the quarter), share-based compensation ($829m TTM, 8.8% of revenue, +40% in a year) and the Celestial earnout ($434m in 1H). TTM net income of $2.64bn includes a one-off gain of $1.83bn from the divestment of automotive ethernet. Receivables have risen $767m in a year.

    • Capital allocation
      Has bought Celestial AI for 3.5bn $ (1bn cash, 24.6m shares plus an earnout of up to 24.4m shares) and XConn for 469m $, even though neither has material revenue yet (Celestial is not expected to contribute until 2H FY28). Buybacks of 2.4bn $ TTM exceed FCF (1.73bn $), yet the share count rose 2.6% anyway.

      Marvell is number two behind Broadcom in custom AI chips and competes on price with Taiwanese design houses (Alchip, GUC). The big deals were bought with equity: Google gets warrants against purchases, and Nvidia received a discount via the preferred shares. This suggests weaker bargaining power towards the hyperscalers.

    • Customer concentration
      Distributor A accounted for 44% of revenue in Q2 FY27 (up from 34% a year earlier), and direct Customer A for 16%. The ten largest customers accounted for 82% in FY26, and four customers hold 72% of receivables. Data centre is 79% of revenue and depends on the AI capex of a few hyperscalers.

    • Dilution
      Google has received warrants for up to 58.97m shares (6.7%) at 206.58 $, which vest one tranche per 500m $ of custom purchases through FY2033; at 275 $ they are already in the money. In addition come Nvidia's preferred shares (21.8m shares at 91.84 $, 2.5%) and the Celestial earnout (up to 24.4m shares, 2.8%). In total this is up to approx. 12% dilution, and the share count is already +3.1% in one quarter.
      sec.gov

      According to market checks, Taiwan's Alchip is the main supplier on AWS' Trainium 3, while Marvell continues to supply Trainium 2/2.5. Microsoft is reportedly looking at Broadcom for Maia, and Broadcom dominates the custom market (Google TPU, OpenAI, Anthropic). The company has not specifically denied the loss.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Data as of 11/10/2026 (price $275). GAAP figures are depressed by roughly $0.9bn of annual amortisation of acquired intangibles (Inphi, Cavium, Celestial), share-based compensation and the revaluation of the Celestial earn-out, while TTM earnings are lifted by a one-off gain of $1.83bn from the sale of automotive ethernet to Infineon. Nvidia's $2bn investment (31/3/2026) is confirmed: convertible preferred shares with a conversion price of about $91.84 (max. 21.8m shares, about 2.5%), not converted as of 1/8. The Google warrant (59m shares at $206.58) is probably booked as a discount on revenue when earned. Customer shares at AWS, Microsoft and Google are not disclosed directly (only 'Distributor A' and 'Customer A'). POET's cancelled Celestial order (April 2026) is a POET problem and gives no deduction here. The Trainium 3 loss to Alchip is market rumour, not confirmed by the company.
