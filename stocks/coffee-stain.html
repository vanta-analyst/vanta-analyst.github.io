A price of SEK 19.54 and 223.2m shares (A+B) give a market cap of about SEK 4,361m (DKK 2,880m), in line with Nordnet. The fiscal year runs from April to March; Q1 FY26/27 = April-June 2026. Reported earnings are low due to large amortization of intangible assets from the Embracer era (goodwill 2,045m), so P/E 71 is misleading. Valheim 1.0 was released 9/9-2026 on PC, Xbox, PS5 and Switch 2; sales figures have not been published. Industry: 'Software, cloud & internet' is closest for games. Operating company with no burn, so runway is null. Short data not examined.
