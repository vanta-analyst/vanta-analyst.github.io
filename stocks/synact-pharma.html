    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −27 of −30
    • Margin
      No revenue. The operating result was −SEK 116.5m in 2025 and −55.1m in H1 2026, and there have been losses every year since the listing in 2016.
      storage.mfn.se
      −10
    • Clinical evidence
      The phase 2b study ADVANCE (246 patients) missed the primary endpoint DAS28-CRP; ACR20 was over 75% vs about 60% on placebo at 40 mg (p = 0.06). It is the third RA study without a hit primary endpoint (EXPAND 2023 and RESOLVE part A were also negative), and the case for phase 3 rests on secondary endpoints (SDAI) and post hoc subgroups.
      edisongroup.com
      −8
    • Capital allocation
      The share count rose 12.7% over a year (49.9 to 56.2m), including a directed issue of SEK 51.9m in March 2026. At the same time, own shares were bought for SEK 5m in January–February 2026, shortly before new shares were issued, while cash was small.
      storage.mfn.se
      −6
    • Market and moat
      RA is a crowded market with MTX, TNF inhibitors, IL-6, JAK inhibitors and cheap biosimilars. Resomelagon is to be positioned as an add-on to MTX in newly diagnosed RA with a modest effect difference; the patent in the USA (exclusivity to 2044) is a strength.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
