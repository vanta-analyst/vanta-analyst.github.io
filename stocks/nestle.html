    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −12 of −35
    • Governance and management
      Three top executives in 13 months: Schneider was removed in 2024, and his successor Freixe was fired in September 2025 for a relationship with a subordinate. Chairman Paul Bulcke had to leave early after criticism of his handling of the matter, and Pablo Isla became chairman on 1/10/2025.
      swissinfo.ch
      −4
    • Debt
      Net debt is CHF 56.7bn vs CHF 32.9bn in 2021, and debt/EBITDA is 3.7. The debt grew while shares worth CHF 27bn were bought back in 2021–24, and the dividend equals 107% of reported net profit.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Earnings and guidance
      Net profit fell 31.4% to CHF 3.47bn in H1 2026, and underlying operating profit fell 2.8%. The currency effect was −6.2% on sales, and the restructuring (Fuel for Growth, factory closures) is still costing money.
      foodnavigator.com
      −3
    • Execution of portfolio clean-up
      Water (50% to Platinum Equity at an EV of EUR 4.9bn), mainstream vitamins and ice cream have been put up for sale or sold. That is sensible, but it ties up management's focus until 2027 and removes revenue.
      bluewin.ch
      −1
