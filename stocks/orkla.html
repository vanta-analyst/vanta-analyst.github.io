Caveats

What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Orkla is a portfolio company (consumer goods, Orkla India and about 43% of Jotun as an associate). Net income for 2025 (NOK 11.5bn) and the 2025 P/E of 9.7 are lifted by disposal gains (including the sell-down in Orkla India), so 2025 trailing figures are not comparable. The 6.5% dividend yield includes a special dividend. Private-label pressure and market shares by country have not been examined in detail.
