    The speculative corner.

    Small Nordic companies trading below DKK 3 a share, often with little revenue and a short cash runway. We give each one two separate ratings.

    High risk. Most stocks at this price lose money, dilute their shareholders and can go to zero. A high asymmetry rating is not a recommendation; it only says the possible upside is large relative to what we think could go wrong. Not investment advice.

    Two ratings, side by side

    Vanta score · 1–100

    The normal Vanta model: business, price and cracks. It measures quality, and penny stocks usually score low.

    Asymmetry · 1–10

    A separate speculative rating of potential against risk: market value, upcoming catalysts, partners, cash runway and how close the price is to its low.
