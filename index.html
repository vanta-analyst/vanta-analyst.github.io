      Three tests

      The most a stock can lose in each step. Three data adjustments follow: growth-adjusted price, analyst upside and estimate revisions.

      The full method
      −30

      Business

      Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? Margins, returns on capital and the quality of growth.

      −35

      Price

      Is the valuation fair against its own history and its growth? PEG and analyst upside count too.

      −35

      Cracks

      Cash flow, debt, customers, insider selling and cut guidance. Up to +15 back for documented strengths.
